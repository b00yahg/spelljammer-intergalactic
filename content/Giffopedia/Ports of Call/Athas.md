Athas is a dying desert world at the center of its own crystal sphere, orbiting a crimson sun. Arcane magic there draws its power from surrounding life, permanently sterilizing the ground around the caster. Metal is scarce enough that weapons are commonly made from bone and wood, and the Sea of Silt has replaced open water with dust. No gods answer prayers on Athas, and seven sorcerer-kings rule its city-states over a population in which psionic ability is near universal.
