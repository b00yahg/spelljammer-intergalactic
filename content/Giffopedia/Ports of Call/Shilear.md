The Shilear Cluster is a mining community in the far reaches of the known Flow. Its most populated body is the asteroid Shilear, which shares its name with the settlement built on it. The settlement is filled with outcasts from the rest of the universe who have found a quiet life mining away a glowing energy source known as Glob.
