Lil' Avernus is a mega city populated largely by refugees from the Lower Planes displaced by the Blood War. Its streets are lit continuously by neon, and the temperature holds at roughly a hundred degrees. Below towering skyscrapers, fiendish gangs battle each other and the imposing angelic police force.
