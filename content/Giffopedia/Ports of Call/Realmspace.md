Toril is the world at the center of Realmspace, the third planet out and the most populated. Its landmasses include Faerun, Kara-Tur, Zakhara, and Maztica, and the planet is close to Earth in size. Residents call the space beyond it the Sea of Night. The interior of the surrounding crystal shell is inscribed with glyphs nobody has fully translated, some of which function as portals to other planes.
