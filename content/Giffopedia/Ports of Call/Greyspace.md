Oerth is the world at the center of Greyspace, and it occupies the position a sun would normally hold, so the entire system turns around it. Its largest continent is Oerik, whose eastern subcontinent, the Flanaess, is the best charted region on the planet. The surrounding crystal shell carries a pearl-like sheen with star-gems set into it. Travel from Greyspace to Realmspace runs in both directions, while travel to Krynnspace is one way.
