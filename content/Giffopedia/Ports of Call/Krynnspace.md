Krynn is the world at the center of Krynnspace, mostly ocean, with Ansalon in the southern hemisphere the best known of its continents. Three moons govern its magic, a wizard drawing power from whichever one matches their alignment. Much of this world's history is defined by a series of wars between a moralistic battle between dragons and mankind.
