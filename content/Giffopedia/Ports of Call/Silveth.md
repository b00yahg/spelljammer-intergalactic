Silveth is an asteroid chartered as sovereign ground by the Imperial Elvish Navy. Companies registered there are exempt from the tariffs levied at every other port, and most Elvish-affiliated businesses are registered there as a result. The asteroid has been completely terraformed, laid out in concentric rings beneath a glass dome with the pavilions of its largest firms at the center. Its outer rings are demolished and rebuilt on a regular cycle to display current industry.
