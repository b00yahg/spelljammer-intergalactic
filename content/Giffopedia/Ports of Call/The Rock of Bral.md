The Rock of Bral is an asteroid city suspended in the Phlogiston and the primary trade crossroads for business conducted beyond civilized space. Its lower quarter is called Blue Moon, after the blue crescent of light that falls across it. Blue Moon serves as the unofficial headquarters of the Aces.
