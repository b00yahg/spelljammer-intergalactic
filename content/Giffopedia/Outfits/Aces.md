Aces are freelance bounty hunters. Contracts are announced publicly on the Wildcard broadcast and claimed by whichever crew reaches the target first, which makes the work well paid and dangerous in equal measure. The Full House is the public leaderboard ranking the top crews, with the top four being named Jack, Queen, King, and Joker.
