The Imperial Elvish Navy is a military force that protects elven interests across the spheres. Command runs through a Council of Admirals under a Grand Admiral. Non-elves may serve as crew but are not promoted to command, and elven spelljamming helms are never permitted to pass into non-elven hands. The Navy sustained heavy losses in the Second Unhuman War in both resources and reputation.
