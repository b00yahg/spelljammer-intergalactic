The AAPD is a company of angels charged with the holy task of keeping order in Lil' Avernus. Most of its angels drew the posting as punishment for refusing a divine task. A rare few volunteer. Organized patrol factions police the streets from chariots drawn by pegasi, blue and red lights flashing.
