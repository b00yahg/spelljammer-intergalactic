The Sindiath Line is a megacorporation whose leadership consists of immortal elves, who continue acquiring and developing holdings without ever vacating their positions. Known subsidiaries include Ainscorp (fleet provisioning), Greela Translight Logistics (cargo and transport), Verdance Arcanomateriel (arcane components), and Dawnthread Communications (long-range comms). Its working relationship with the Imperial Elvish Navy is an open secret.
