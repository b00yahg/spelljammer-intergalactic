> [!info] Now Broadcasting
> Five bounty hunters and one secondhand ship, in a galaxy that keeps sending the bill.

**Spelljammer: Intergalactic** is an ongoing Dungeons & Dragons campaign played out across the Phlogiston, the rainbow ocean that runs between worlds. The crew works as Aces, freelance bounty hunters who take their contracts off the Wildcard broadcast. This site is where every session gets written down.

Forty-three sessions are posted here so far, running from Episode 1 up to Episode 37, with interludes and specials filling the gaps. Act One is finished. Act Two is underway.

## Start here

- [The Broadcast](the-broadcast.base) holds every recap in reading order, grouped by act. New readers start at Episode 1.
- [Nebula Net](nebula-net.base) is where the crew keeps their profiles, alongside the people they keep running into.
- [Giffopedia](giffopedia.base) covers the outfits and the ports of call.
- [Progress Report](progress-report) tracks how far the campaign has gotten.
- [Soundtrack](soundtrack) is what plays at the table.

## A note on the recaps

The players write these themselves, session by session, so the voice shifts depending on who took notes that week. Indented text is read-aloud from the table. The comment sections at the bottom of an episode are in-world, posted by whoever was watching the broadcast.
