**Spelljammer: Intergalactic** is an ongoing Dungeons & Dragons campaign played out across the Phlogiston, the rainbow ocean that runs between worlds. The crew works as Aces, freelance bounty hunters who take their contracts off the Wildcard broadcast.

> So, you wanna be Aces.
>
> First thing to know: It's like any other business.

## Start here

- [Progress Report](progress-report) lists every episode in order, act by act. New readers start at Episode 1.
- [Nebula Net](nebula-net.base) is where the crew keeps their profiles, alongside the people they keep running into.
- [Giffopedia](giffopedia.base) covers the outfits and the ports of call.
- [Field Manual](field-manual.base) has the house rules the table plays with.
- [Soundtrack](soundtrack) is the jams of the cosmos, tuned in from wherever the crew happens to be docked.
- [Gallery](gallery) is every piece of art made for the campaign, credited to the artists who drew it.
