# The Rock of Bral: Downtime Activities Guide

## Introduction

Between grabbing bounties and slinging through the philogistion, your characters have time to pursue personal projects, make connections, develop skills, and engage with The many NPCs in the Rock of Bral.

This guide outlines the downtime system for our Spelljammer campaign, adapted from the Player's Handbook and Xanathar's Guide to Everything. If you want more info about these activities, you can read them THERE!

> [!NOTE] Time Compression
>
> In our digital world, everything is faster! Activities that normally take weeks in standard D\&D now take the same number of days in our campaign (with a minimum duration of 1 day). This should give players more opportunity to expand on things!

## Downtime System Overview

Downtime periods occur between major story arcs, providing opportunities for character development, world exploration, and mechanical advancement outside the immediate pressures of the main plot.

During each downtime session, I'll rotate spotlight time between players to ensure everyone gets fair opportunity to play. However, you 100% have the freedom to join others in your activities without it counting against individual spotlight time. For example, if Otto wants to do some training, they can bring an emotional support D'arby for roleplaying

A typical downtime session follows this structure:

### 1. Previous Arc Conclusion/Setup

- Resolving any loose ends from the previous adventure

### 2. Downtime Activities & Roleplay

- Players declare and resolve their chosen activities
- Free roleplay scenes anywhere in the Blue Moon
- Special DM interactions during outskirts visits

### 3. Spamazon Package Opening

- Delivery of any items ordered through Spamazon
- Examination and integration of new equipment

### 4. Weekly Reconvening

- Gathering at The Laughing Beholder to watch Wildcard
- Get new bounties and adventure hooks
- Setup for the next story arc

> [!TIP] Flexibility First
>
> While this document provides structured options for downtime, I encourage creativity and flexibility. If you want to attempt something not covered by these rules, discuss it with me. The goal is to enhance storytelling and character development, not restrict it.

> [!IMPORTANT] Spamazon: Providing Instant Gratification!
>
> Shopping does not need to consume valuable session time! You're free to order whatever you need from Spamazon before we meet, and packages will be delivered at the end of downtime. Just let me know what your buying (or better yet, send me your spamazon recipt). You can download Traditional shopping can still occur at specific locations like Gaspar's Reclamation during your visits.

## Carousing

When the void between worlds gets lonely and the credits are burning a hole in your pocket, there's no better activity than drinking with strangers. Sharing tips, swapping tales, and making connections can pay dividends when you least expect it.

**⏱️ Duration:** 1 day\
**💰 Cost:** Varies by social class targeted

| Social Class | Cost   | Examples of Contacts                                  |
| ------------ | ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Lower class  | 10 gp  | Dockworkers, mercenaries, traders, mechanics          |
| Middle class | 50 gp  | Guild members, spellcasters, ship captains, merchants |
| Upper class  | 250 gp | Nobles, diplomats, faction leaders, wealthy investors |

> [!WARNING] Upper Class Restrictions
>
> Carousing with the upper class requires either a noble background, sufficient existing contacts, or a successful Deception check with a disguise kit to pass as visiting nobility.

### How It Works

After a day of fine food, strong drink, and socializing, make a Charisma (Persuasion) check and consult the table below:

$\text{Carousing Check} = d20 + \text{Charisma modifier} + \text{Persuasion proficiency (if applicable)}$

|Check Total|Result|
|:-:|---|
|1-5|Made a hostile contact|
|6-10|Made no new contacts|
|11-15|Made an allied contact|
|16-20|Made two allied contacts|
|21+|Made three allied contacts|

### About Contacts

Contacts are NPCs who share a bond with your character. They come in two varieties:

- **Allied Contacts:** Friends who will render aid to you, but not at the risk of their lives.
- **Hostile Contacts:** People who work against you, placing obstacles but stopping short of committing crimes or violence.

> [!NOTE] Contact Limitations
>
> You can have a maximum number of unspecified allied contacts equal to 1 + your Charisma modifier (minimum 1). Named, specific contacts don't count toward this limit.

Once a contact has helped or hindered you, you'll need to carouse again to get back into their good graces. A contact provides help once, not help for life. They remain friendly (or unfriendly), but this doesn't guarantee assistance.

## Training

The Laughing Beholder attracts expert aces from across The Philogistion who are willing to share their knowledge—for a price. Whether you're looking to learn an exotic language or master a new tool, you'll find some vetern here who can teach you.

**⏱️ Duration:** Up to 10 days, reduced by your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1 day)\
**💰 Cost:** 25 gp per day

### How It Works

After paying for instruction and completing the required training period, you gain proficiency in your chosen language or tool. Training progresses as follows:

1. Declare the language or tool proficiency you wish to learn
2. Calculate training duration: 10 days - your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1 day)
3. Pay 25 gp per day of training required
4. At the conclusion of training, gain the proficiency

### Popular Trainers

| Expertise           | Trainer           | Special Notes                                                                               |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Weapons Proficency  | "Quickdraw" McGee | In quick draw fashion, McGee can teach you for half the time for double the price.          |

## Research (World Wide Weave)

The Garden's information repository connects to the World Wide Weave—a vast network of knowledge spanning The Philogistion. With the right credits and patience, you can access data on almost anything in known space.

**⏱️ Duration:** 1 day\
**💰 Cost:** At least 50 gp (additional investment improves results)

### How It Works

1. Declare a specific research focus (a person, place, or thing)
2. Make an Intelligence check with these potential bonuses:
   - +1 per 50 gp spent beyond the initial 50 gp (maximum +6)
   - Advantage if it is about a topic you know very well

$\text{Research Check} = d20 + \text{Intelligence modifier} + \text{Bonus from additional spending}$

| Check Total | Information Gained   |
| :---------: | -------------------- |
|     1-5     | No information       |
|    6-10     | One piece of lore    |
|    11-20    | Two pieces of lore   |
|     21+     | Three pieces of lore |
Each piece of lore is the equivalent of one true statement about a person, place, or thing. Examples include knowledge of a creature's resistances, the password needed to enter a sealed dungeon level, the spells commonly prepared by an order of wizards, and so on.

> [!NOTE] Research Depth
>
> The specificity of your research question directly impacts the usefulness of the information you receive. Broad queries like "Tell me about pirates" will yield general information, while specific questions like "What are the weaknesses of the Gith raiding vessel Mindbreaker?" will produce more actionable information. Neither is better then the other, but do keep in mind your questions matter

## Religious Service (Observatory/Shrine)

The Garden's observatory offers a unique spiritual experience. Here, a combination of advanced telescopes and reserved shrines for multiple gods allows worshippers to gaze upon the celestial bodies associated with their deities while performing sacred rites.

**⏱️ Duration:** 1 day\
**💰 Cost:** None

### How It Works

Spend a day in devotion or service at the observatory shrine, then make either an Intelligence (Religion) check or a Charisma (Persuasion) check.

$\text{Religious Service Check} = d20 + \text{Ability modifier} + \text{Skill proficiency (if applicable)}$

|Check Total|Result|
|:-:|---|
|1-10|No effect|
|11-20|Earn one favor|
|21+|Earn two favors|

### Divine Favors

A favor, in broad terms, is a promise of future assistance from a representative of the temple. It can be expended to ask the temple for help in dealing with a specific problem, for general political or social support, or to reduce the cost of cleric spellcasting by 50 percent. A favor could also take the form of a deity's intervention, such as an omen, a vision, or a minor miracle provided at a key moment. This latter sort of favor is expended by the DM, who also determines its nature.

> [!IMPORTANT] Favor Limitations
>
> Your gods have more to deal with then your bullshit. You have a limit of 1 + your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) favors available at any given time. Favors need not be expended immediately, but they are not permanent resources.

# Gaspar's Reclamation

![[Images/nicolas-bouvier-artwork-images.jpg]]

Gaspar's Reclamation Garage occupies a sprawling garage on the edge of The Rock, a chaotic wonderland of magical artifacts in various states of disassembly. The air crackles with arcane energy, and workbenches overflow with components salvaged from a hundred worlds.

Unlike the mass-produced goods flooding Spamazon's markets, Gaspar specializes in what he calls "analog" magic items—classic artifacts recovered from groundling planets' dungeons and ruins. His fleet of reclaimer ships scours the Known Spheres for these treasures, bringing them back to be studied, restored, and "rigged" with modifications.

- **Gaspar** - Middle-aged human reclaimer and founder of Reclamations. His passion to preserve magical artifacts through innovative workarounds has put him and his company in a neitch market. He speaks in technical jargon about his quest to develop universal principles of magic item crafting.
- **Meat** - Tiefling "professional test dummy" who treats wild magic mishaps like minor inconveniences. This perpetual couch-crasher (currently on Gaspar's couch) will do any odd job for the right price, has no concept of personal space, and maintains a sunny disposition despite regularly being transformed by magical accidents.

## Mundane Item Crafting

Tilly maintains several top-notch workbenches equipped with tools for everything from metalworking to fine leathercraft. For a reasonable fee, you can use these facilities to craft non-magical equipment, weapons, armor, or other gear.

**⏱️ Duration:** GP cost ÷ 50 days\
**💰 Cost:** Materials worth half the item's selling cost

### Requirements

- Appropriate tools and proficiency in their use
- Raw materials worth half the item's selling cost
- Safe storage space for works in progress (provided at Tilly's)
  $\text{Crafting Time (days)} = \frac{\text{Raw Matterials Cost (gp)}}{50}$

### How It Works

1. Determine what you want to craft and its gold piece value
2. Acquire raw materials worth half that value
3. Calculate crafting time by dividing the item's raw material cost by 50
4. Use the appropriate tools to complete the crafting process

> [!NOTE] Collaboration
>
> Multiple characters can work together to reduce crafting time. Divide the time needed by the number of qualified collaborators (all must have the appropriate tool proficiency).

### Tool Proficiency Guide

|Proficiency|Items You Can Craft|
|---|---|
|Herbalism kit|Antitoxin, potion of healing|
|Leatherworker's tools|Leather armor, boots|
|Smith's tools|Armor, weapons|
|Weaver's tools|Cloaks, robes|

## Crime

When the main yard shuts down and the office lights go dark, Tilly's Jamstop transforms into a staging ground for those seeking less legitimate income. The labyrinthine piles of scrap provide perfect cover for planning operations targeting various marks around The Rock.

**⏱️ Duration:** 1 day\
**💰 Cost:** At least 25 gp for information gathering

### How It Works

1. Spend 25 gp gathering information on potential targets
2. Choose a DC based on your target and desired profit:

|Target|DC|Potential Value|
|---|:-:|---|
|Struggling merchant|10|50 gp|
|Prosperous merchant|15|100 gp|
|Noble|20|200 gp|
|Richest figures in town|25|1,000 gp|

3. Make three skill checks, all against your chosen DC:

$\text{Infiltration Check} = d20 + \text{Dexterity modifier} + \text{Stealth proficiency (if applicable)}$

$\text{Acquisition Check} = d20 + \text{Dexterity modifier} + \text{Thieves' Tools proficiency (if applicable)}$

$\text{Exfiltration Check} = d20 + \text{(Int, Wis, or Cha)} + \text{(Investigation, Perception, or Deception))}$

### Results

| Successful Checks | Outcome                                                                                                                            |
| :---------------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|         0         | **Caught and jailed**: Another player can choose to pay a fine equal to potential profit and spend 1 day in jail per 25 gp of fine |
|         1         | **Heist fails but you escape**: No reward, but no punishment                                                                       |
|         2         | **Partial success**: Gain half the potential value                                                                                 |
|         3         | **Complete success**: Gain full value                                                                                              |

> [!WARNING] Criminal Record
>
> Being caught committing crimes may affect your reputation on The Rock and limit future opportunities with legitimate businesses or officials. The Princes of Bral maintain records of known criminals.

## Gambling

The Black's Jack Casino floor is a sensory explosion of flashing lights, holographic displays, and the constant chime of winnings. From classic card games to exotic alien gambling systems, the possibilities for winning—or more likely losing—a fortune are endless.

**⏱️ Duration:** 1 day\
**💰 Cost:** Stake of 10-1,000 gp (your choice)

### How It Works

1. Choose your stake amount (minimum 10 gp, maximum 1,000 gp)
2. Make three skill checks:

$\text{Wisdom (Insight)} = d20 + \text{Wisdom modifier} + \text{Insight proficiency (if applicable)}$

$\text{Charisma (Deception)} = d20 + \text{Charisma modifier} + \text{Deception proficiency (if applicable)}$

$\text{Charisma (Intimidation)} = d20 + \text{Charisma modifier} + \text{Intimidation proficiency (if applicable)}$

> [!TIP] Gaming Set Proficiency
>
> If you have proficiency with an appropriate gaming set, you can replace the relevant skill in any of these checks with your gaming set proficiency.

For each check, the DC is determined randomly:

$\text{DC} = 5 + 2d10$

### Results

|Successful Checks|Outcome|
|:-:|---|
|0|**Complete Loss**: Lose all the money you bet and accrue a debt equal to that amount|
|1|**Partial Loss**: Lose half the money you bet|
|2|**Modest Win**: Gain your bet plus half again more (150% of your bet)|
|3|**Jackpot**: Gain double the amount you bet (200% of your bet)|

## Virtual Combat Arena

_Battleborn Scro Power 3_, Black's Jack's Virtual Combat Arena, features state-of-the-art combat simulation technology. It has also become one of the most respected fighting games in the galaxy, with tournaments hosted every week.

**⏱️ Duration:** 1 day\
**💰 Cost:** None

### How It Works

1. Enter the arena and select your difficulty tier
2. Make three skill checks:
   $\text{Strength (Athletics)} = d20 + \text{Strength modifier} + \text{Athletics proficiency (if applicable)}$
   $\text{Dexterity (Acrobatics)} = d20 + \text{Dexterity modifier} + \text{Acrobatics proficiency (if applicable)}$
   $\text{Constitution (Special)} = d20 + \text{Constitution modifier} + \text{Largest Hit Die roll}$

> [!NOTE] Weapon Option
>
> If desired, you can replace one of these skill checks with an attack roll using one of your weapons. The arena's sensory interface allows your actual weapon techniques to be translated into the simulation.

For each check, the DC is determined randomly:
$\text{DC} = 5 + 2d10$
A separate DC is generated for each check, representing the variable challenges presented by different opponents.

### Results

|Successful Checks|Prize|
|:-:|---|
|0|**Eliminated**: Lose your bouts, earning nothing|
|1|**Rookie Tier**: Win 50 gp|
|2|**Contender Tier**: Win 100 gp|
|3|**Champion Tier**: Win 200 gp|

> [!TIP] Did someone say Sports Gambling?
>
> Non participating players can bet on companions' matches even if you're not fighting yourself. When placing a bet, you sets odds as ratios (e.g., 3:1) - the first number is what you win per gold wagered if correct. Example: Betting 20 gold at 3:1 odds on your friend reaching Champion Tier would pay 60 gold plus your original bet if they succeed. Odds reflect expected outcomes: favorites have lower ratios (2:1), underdogs have higher ratios (5:1). AR displays show live-updating odds throughout matches.

## Relaxation

When the void gets too vast and the job too intense, Black's Jack offers multiple venues for unwinding. From VR lounges to massage parlors to immersive theater experiences, there's something for every taste.

**⏱️ Duration:** 1 day\
**💰 Cost:** Modest lifestyle (1 gp per day)

### How It Works

Maintain at least a modest lifestyle for the day and choose from various recreational activities:

- **Immersive Cinema**: Experience stories from across the spheres in full sensory detail
- **Therapeutic Services**: Massages, meditation chambers, and rejuvenation tanks
- **Live Entertainment**: Music, dance, and theatrical performances from diverse traditions

### Benefits

- Advantage on saving throws to recover from long-acting diseases and poisons
- At the end of the day, you can end one effect preventing hit point regeneration OR restore one reduced ability score
- This benefit cannot be used if the harmful effect was caused by magic with an ongoing duration

# The Starcade

![[Images/EC9oGm5VUAAs6RR.jpg]]

The Starcade stands as a relic of a bygone era — an abandoned entertainment complex that now belongs to you and your crew. Once filled with the beeps and flashes of arcade cabinets and the excited shouts of children, this sprawling facility has fallen into disrepair after years of neglect.

But in your hands, it can become something more: a headquarters, a refuge, a base of operations that evolves with your adventures. Using the bastion system, you can transform The Starcade into a personalized stronghold that reflects your character and provides valuable services during your downtime.

> [!NOTE] Bastion Management
>
> Managing The Starcade uses the Bastion system from the 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide. This is more involved than typical downtime activities but provides more substantial and lasting benefits.

## Bastion Basics

As owners of The Starcade, you can customize and expand it over time. Your Bastion provides:

- A personal stronghold that grows with your character
- Specialized facilities that provide mechanical benefits
- Loyal hirelings who maintain and operate facilities
- A creative space for roleplaying and story development

### Level Requirements

The Starcade becomes available when you reach level 5. The number of special facilities you can have increases as you level up:

| Level | Special Facilities |
| :---: | :----------------: |
|   5   |         2          |
|   9   |         4          |
|  13   |         5          |
|  17   |         6          |

## Bastion Structure

The Starcade consists of basic and special facilities. Think of basic facilities as the essential rooms of your headquarters, while special facilities are specialized areas that provide game benefits.

### Basic Facilities

The Starcade starts with two free basic facilities:

- One Cramped facility (4 squares on map)
- One Roomy facility (16 squares on map)

Choose from these options:

- Staff Quarters
- Food Court
- Parlor
- Laser Tag Arena
- Prize Warehouse
- Storage

#### Adding Basic Facilities

You can add more basic facilities by spending money and time:

|Facility Size|Cost|Time Required|
|---|---|---|
|Cramped (4 squares)|500 gp|20 days|
|Roomy (16 squares)|1,000 gp|45 days|
|Vast (36 squares)|3,000 gp|125 days|

#### Enlarging Basic Facilities

You can expand existing basic facilities:

|Size Increase|Cost|Time Required|

### Special Facilities

Special facilities are where the real benefits of your Bastion appear. Each provides unique advantages based on orders you issue.

> [!IMPORTANT] Facility Requirements
>
> Each special facility has level requirements and may have class-specific prerequisites. For example, an Arcane Study requires the ability to use an Arcane Focus or a tool as a Spellcasting Focus.

## Issuing Orders

Each Bastion turn (1 day), you can issue orders to your special facilities if you're present at The Starcade. If you're away, The Starcade defaults to the "Maintain" order unless you use magic to communicate with your hirelings.

Available orders include:

### Craft

Hirelings create an item appropriate to the facility. During crafting, the facility cannot be used for other purposes.

$\text{Crafting Time (days)} = \frac{\text{Item Cost (gp)}}{50}$

### Empower

The facility grants a temporary bonus or benefit to you or an ally.

### Harvest

Hirelings collect resources produced by the facility.

### Maintain

The entire Starcade focuses on maintenance rather than special orders. This triggers a roll on the Bastion Events table (see below).

### Recruit

Hirelings bring new defenders or allies to The Starcade.

### Research

Hirelings gather information on a topic of your choice.

### Trade

Hirelings buy and sell goods or services related to the facility.
