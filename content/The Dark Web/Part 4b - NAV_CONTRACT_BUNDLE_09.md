> #### IEF MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
>
> **CLASSIFICATION: SABLE-7 | EYES ONLY**
> **NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION BEYOND DESIGNATED RECIPIENTS**
>
> ---
>
> **MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING**
> **Between the Office of Strategic Acquisitions, Elven Imperial Fleet**
> **And the Independent Contractor Designated 2NGEL**
>
> **Reference Number:** IEF-OSA-1364-0077
> **Date of Execution:** 14th Day of Ches, 1364 DR
> **Clearance Required:** Admiralty Level or Above
>
> ---
>
> ##### I. PARTIES
>
> This Memorandum of Understanding ("MoU") is entered into between the **Office of Strategic Acquisitions** ("OSA"), acting under the authority of the Admiralty Council of the Elven Imperial Fleet ("the Fleet"), and the independent research contractor operating under the professional designation **2NGEL** ("the Contractor"), whose credentials have been verified through separate channels and whose identity is held under sealed classification per OSA Protocol 9.
>
> ##### II. PURPOSE
>
> This MoU establishes the terms under which the Contractor shall develop, refine, and deliver a compound provisionally designated **Compound Sable** for evaluation and potential deployment by the Fleet in the ongoing conflict against scro and affiliated hostile forces (herein referred to as "the Adversary").
>
> The Contractor has presented extensive research into the neurological and psionic architecture of orcoid species, with particular emphasis on the scro. While the scro have distinguished themselves from their orcoid predecessors through rigorous cultural conditioning and martial discipline, the Contractor's findings indicate that the underlying neurological substrate remains fundamentally unchanged. The aggression response patterns, the territorial dominance impulses, and the reflexive hostility toward perceived rivals that characterized the goblinoid hordes of the First Unhuman War are not absent in the scro. They have been suppressed. Trained over. Built upon. But the foundation remains.
>
> Compound Sable is described by the Contractor as a bio-arcane aerosolized agent designed to interact with this underlying architecture. Upon exposure, the compound strips away the learned behavioral conditioning that allows scro units to function as a coordinated military force, exposing the raw, unmodulated aggression beneath. Affected subjects do not become passive or incapacitated. They become what their ancestors were: violent, disorganized, and unable to distinguish between ally and enemy. Unit cohesion, tactical reasoning, and hierarchical obedience collapse as the natural state reasserts itself.
>
> The Contractor has represented that Compound Sable is species-targeted, keyed to neurological structures unique to orcoid physiology, and will produce no significant cognitive or physiological effects on elven, human, dwarven, or other non-orcoid personnel. The Fleet's Medical Research Division has been granted access to preliminary trial data supporting this claim, and independent verification is ongoing.
>
> ##### III. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CONTRACTOR
>
> The Contractor agrees to the following:
>
> a) To continue research and development of Compound Sable at facilities and through supply chains of the Contractor's own arrangement. The Fleet will not be informed of, nor assume responsibility for, the locations or methods of said research, though acknowledge that delivery of supplies will be required to discreetly be delivered in outer regions of philogistion, commonly Scro territory.
>
> b) To deliver, upon reaching operational readiness, a sufficient quantity of Compound Sable to enable sustained deployment across a theater-scale engagement zone, defined as no fewer than three (3) simultaneous battlefield applications covering a combined effective area of no less than twelve (12) square nautical leagues. The Contractor will additionally provide detailed deployment protocols, including recommended dispersal altitude, optimal atmospheric conditions, expected onset duration, and minimum safe distance for allied personnel.
>
> c) To maintain absolute operational security regarding this agreement. The Contractor shall not disclose the existence of Compound Sable, the nature of the research, or the identity of the commissioning party to any third party, governmental body, mercantile organization, or independent actor, under any circumstances.
>
> ##### IV. OBLIGATIONS OF THE FLEET
>
> The Fleet agrees to the following:
>
> a) To compensate the Contractor in the amount of **75,000 gold crowns per quarter-cycle** for the duration of the development period. Payment shall be disbursed through the OSA's financial operations division via **encoded ledger transfer to the Contractor's designated arcane-locked vault address**, a unique receiving sequence provided by the Contractor and verified through dual-signature confirmation. No physical currency shall change hands. No transfer records shall be entered into the Fleet's general accounting systems. The OSA's financial operations division shall maintain a separate, sealed ledger accessible only to the Director of Strategic Acquisitions and the signatory Admiral.
>
> b) To extend to the individual known as **Theo Arytiss**, confirmed by the OSA as the person operating under the designation 2NGEL, provisional diplomatic immunity within all spheres in which the Fleet maintains jurisdictional authority. This immunity shall shield the named individual from detention, search, seizure, interrogation, or extradition by Fleet personnel, Fleet-affiliated constabulary forces, Sindiath Line security operations, and any civil or criminal authority operating within Fleet jurisdiction. The Fleet acknowledges and pardons outstanding warrants and criminal proceedings existing against the named individual. This immunity is contingent upon the Contractor's continued compliance with the terms of this MoU and may be revoked at the sole discretion of the Admiralty Council.
>
> c) To refrain from conducting independent surveillance of the Contractor's research operations, supply chains, or personnel, except in cases where credible evidence of a breach of this MoU has been presented to the Admiralty Council.
>
> d) To restrict knowledge of this MoU and the existence of Compound Sable to a compartmentalized circle of no more than twelve (12) designated individuals within Fleet Command. The existence of this agreement shall not be entered into any general Fleet record, log, or archive.
>
> ##### V. ETHICAL AND LEGAL STANDING
>
> Both parties acknowledge that Compound Sable, as a bio-arcane weapon targeting cognitive function, occupies a contested position under the Elven Accords on Conduct in Warfare (EACW), specifically Provision 14.3 regarding the use of agents that impair cognition or volition.
>
> The Fleet's position, as advised by the Office of the Judge Advocate, is that Compound Sable does not violate Provision 14.3 on the grounds that the compound does not introduce foreign cognitive states, but rather removes artificial behavioral conditioning to restore the subject's natural neurological baseline. The Fleet further notes that the EACW was drafted with the assumption that both parties to a conflict would be signatories, and that the Adversary has never recognized, adopted, or adhered to the Accords.
>
> The Contractor has offered no opinion on this matter and has requested that this section of the MoU be noted as reflecting the Fleet's legal interpretation exclusively.
>
> ##### VI. TERMINATION
>
> This MoU may be terminated by either party, at any time and for any reason, through written notice delivered via the established secure communication channel. Upon termination, all materials, data, and documentation related to Compound Sable in the possession of the terminating party shall be destroyed or returned as specified in Annex D. All financial obligations incurred prior to the date of termination shall be honored in full. No further payments shall be issued following termination.
>
> In the event that the broader conflict concludes before Compound Sable reaches field-ready status, the Fleet reserves the right to either continue or terminate development at its discretion.
>
> ##### VII. SIGNATURES
>
> **For the Elven Imperial Fleet:** Admiral Eleanor Rigby, Office of Strategic Acquisitions _Sealed under Admiralty Authority_
>
> **For the Contractor:** 2NGEL _No seal. No title. Signature only._

> #### SCRO HIGH COMMAND MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
>
> **CLASSIFICATION: IRON CIRCLE**
> **BY ORDER OF THE WAR COUNCIL — RESTRICTED TO COMMAND TIER ONLY**
>
> ---
>
> **MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING**
> **Between the War Council of the Scro Ascendancy**
> **And the Independent Contractor Designated 2NGEL**
>
> **Council Reference:** WC-EXT-1364-012
> **Date of Execution:** 9th Day of Tarsakh, 1364 DR
> **Authorization:** War Council Quorum, Unanimous
>
> ---
>
> ##### I. PARTIES
>
> This Memorandum of Understanding ("MoU") is entered into between the **War Council of the Scro Ascendancy** ("the Council"), acting as the supreme governing and military authority of the scro people, and the independent research contractor operating under the professional designation **2NGEL** ("the Contractor").
>
> The Council notes that the Contractor approached the Ascendancy through intermediary channels with a proposal of considerable strategic significance. The Contractor's credentials have been independently assessed by the Council's intelligence directorate and found to be legitimate. The Contractor's true identity remains unknown to the Council. The Council has accepted this condition as a necessary element of the arrangement.
>
> ##### II. PURPOSE AND BACKGROUND
>
> This MoU establishes the terms under which the Contractor shall develop and deliver two interconnected strategic assets for use by the armed forces of the Scro Ascendancy.
>
> **Asset One: Designation "Leviathan"**
>
> The Contractor has previously delivered to the Council a prototype primary-class creature known as a witchlight marauder, cultivated through proprietary bio-arcane methodology developed by the Contractor. The Council's scientists have confirmed the specimen to be a genuine primary marauder, consistent in effectiveness though different in biological structure with the historical weapons created by orc shamans during the First Unhuman War.
>
> Under this MoU, the Contractor shall produce and deliver a fully matured, deployment-ready primary witchlight marauder ("Leviathan"), evolved from the delivered prototype. Leviathan shall be delivered in a state of induced dormancy suitable for transport and strategic positioning. The delivery timeline shall not exceed **five (5) standard years** from the date of execution of this MoU.
>
> **Asset Two: Designation "Protocol Ascendance"**
>
> The historical limitation of the witchlight marauder as a weapon of war has always been the impossibility of control. The original orc shamans who engineered these creatures were destroyed before a reliable command methodology could be preserved. The witchlight key, referenced in fragmentary historical sources, has never been recovered. Without a means of direction, a marauder is as dangerous to the deploying force as it is to the enemy.
>
> The Contractor has proposed a solution. Protocol Ascendance is described as a bio-arcane compound gas that, when administered to a trained orc shaman or psionic operator, will expand the operator's cognitive reach and attunement to a degree sufficient to establish a sustained empathic link with the marauder's residual neural architecture. Under Protocol Ascendance, a prepared operator would be capable of providing directional impulse and target designation to the creature through direct psionic interfacing, bypassing the need for the witchlight key entirely.
>
> The Contractor has represented that this approach works through the operator rather than through the creature. The marauder needs only to be guided. Protocol Ascendance gives the Ascendancy's shamans the capacity to do what their ancestors could not: to wield the marauder as a weapon of precision rather than a force of indiscriminate destruction.
>
> ##### III. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CONTRACTOR
>
> The Contractor agrees to the following:
>
> a) To produce and deliver the fully matured Leviathan within five (5) standard years from the date of execution, in a state of induced dormancy, to a location and through a transfer method to be mutually agreed upon no later than one (1) year prior to the delivery date.
>
> b) To develop Protocol Ascendance to a stage of operational readiness within the same five-year period. Upon completion, the Contractor shall deliver sufficient quantities of the compound for a minimum of four (4) field-ready applications, along with comprehensive documentation detailing administration protocols, operator preparation requirements, effective duration of the psionic link, and known risks.
>
> c) To provide quarterly progress briefings on both Leviathan and Protocol Ascendance, delivered through the established secure intermediary.
>
> d) To provide advisory support during initial field testing of Protocol Ascendance, should the Council request it.
>
> e) To maintain total secrecy regarding this agreement, the existence of both Leviathan and Protocol Ascendance, and all related research. The Contractor shall not disclose any element of this arrangement to any party, whether governmental, mercantile, or private, under any circumstances.
>
> ##### IV. OBLIGATIONS OF THE COUNCIL
>
> The Council agrees to the following:
>
> a) To grant the Contractor safe passage and operational immunity within all territories, stations, and vessels under the authority of the Scro Ascendancy. This includes immunity from detention, interrogation, or search by military police, intelligence personnel, or any subordinate authority. This immunity extends to the Contractor's designated agents and supply carriers, provided they are identified through the agreed signaling protocol.
>
> b) To provide the Contractor, upon request, with biological samples, psionic baseline data, and access to volunteer test subjects from among the Ascendancy's psionic corps. All such cooperation will be managed through the Council's Directorate of Special Projects and will not be disclosed to the general officer corps.
>
> c) To restrict knowledge of this MoU and Protocol Ascendance to the members of the War Council and no more than six (6) additional personnel designated by unanimous Council vote. No written or encoded reference to Protocol Ascendance shall enter any military record, operational log, or communication channel not specifically designated for this purpose.
>
> ##### V. STRATEGIC CONTEXT
>
> The Council enters into this agreement with full awareness of its weight. The scro people have fought this war with honor, discipline, and sacrifice. The teachings of Dukagsh have sustained us through losses that would have broken a lesser people. We do not seek weapons of terror. We seek weapons of conclusion.
>
> The elven Imperial Fleet has proven a more resilient adversary than our initial projections allowed. Their capacity to absorb losses, to rebuild, and to adapt has extended this conflict beyond the timeline envisioned by the original war plan. The Ascendancy's reserves of ships, personnel, and materiel are not inexhaustible. The Council has a duty to its people to pursue every viable path toward a decisive resolution.
>
> Leviathan and Protocol Ascendance, together, represent such a path. A single directed deployment against a critical elven installation would fundamentally alter the strategic calculus of this war. It would demonstrate to the Fleet that continued resistance carries a cost beyond anything they have yet imagined. It would, in the Council's assessment, bring the elves to the negotiating table on terms favorable to the Ascendancy.
>
> This is not a decision made in desperation. It is a decision made in clarity.
>
> ##### VI. TERMINATION
>
> This MoU may be terminated by either party, at any time and for any reason, through written notice delivered via the established secure intermediary channel. No justification or explanation is required. Upon termination, all materials and data shall be handled according to the disposal terms in Schedule 3. All financial obligations incurred prior to termination shall be honored in full. No further payments shall be issued following termination.
>
> Should the broader conflict reach a negotiated conclusion before Leviathan and Protocol Ascendance achieve operational readiness, the Council reserves the right to continue development independently.
>
> ##### VII. SIGNATURES
>
> **For the War Council of the Scro Ascendancy:** General Llib Wolagnub, Chair of the War Council
>
> **For the Contractor:** 2NGEL _No seal. No title. Signature only._
