The crew had the Tablet, and they had Echo, a psionic prodigy who can crack any encryption. Echo broke the Tablet's cipher, but the files inside stayed locked behind passwords.

The passwords were out in public. Three websites reached the crew one at a time, and each one hid a key. Every key opened another file on the Tablet, and every file pulled the crew deeper into Project Cain.
