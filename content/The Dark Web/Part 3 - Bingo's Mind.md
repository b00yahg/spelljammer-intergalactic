The stranger from the chat came back with a gift. It was a data file lifted from the Pragmatic Order of Thought, and it held the digital parts of [[Bingo]]'s mind.

Bingo is a tiefling gang member modded with so much magic hardware that his body and his mind are more machine than man. Years of wear and demonic influence have scrambled what's left in there. Clear the encryption, and the file starts to talk.

As the players opened up the program, they played through his carnival games to pull eight memory fragments out of his head. Each of those are bellow.
