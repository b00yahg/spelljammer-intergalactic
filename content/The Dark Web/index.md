Between sessions, the crew kept getting messages they were never supposed to see. Somebody out there was feeding them secrets, one cipher at a time. This is the full record of that trail, in the order the crew found it.

Every puzzle here is open. If you want to play along, work each puzzle before you open the answer. If you just want the story, open it and keep reading.

## The Files

- [[Part 1 - Error Message 3000]] is where it started. A stranger breaks into the crew's chat, speaking only in code.
- [[Part 2 - The Many Lives of 2NGEL]] digs under every name 2NGEL has worn to find his true one.
- [[Part 3 - Bingo's Mind]] cracks open a thief's head and finds a carnival inside.
- [[The Dark Web/Part 4 - The Cain Protocol/index|Part 4 - The Cain Protocol]] hands Echo the Tablet and sends the crew hunting for passwords across the public web.
