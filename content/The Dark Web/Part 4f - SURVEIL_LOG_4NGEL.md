> ##### ATHAS
>
> - Psionic-dense world, completely isolated from the wider cosmology. No spelljamming infrastructure, no portal network, no divine presence.
> - The local population has no awareness that other worlds exist. Psionically sophisticated but technologically primitive.
> - Magic on Athas functions through "defiling," which drains life energy from the surrounding environment. This is relevant to the facility's operations.
> - **The vessel buried at Site Null is the only known entry/exit point to the planet's surface.** 2NGEL's ship cannot enter Athas's atmosphere directly due to the world's planar isolation and the absence of spelljamming-compatible conditions in the local space.
>
> ##### 2NGEL: TRANSIT METHOD
>
> - 2NGEL's ship maintains a **stationary position in the Athasian upper void**, well above the atmospheric boundary, cloaked behind a sustained illusion field that mimics the local starfield. From the surface, it is invisible. From orbit, it looks like empty space.
> - To reach the surface, 2NGEL **drops.** There is no elegant way to say this. He seals himself in a reinforced descent pod, ejects from the ship's ventral bay, and falls through the atmosphere on a ballistic trajectory to a **fixed landing zone in the open wastes.** The pod is shielded against thermal stress but is not magically propelled. It is, functionally, a rock with a man inside it. From the landing point, the nearest settlement — the city of Balic — lies a couple of hours' march to the east.
> - Deceleration is handled by a burst of psionic levitation in the final seconds before impact. 2NGEL initiates this himself. If he mistimes it, he dies. He has never mistimed it.
> - 2NGEL's standard approach is to head west on foot, make contact with his intermediary, and proceed to Site Null from there under escort. The journey from the Silt Stalker ranging grounds to Site Null takes several hours through open desert.
> - **Return is the reverse.** The pod contains a psionic beacon that the ship locks onto for retrieval. 2NGEL activates the beacon, the ship extends a tractor field, and the pod is pulled back to altitude. This process takes approximately four minutes and is the most vulnerable window in his entire operation.
> - The pod is stored in the vessel's navigation chamber between visits. It is roughly two meters long, oblong, scarred from repeated atmospheric entry. Hard to miss.
>
> ##### SITE NULL: LOCATION
>
> - Crashed Illithid vessel buried approximately four meters beneath sand and shale in the **southern scree wastes, southwest of Balic.**
> - Open rocky badlands transitioning to deep sandy waste. No roads, no trade routes, no settlements within half a day's march in any direction.
> - The site sits within territory claimed by the **Silt Stalker tribe**, a nomadic elven raiding clan split into three sub-clans: Fire Bow, Fire Dagger, and Fire Sword. They are hostile to outsiders but will trade if the price is right.
>
> **Local Contact:**
>
> - 2NGEL maintains an intermediary within the Fire Dagger clan, identified only as **"the Runner."** As he travels into their territory to the west of the pod landing, the runner quickly finds him
> - The Runner manages supply movements, initiates return psionic beacon, provides advance warning of Balican patrols, and ensures Silt Stalker raiding parties avoid Site Null. Payment is in water and obsidian.
> - The Runner knows the exact route to Site Null and has made the trip dozens of times.
> - 2NGEL uses a **spoken passphrase with the Runner** to confirm identity at the waystation contact point. I have not been able to determine the current phrase, but prior intercepts suggest he rotates it on a **lunar cycle tied to Athas's moon Ral.** The Runner will not engage without it.
>
> ##### ANDROPINIS
>
> - Sorcerer-king of Balic, 2NGEL's primary research partner on Athas. One of the most powerful beings on the planet. Treats the project as an intellectual exercise and a further opportunity of colonial collateral. This checks out.
> - Andropinis visits Site Null on a **predictable schedule: every 7 local days, arriving at midmorning, departing before dusk the same day.** He does not stay overnight. He does not deviate from this pattern. He has maintained this cadence for the entire duration of the project.
> - His arrival and departure are instantaneous. A portal manifestation has been observed at a fixed point near the primary entry, consistent with an arcane teleportation circle. The origin point is presumed to be somewhere within his palace complex in Balic, though this has not been confirmed.
> - He travels with a minimal escort — two to four templars who remain at the surface entry point. They do not enter the facility.
> - **Communication with 2NGEL has lapsed.** Andropinis has not received a relay transmission in some time and may assume the project is on hold.
> - He has his own interests. They never fully aligned with 2NGEL's. Whether he represents a potential asset or an additional threat depends entirely on how he is approached and what he is told.
>
> ##### SITE NULL: FACILITY
>
> Here is what I have been able to piece together about the interior layout, based on relay intercepts, 2NGEL's filed reports, and the Andropinis documentation.
>
> **Entry Points:**
>
> - **Primary:** Port-side hull breach. This is the main access, partially reinforced with off-world resin compounds. Descends into the mid-deck.
> - **Secondary:** Ventilation shaft approximately 40 meters northeast of the main entry, concealed beneath a cairn of stacked scree stones. Narrower. Leads directly to the lower deck near the biopools.
>
> **Interior:**
>
> - **Upper deck — Navigation chamber.** Primary workspace. Contains the psionic relay used for off-world communication and the descent pod. Heavy ambient psionic resonance from decades of residual crew imprint. Expect disorientation on first entry.
> - **Mid-deck — Containment wards.** Former holding and observation cells for research subjects. May still contain monitoring equipment and residual psionic shielding apparatus.
> - **Lower deck — Biopools.** Six cultivation basins containing active Cradle Fluid. This is the heart of the operation. The biopools are maintained by the vessel's autonomous biological systems and will continue functioning indefinitely without supervision.
>
> **Access Keys:**
>
> - The navigation chamber relay requires a **psionic authentication sequence** to initiate off-world communication. 2NGEL uses a specific psionic signature pattern that I have partially reconstructed from relay intercept data. The partial sequence is appended to this report as Attachment A.
> - The sealed storage cases on sub-level 2 (containing supplementary research documentation, culture batch records, and subject session logs) require **dual-key arcane authorization: Andropinis and 2NGEL simultaneously.** Neither key alone opens them. This is relevant if the documentation becomes a priority target.
>
> ##### 2NGEL: COMMUNICATION AND VISIT SCHEDULE
>
> **Communications:**
>
> - 2NGEL checks in via the psionic relay on a **standard 30-day cycle.** These are brief, structured transmissions: status updates, supply requests, data summaries. Usually under ten minutes.
> - He has missed a scheduled check-in as of this report. This is due to the destruction of his ship by the Ocean Court Express.
> - Standard physical visits are unannounced and few, but the furthest they have been apart is **120 local days.** Duration: 2-4 days on-site. Activities: biopool inspection, data review with Andropinis, relay maintenance, meetings with intermediaries.
>
> **Hiding Protocol:**
>
> - When 2NGEL needs to go dark, he retreats to the vessel and **goes underground.** He seals both entry points from inside using the ship's biological systems, which can grow tissue barriers across breaches in a matter of hours, locking all doors with his psionic code.
> - He has used this protocol twice in the observed period, both times for durations of approximately five days.
> - **While sealed in, he cannot be reached by relay.** The tissue barriers dampen the signal. This is the only window during which he is truly out of contact.
>
> ##### BIOPOOLS: NEUTRALIZATION
>
> - The Cain cultures are stable and self-sustaining. They do not require 2NGEL's presence to maintain viability.
> - **Draining the Fluid** is impractical. The substrate is psionically active and resists removal from the basins.
> - **Introducing a resonance-disrupting contaminant** is theoretically possible but I do not know what compound would achieve this. Someone with expertise in Illithid biotechnology or advanced psionic disruption might.
> - **Biopool Three contains the marauder specimen.** It has been growing for over a year. I do not know its current developmental stage.
