Anyhow, as to H4G, we discuss the first move.

We speak with Darrett about what we know about H4G. He's an astral elf, normal social media kinda comedian. Posts "reviews" about other creators. But this is new to him, this energy. He's talked about having an engineering degree before, but... never used it for something like this.

Otto says that he thinks most of these creators aren't educated; is he an industry plant? His history is a bit unknown by Darrett, and a lot of his old posts have been scrubbed.

We discuss getting that "invitation". Maybe we hack around, or... maybe we find one of her highest spenders, and try to get that invitation from them?

Claire and Pixie start tracking down as much information as they can on highest spenders, and Pixie tries to essentially track their IP addresses. They do find a good hit: someone on the southern end of the [[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]], not actually too far from Tilly's Truck Stop. However, trawling their digital footprint, we know they spend most of their time at Black's Jack's Arcade. He's one of Bambi's top 5 spenders, and when he doesn't spend his time watching her, he seems to spend his time becoming a pro at various competitive games. A pro in Scro Smasher, a platinum gold triple on Sorcerer Supreme, and has logged thousands of hours in Final Fatality. He's got a bit of niche internet microcelebrity status even outside Bambi's community. We identify him name, Adam Thrall, but he usually uses his gamer tag, Master Beef.

D'Arby asks if it's really worth doing the bounty. Of course we're not, says Otto. "Of course we are!" says Claire. She and Otto argue a bit about whether it's worth taking out this bounty. Otto contends that, if content creation is like combat, she's like a knight who does no real combat but has killed a LOT of tadpoles. Claire points out that the tadpoles have a lot of money and are really stupid. We could use their money!

In any case, we have his home address, so Otto suggests starting there.

We enter his apartment complex. With a Detect Magic, there does seem to be a silent _alarm_ kind of cast. Except... it's not a traditional Weave-based thing. It's made of _ki_.

D'Arby says he can help Squall with that, and they work on unlocking the door and disabling the trap _at the same time_.

It's a very old apartment with a couple doors on one side, a kitchen. It's relatively clean, and smells of lavender, scrubbed with uncanny discipline. A mahogany table and an old TV playing some ancient game show. Neon light filters out from under a door. Behind the TV, an old orcish woman is asleep. He lives with his mom.

D'Arby sidles over to Otto. "How are we taking her out, captain?"

Otto sighs and says he doesn't want to involve civilians. Darrett and Claire point out they can use spells to help D'Arby sneak in. Otto insists that this is unacceptable to his code.

D'Arby knocks on the door. That, Otto can accept.

The old woman wakes up and answers the door. D'Arby asks about her son, how he's here about a convention thing.

"Oh, are you here for the LEAN party or whatever? I thought that was Friday." She tries to yell for her son.

D'Arby says he doesn't want to trouble her and we can go get him ourselves.

She concedes that a nice man like himself can't be a burglar and lets him in. She asks if he wants a biscuit. D'Arby asks if it's a right side or left side biscuit. The left siders all do those soft, fluffy biscuits and he doesn't like that. She assures them that these are proper, crunchy cookie biscuits.

We enter. On his desk, there's a computer set up with 5 different monitors. His bed is tucked in. An open drawer in the desk has a journal sitting in it. A pyramid of energy drinks sit atop it. And, a loud hum comes from a giant helmet, like a Spelljammer helm connector.

We discuss our findings: energy drinks half drunk, one seems recent. The computer, Claire assesses, is top of the line. The headset, though, is the true treasure: a 20k gp piece of hardware that is quite rare. Claire explains this to the party, then pops the headset on.

She disappears, with an outline of where she used to be.

She sees herself in a white void, except for a URL typing box that floats in the air.

The party tries to contact her via comms, which works. They report that she's disappeared and asks what she sees. She considers what she knows, and this is in line with how this tech is supposed to work. It's something akin to jacking in or entering a demiplane, but instead it projects your entire life force and most of your physical self, something that is basically like teleportation.

And crucially....

**If you die in the game, you die in real life.**

Claire takes off the headset and shares that we figured out where the meeting is supposed to be. But we need to figure out the command word, Squall says. Seems to be the case, yeah.

D'Arby continues poking around the room. He finds several locations marked in his journal, alongside records of his gaming results, with various hiatuses. Various monk monasteries across the Flow are the locations marked. The dates visited are the same time as his breaks and his win rate spikes after each disappearance.

And, D'Arby finds a place where he's saved his passwords.

We log onto the computer and search through her Spacetreon account. She's left messages for her most-paid subscribers, inviting to adore her, etc... and to Protect Her, at the special event... which takes place in the Dark Weave, unregulated by the [[The Sindiath Line|Sindiath Line]].

The instruction is as follows: Load up a URL, pandora.goodberry, then... "Lock your doors, sit down, open it". To use a headset of Class B or higher (they're the full immersion ones). That will take you to BambiCon.

And one final thing: "Unfortunately, there are many people who aren't part of the [[Bamboys]] Nation. People who are out to bring me harm. We live in this utopia, but if they find us... are you, my brave knight, willing to protect me?" There's a Yes/No prompt, which Master Beef has already clicked.

Now, we have a problem: this is a one-person headset. However... there could be a way to hack it—modifying the system to be able to connect to our personal devices in our comms, and suck our life force in, the same way the headset would.

However, this is jailbreaking, and there's gonna be a glitch: because it's connected to one headset, we use the headset wearer as an anchor: in the digital space, you cannot get more than 30 feet away from the anchor or you get disconnected.

Otto brings up an alternate solution: could we find an arcade and rent/use their headsets? Black's Jack's Arcade does have that. We could rent one for 500gp a day for the party, but Claire would have to hack them a little.

We go to Black's Jack's, where Squall and Vayb may not be allowed. Otto smooth talks the guards at the door, but needs some assurances in order to allow Squall's entry. They want to force us to pick the amount of days up front, but Claire threatens to lambast them online for being mean to a famous customer, so they agree to let us figure it out day by day—as long as we post a picture on her blog of us enjoying their tech. We agree.

He leads us to the headset lounge, warns us of the dangers of off-grid sites, and then leaves. Claire jacks in and gets us off-grid.

As we get ready to jack in, Darrett reports that he's found more H4G stuff. He's begun his QnA, and he seems to be speaking about us. Otto asks him to share it. Darrett asks if he's sure. "It... isn't very flattering." Otto replies that he literally doesn't care.
