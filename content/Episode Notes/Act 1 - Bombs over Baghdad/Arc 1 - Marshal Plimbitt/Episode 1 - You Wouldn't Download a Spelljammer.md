> So, you wanna be Aces.
>
> First thing to know: It's like any other business.
>
> When there's business to be conducted beyond the reaches of civilized space, that business inevitably flows through the meteor-carved streets of the Rock of Bral. This celestial waypoint hangs suspended in the rainbow ocean of the Phlogiston, where vessels known as Spelljammers dock and depart with cargo both mundane and mysterious. Here, in this crossroads of a thousand worlds, everything has its price – and everyone has something to trade.
> At the heart of the Rock's more colorful enterprises lies the profession of the Aces: bounty hunters who deal not in common commodities, but in the capture and delivery of individuals who'd rather not be found.
>
> For those seeking to make their mark in this specialized field, all paths lead to the Laughing Beholder, an establishment that has carved its niche in the lower quarters of the Rock. Its notorious reputation stems not just from its unusual proprietor, but from its exclusive broadcast of "Wildcard" – the weekly show where new bounties are first announced to the public.
> That pursuit has brought the five of you through the neon-lit entrance of the Laughing Beholder. The bar's interior presents a curious blend of worn comfort and otherworldly charm – Vision Crystal Arrays line the walls while a vintage jukebox fills the space with the gentle strums of a spacefarer's ballad. Behind the long counter, a Beholder orchestrates an elaborate dance of floating bottles with its eyestalks, while an astral elf in a fitted black jacket polishes glassware and a pink-haired firbolg in a black dress hums between empty tables. The unusual quiet of the normally bustling establishment only serves to heighten the sense of anticipation as you wait for the broadcast that could change your fortunes forever...

The _Laughing Beholder_ bar is where Wildcard is broadcast. Bounties announced to the public. That broadcast is what brings us here now.

We step in, and **[[Large Luigi]]** greets us—asking if we were gonna cause trouble, and if he would need to bust out the paralyzing ray. He then laughed and explained that that was an old beholder joke.

## Meet the Crew

**[[Vayb|Vayborexes]]**, tall scruffy roguish type, approaches the bar. Hands deep in his pockets and slouching. He finger guns to the waitress, then waves **[[Squall]]** over to him as he sits down.

**Squall** obliges. They are an android, whose face is a blue reflective screen. Following **Vayb** so thoughtlessly that it looks almost like he just has a robot.

**Large Luigi** floats over and takes their orders, getting a beer for **Vayb**. **Squall** inquires about the menu. As he interacts with **Vayb**, **Vayb** notices that **Large Luigi** has many rings on his eye stalks.

Indie rock starts playing as a tall humanoid with a tacky dress shirt saunters over to the bar. He explains that he has no money but asks if the bar would accept an "Eye-Owe-You". **Luigi** asks if he wants to start a tab. He sighs, says he was working on it, and then tries another pun.

On the TV, an ad plays for a (something I didn't fully hear?), paid for by the [[The Sindiath Line|Sindiath Line]]. **[[Otto]]** asks the barkeep what channel is on. The beholder tells him he doesn't need to be formal—his name is **Luigi**. **Otto** laments that the TV isn't on the game.

**Otto**, **Squall**, and **Vayb** start interacting, **Vayb** orders a drink for him, too. They chat about sports for a bit. **Otto** ends up asking their names. **Vayb** says that's kinda privileged information in his line of work, but gives it anyway. **Otto** asks what line of work. **Vayb** asks for his name first—**Octavius van Luxe**, but his friends call him **Otto**. **Squall** says that he is annoyed by this man. **Vayb** tells him that you're not supposed to just say something like that, then asks if they have looked on the menu.

Meanwhile, **[[D'Arby]]** goes looking for a jukebox, wearing his flashy leather jacket motorcyclist-esque getup. He starts perusing the tunes, jamming out to whatever is currently playing. He finds an old plasmoid hit called "Shift Through You"—one of his old bops. He tried to hit the jukebox to get it going, but it failed and began a different song. (DM starts playing Cooking By The Book).

Meanwhile, **[[Claire]]** has been vlogging. **Otto** poses or makes a handsome face whenever her camera catches him. Despite the facelessness, **Squall** looks BAFFLED. **Luigi** tells her to get his good side and strikes a pose himself, then realizes he needs to make drinks.

**Vayb** strolls over to **D'Arby**, tells him that sometimes it's worth the money, then realizes he has no money. Meanwhile, **Otto's** team in the sports game scores a goal, so he orders a round of Death Helm Porter for everyone.

Meanwhile, **Elly** approaches **D'Arby** and welcomes him to the _Laughing Beholder_, asks to take his order. **D'Arby** looks for something sweeter to drink. **D'Arby** idly watches the game on TV and enjoys the atmosphere. Then, realizing Wildcard begins soon, he asks **Elly** the time—it's 5pm. He asks if she can turn the TV on to Wildcard. She says sure, but explains that Wildcard begins in three hours.

All of us _had_ received the information that Wildcard was at 5. It was, however... in a different time zone, **D'Arby** realizes. He apologizes, explaining that he is too used to Elven Standard Time, but the _[[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]]_ uses its own time zone. He thanked **Elly**, embarrassed about the mistake.

**Otto** swings over to reassure **D'Arby**, saying that we have three hours of this (gesturing to the TV), and suggesting they enjoy it together!

## Enter Starfist

Then a towering four-armed giant in audacious clothing and a wide-brimmed feather hat steps in. He's bejeweled all over. He moseys down with the casual menace of someone who knows what he's doing. Quickly following him are three tiny autognome robots, marching in a line. They claim the nearest table. He starts carving initials.

**Elly** approaches and very nervously greets the giant and asks what he needs. He takes his time, still carving, and asks if she can see if the barkeep can make a (Intense mercenary drink name) and orders some purple worm rings.

As this happens, **Claire** returns the porter that **Otto** bought. At the same time **Otto** tries to buy more. She overpays for a strawberry daiquiri.

Meanwhile, **Elly** tells the giant what his total is. He says he has no money, very menacingly. **Vayb**, at the bar, tells **Claire** "Princess. When things go south, get behind the bar." **Otto** takes the time to intervene, and brings his new porters to them. The giant thanks him and downs the drinks rapidly. He tells **Elly** that **Otto** will be able to pay for everything of his. **Otto** tells him that it's okay—we've all been down on our luck, he understands what it's like to have no money.

The rest of our characters notice, at the same time, that this giant bears a remarkable resemblance to a wanted poster on a board on the wall. **Vayb** thumbs over to the poster to **Squall**. **Squall** comments that they were thinking the same.

## Aftermath

After the fight, **Vayb** turns to **Squall** and says "**Squall**! What did we talk about!" They apologize, citing that they realized it was a bad idea after they had already fired the shot.

**Luigi** floats over and tells us that we made a mess of things—a bit of an oopsie daisy—in his bar. **Otto** replies that he thought he had things under control but that things got out of control.

**Large Luigi** offers to split the bounty with us and maybe get a favour later, in return for using a Revivify scroll he keeps under the bar. We ended up accepting. The giant, upon being resurrected, shrieks and raves about how "THERE'S NOTHING!! Oh god, there's nothing!!". **Otto** bonks him over the head to knock him unconscious.

He asks if he should process this bounty with us as a crew—we shrug and agree. Then he asks if we have a ship, which we do not. **Large Luigi** is taken aback by this and informs us that we really need a ship to pursue bounties—most of them don't exactly come walking right into the bar.

**Large Luigi** does list ship sales on the Form—the bulletin board screen in the corner. **Vayb** looks at it and finds a ship listed for 25,000 gold. He balks at this—but then spots a different ad for a cycle race with a reward of 25,000 gold, with an absolutely HYSTERICAL comment section.

We discuss the prospect of taking on this race. **D'Arby**, for his part, seems quite interested—so we surmise we might do it.

As we settle down, the group goes to share the purple worm rings that the giant had ordered. With a race ahead of us, and a ship to win, we end the first session here!
