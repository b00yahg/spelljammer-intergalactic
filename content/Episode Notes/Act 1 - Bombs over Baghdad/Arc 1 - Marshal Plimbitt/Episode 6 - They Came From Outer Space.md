We start with [[Vayb]]!

Disconnecting from a helm can be jarring, swapping from a second state of consciousness back to himself. He was in the _flow_, as some call it—twisting and turning the train through the air. Then he's disconnected, and he's discombobulated, but he focuses himself while forcing himself to not spit up.

[[PIXIE|Pixie]] speaks up. "Helmsman! Your attention is needed in the center cart!"

Vayb forces himself to his feet, wobbles, then he listens to Pixie and departs. He finds the squad in the cart, and hears the sounds of cheering from the comms. Propulsions from the ships of Kalaman returning to their tower.

Claire's familiar pops out and paws at her in shock—the first time the party met k1k1. Vayb arrives, and just stops in shock. [[Squall]] puts a hand on his shoulder, comfortingly. [[D'Arby]] and [[Otto]] are also pretty burnt up. D'Arby silently leans against the wall and tries to smoke his ruined pack of cigarettes.

Vayb tries to coax k1k1 over. D'Arby breaks the silence and asks if it was possible that [[Claire]] turned into a rat to save herself or something. Squall says that they don't think so. Vayb picks up k1k1 and tears up.

He confirms with the crew and that they might be able to do something if they move quickly. The group brings up that that's what they/Luigi did with Starfist—Squall says they'd trade Starfist for her in an instant.

Vayb asks Pixie about how they might move Claire. She says she could raise Claire's body as a zombie. Squall says that sounds horrible. Pixie then says she has a second idea, and presents a small wooden sphere. She does not elaborate. The party yells at her to explain.

She says it's a magic item akin to a bag of holding, that sucks a specific thing into a pocket in the Feywild. Vayb asks if that would work, and they could pull her out later—Pixie says it would, if they don't want to carry her, like a real Ace would! The group ignores that comment, confirms mechanics, and places Claire in the pocket dimension.

Vayb helps D'Arby to his feet, and the party puts Claire into the pokéball. Pixie autopilots the ship down to Kalaman.

The Ocean Court Express draws a curious glance from the people below. The white walls of the city tower. A lighthouse casts a glow across the sea through the morning fog. We guide our ship to the docks and find more traditional vessels in the harbor. Sailors from all around the planet wander the docks. Many funnel into a tavern in the distance. Merchants hawk their wares in the market. Guards patrol the city. The city is full of the smell of the sea, bakeries baking bread, and more. Various other flying ships land toward the center of the city—a cliffside that springs up to a castle, standing and looming protectively over the city.

"Welcome to Kalaman, [[Aces]]."

Vayb stays focused, and asks Pixie where she can point us, to find someone who might be able to resurrect Claire. Pixie lacks specific data, but points us to the palace, or just suggests asking around.

Remembering our communications with General Becklin, the party sets off to the castle. The Knights of Solamnia patrol the area, with arcane weapons in their hands. The castle looks scarred by dragonfire. Vayb gets their attention and says he needs to see someone in the castle. The guards snap to attention and tell him to halt. "No individuals outside the Knights of—" and Vayb interrupts them, telling them to send for Becklin. They stammer, trying to say they have a thing going, but Vayb interrupts them over and over until they get mad and one of them goes to get Becklin. Gregory's partner reassures him that their "one tells the truth, one lies" thing is good, people just don't get the vision.

D'Arby steps in and tries to reassure everyone, saying that tensions are naturally high after the dragons, and that no one means anyone any harm.

The guards concede that, if we really know Becklin they can fetch her—D'Arby mentions that we did speak to her over the comms, which makes the guards realize that we're the "shark people", and they say that Becklin absolutely wants to meet us!

Vayb lights a cigarette, then realizes D'Arby's are basically all gone, so he hands D'Arby an extra cig.

The party is brought to an octagonal table. Various important people discuss around the table. General Becklin is leading some sort of tactical discussion, about how now is the time to strike—then is interrupted by the party.

Vayb says "Hey..." and asks the council for help. They ask who the hell we are and why we're in here. Vayb says we're from the ship and fought for them and that we need help. The council seems to look to Becklin about what to do.

She says that Kalaman is appreciative of our efforts. She says that resurrection spells are scarce, but... they do have one. She calls for [[Darrett]], who scrambles and pants on his way in. She tells him to get the resurrection spell from the vault, after explaining who we are. He looks at us and asks "are they... _wow_." She yells at him to get going.

Darrett returns with a scroll, which she says an arcane caster can use. Squall places Claire's body on the table. Vayb connects psychically to the scroll. Its magic shoots out and brings Claire back. She just goes "_owwwwww_". Vayb welcomes her back to the land of the living. Claire tries to process the fact that she died.

> \[!Squall Note]
> :D

The Knights of Solamnia thank us again. The party goes to leave. One of the councilors asks where we got our weapon—noting how remarkable it is. Vayb says we shouldn't really say. Claire asks where they got theirs—from someone named Civlar. They try to press us, and Vayb explains that we're looking for a peddler of our own, who sells wares like ours, and does so illegally. They try to press us for more detail, but Becklin cuts in and shuts both sides up, telling us they have business and to get out. Vayb offers a genuine apology for causing trouble in our moment of fear.

> \[!Claire Note]
> we get nothing? did i... waste our reward by dying??

The door is then closed on us, leaving us with Darrett. Vayb asks him to show us to the guy in the lighthouse (I might have missed when that came up in the RP, oopsie). Then maybe Sivlar.

Darrett starts nerding out and asking us where we're from. Claire replies that if we tell him, we have to take him with us. Squall pulls us into a huddle to ask about the Prime Directive stuff and how that goes. We establish that what Claire said is basically true, and that the Prime Directive is more on a societal level. Squall tries to check on Claire, but D'Arby keeps us moving while we lok for food and a healer, so we forget about it for the moment.

Darrett tells us that, at the end of the day, his squire position is very important to him and that he's about a year away from becoming a knight, so he doesn't want us to tell him if that's true. D'Arby asks him about his knight training and what it is. Darrett explains a bit. D'Arby tries to test Darrett by weakly karate chopping him, and Darrett competently parries it, impressing D'Arby.

We arrive at the lighthouse, where Darrett warns us that the person inside is a little... and he does the cuckoo gesture. Darrett knocks on the door. Nothing happens. We wait. An eyeball appears in the door, and a voice booms, asking who we are. D'Arby says hey. Claire points at D'Arby. The guy doesn't trust us for a sec, but relents when we agree we're not here to take his birds.

Darrett waits outside. Vayb asks, and Darrett says squires are supposed to wait, and only come in if invited. Vayb and Claire say that he can come if he wants. He reluctantly comes in, seemingly not wanting to enter.

We meet the man inside. He roughly pats down D'Arby. He whispers into D'Arby's ear "if this is a recording device, I'm not saying shit." D'Arby points out he just did. The man says touché, then heals him. Claire is next. She insists that he will just have to trust there are no recording devices. He narrows his eyes. His seagull squawks and the man says it says it likes D'Arby's vibe, so he agrees to let Claire just get her healing. D'Arby also points out she was recently on fire so her equipment would be damaged anyway—Claire realizes in horror that her camera might have been destroyed.

We move on to talking with the man about information. He confirms we were in the aerial battle. The group of people discusses whether we want to share information or keep secrets from each other. But eventually the man relents and asks if we saw any anomalies in the aerial battle. Vayb goes outside to use the comms and confirms with Pixie that we didn't see any anomalies.

The man says this information HAS to stay between us—if it gets shared, calamity would ensue. D'Arby says we're good, we don't really have friends, anyway. The group concedes that Claire probably does, but...

The man pulls out an arcane locked chest and grabs a piece of paper for us. It has an image of a classic grey alien. Vayb and D'Arby inspect it and conclude that it looks like a bastardized drawing of an astral elf. They side eye Claire.

Vayb asks what the picture is and what it means to him. The man replies that it's classified. He and D'Arby if the man drew this himself, and compliment his skills. (He did draw it, actually) The conversation continues until Vayb reassures this man about his beliefs and tells him that he also "wants to believe" in these things.

The man draws the blinds closed and says he has theories. He's a red mage. This means he and his companions study the cosmos, specifically Lunitari, the Red Moon. As a scholar, he must understand and measure all the forces out there, on the scales of battle. It was a simple job for most of his life, however... He thinks there might be forces of nature beyond this world. That this drawing is a sketch of extraterrestrial life. And that "they've come to eat our brains."

D'Arby has zoned out and is looking around for anything to touch or play with. D'Arby grabs a strange stitched cloak.

The man says he thinks this all connects to current events—the dragons' return, the war, all of it.

Squall calls a group huddle to discuss what we think and what we want to do.

(Note: Man's name is Jerrick)

D'Arby asks Jerrick what he wants to do with the aliens—if he's gonna be all genocidal about it. Jerric says he thinks new worlds would be magnificent, but that there is the whole eating brains thing... Claire asks why he thinks that. He hesitates. Tells a story about how he had an encounter with an alien when he was younger, and everyone started acting strange and annoyed by him after he started sharing his theories, so he assumed they were replaced by duplicates.

We chat a while longer with Jerrick about his theories (including lizard people in the government, which is not, thankfully, a dog whistle). Jerrick tells us he's happy that we seem cool about all this. D'Arby says "you're alright, Crazy Jerrick". Jerrick says he wishes that wasn't preceded by Crazy, but he thanks him anyway.

Vayb eventually tries to convince Jerrick to trade for the cloak, offering the trade for something wonderful. Jerrick confirms he's not trying to offer some kind of sexual favour. Vayb confirms he's not. But that he can offer the trip of a lifetime for giving up his worldly possessions. Jerrick says he's good.

Vayb says he can protect Jerrick's brain. Jerrick is interested by this. Vayb tells him to get his aluminum helmet on hand. He tells Jerrick not to put it on just yet, but to put it on when this gets to be too much. He then says he's gonna try to eat Jerrick's mind real quick.

He casts _detect thoughts_ and begins probing into Jerrick's mind. Jerrick feels it, then puts his hat on. Vayb offers that as proof that the hat works, so he can sleep soundly.

Jerrick tells Vayb to cut his hand. He does. Jerrick concedes that he's not bleeding green. And agrees to give him the _cloak of passage_ for that proof.

We departed, cloak in hand, and head out to Civlar's and to find some food.

On the way there, Vayb strikes up a conversation with Darrett to ask if he'd want to go on the adventure of a lifetime. Claire tries to join in by manipulating him about how he did well in the battle and the other knights don't appreciate him. Squall also goes on a long political rant about knights, militarism, and tyranny, to Darrett's dismay.

> \[!Squall Note]
> The military industrial complex has only harmed those whom it is supposed to protect-

We keep on trying to convince him to join us, and keep almost intriguing him. At one point, Squall says "the planet" and we have to walk back that reveal as them mixing up their words. Claire adds some pressure by asking if he needs someone else's permission to be a knight and protect Krynn, and that's kinda what we're here for. Vayb releases some tension by offering a genuine apology for our vagueness, and appreciation for his help, but says Darrett is genuinely welcome in our crew if he wants to come with us.

We arrive at Civlar's shop. Crystal chimes echo as we enter. Polished shelves carry magic items all over the shop. A young woman mans the shop. She's polishing magic wands. Vayb walks into the shop and says hi. The woman doesn't notice until he rings a bell.

She performs a memorized greeting. Vayb asks to speak to Civlar, and is vague about it. Claire jumps in and says we have a business opportunity. Squall murmurs "we do?" and Claire whispers back that "we don't".

A bald mystic-looking man greets us. Says he was told that we have a business opportunity. Vayb takes point and asks about the castle having strange weaponry. Civlar goes on a speech about how he's learned artificery. Vayb asks about his supplier. Civlar says we misunderstand, and that this magic is all his own creation. Claire flicks Vayb on the back of the head and hisses to detect thoughts. He side eyes her mimicking his own thought, but he casts it anyway.

Civlar seems to be annoyed about our presence. Claire switches to asking about learning the craft. He says that she doesn't look to be a mage, so she accuses him of misogyny—he stammers that he just means that she doesn't wear a mage's robes.

Squall asks about the "most distinguished field" of his magic—and his brain fills in the Most Distinguished Field Admiral (blah blah blah) title of our bounty head. Over the conversation, Vayb also sees some sort of space-age communicator pop into his thoughts.

Vayb says that he thinks these things he sells are kinda small time—and that we can help him get into the big time, possibly. Make it really worth his while.

Vayb fails the check and Civlar starts ranting about how Vayb is insulting him and his craft. Vayb grabs him on the shoulder and apologizes, reiterating his point and casting _friends_.

Civlar casts _arcane lock_ on the door behind us. He asks how we know. Vayb replies that he knows how Vaybe knows. Like he said, he's major league.

"Did Becklin send you?"

"No. Luigi did."

"Who's Luigi??"

"Exactly. Like I said, I'm major league."

Vayb threatens him a bit, saying that we don't have much time but we need to speak to the person he's talking about.

Watching the amount of exertion on Civlar's face, it occurs to Squall and Claire that that _arcane lock_ was the most powerful spell he knows.

In the face of Vayb's threats, Civlar breaks down crying about how he doesn't know what's going on, that he was just following Becklin's orders, and he's only ever spoken to [[Marshal Plimbitt|Plimbitt]] like 3 times.

Vayb tries to move on, but Civlar asks why we should let him leave. Vayb convinces him that we're sorry and meant no harm to him personally. He unlocks the door for us.

And Darrett is no where to be seen.
