[[Lets Jam - Ship Combat Mechanics for D&D|Let's Jam]]!

Fight Highlights:

- The fisherman desperately tries to escape, but [[D'Arby]] catches up and cold-cocks him, leaving him for us to interrogate later.
- [[Claire]] tries to test out her brand-new hacking program, slipping into the Weave to try to disable a hippo guardian. She's paralyzed, so it crits, but we discuss the playtest and it's no longer paralysis, it's incapacitation now—BUT it did roll a Nat 20 so the crit still stands. She disables the weapons system the next turn.
- The hippo guardian loses its attack in a puff of pink and blue sparks, and it runs away in fear—[[Squall]] crits on their attack of opportunity. They rush to follow it, since it's moving toward [[Vayb]].
- Claire rolls a Nat 1 to fight a fireward, but the fireward also rolls a Nat 1 so she manages to beat it.
- [[Darrett]] bursts in and asks what's happening—[[Otto]] tells him to be a human shield for Claire. He says "oh! I'm familiar with this."
- Squall crits the hippo guardian going after Vayb AGAIN.
- Claire continues rolling like shit, but manages to take out some firewards because they roll terribly too.
- The rest of the party keeps trying to beat the hippo guardians up.
- Vayb mounts a crane while Claire prepares to jack off and Otto uses his rod to beat off the hippo.
- D'Arby swings his sword, cutting through one of the hippos and causing it to blow up.

As the robots are breaking down and being destroyed, General Becklin bursts in through the door! She yells "Stop, immediately!". Darrett freezes up and panics. She declares that we are under arrest by the Knights of Solamnia.

Squall asks if she's here alone. She is.

Otto tells her she has a lot of nerve telling calling us criminals, when she's illegally trading weapons. She bristles, but he does try to broach peace and talk things out. She stares daggers at Otto, whispers something to herself, and draws her sword. She says that we speak with some sort of authority, but we're strangers with no knowledge of this world, its struggles, and if we have the _audacity_ to threaten her, then "come at me, motherfuckers!"

Squall notes that, if she had any actual authority to arrest us, she wouldn't be here alone. She glares at them, doesn't respond, and doesn't move. Squall tries to read her, but is only able to read one thing: she is entirely unmoved by any of our words, convictions, or faith. Otto tells her that we wanted to talk, and she's the one who wants to fight, so this is on her.

She barks an order to Darrett again. He, shaking, moves to a defensive stance at Claire's incapacitated body.

Claire's turn is next and she immediately gets knocked unconscious by the fireward. She begins convulsing.

> \[!Claire Note]
> note to self: figure out how to do this at range. ouch... :(

Otto asks Becklinn again to speak, pointing out Claire's weak state. Becklinn listens to him, points her sword at Claire, but doesn't do anything yet. Otto calls her dishonourable. She replies that honour doesn't matter in matters of life and death. Otto says that honour matters _most_ when death is on the line.

Darrett, hesitantly, moves and attacks Becklinn, landing a hit. She reacts with surprise and says, "huh."

> \[!Claire Note]
> yessss darrett go offffff

Back to initiative:

- Squall rushes in to Becklinn and activates their new armour feature to force her to attack them.
- Otto steps up. "You want proof that we have authority of a higher power? Very well." His eyes glow gold and runes appear across the vital points in her body as he activates his Hexblade's Curse.
- D'Arby pulls Claire out of the fray. He uses his action surge to take one of Claire's _healing potions_ to heal her.
- D'Arby catches her up on the situation.
- Vayb lands a _hold person_ on General Becklinn. She's _not_ doing well.
- The _hold person_ spell, telekinetically, force chokes Becklinn.
- Darrett apologizes, knocks Becklinn out, and we tie up our new prisoners.

We start dealing with the aftermath of the fight, deciding where to put the prisoners, whether to take the bomb, and Otto and Claire loot some weapons.

Otto and Claire check on Darrett. Otto checks on Claire and asks what happened when she blanked out in the fight, and wanted to see if she was okay. She says that she was trying to hack, and thinks she succeeded. Otto replies that he has a bit of tactical advice: it's very dumb to get so close in a fight if you're not prepared for it. She tells him that "yeah, I figured that part out!!!"—but the vibe is humorous, not genuinely tense.

> \[!Claire Note]
> 🙄

Otto, Vayb, and Claire discuss what to do with the pager once D'Arby gives it to Vayb. As we do, a message from [[Marshal Plimbitt|Plimbitt]] rings. Vayb shows it to us, and tries to temper the fact that he's not very literate.

The message reads: Do you have chess where you're from?

The party is puzzled by this. Vayb asks [[PIXIE|Pixie]] whether this world has chess—interestingly, chess appears on a lot of crystal spheres, where it arises naturally! Claire tells Vayb to just answer yes, since this guy seems dumb and sincere. Plimbitt launches into a long speech about how he thinks that chess is just too simple for his brilliant mind, and how it doesn't even have mana systems or skill trees or--- Vayb just hands the pager to Claire.

Then he asks if the drop went well. Claire replies yes. Plimbitt doesn't believe our lie, since something is wrong with Fisherman John's tracker went dark.

Vayb and Otto rush in to go interrogate him and debate the particulars of a good-cop-bad-cop routine. Vayb points out that he didn't have police procedurals on [[Athas]].

In the meantime, D'Arby's been spending his time with Fisherman John. John wakes up and is scared. D'Arby tries to make conversation with him, and ask how he's doing. He apologizes for hitting John in the face. They make conversation, though D'Arby doesn't get much information out of him, in spite of his good nature. As Otto and Vayb arrive, D'Arby's just setting up a game of solitaire.

Vayb strides in, flicks out a cigarette, and launches into the bad cop routine. John points out that he won't get paid if he talks. Vayb replies that he won't get paid if he _doesn't_ talk. We can pay him if he does, and if he won't, we can throw him into the ocean. Vayb increases the intimidation pressure, stopping just shy of slapping John.

John admits that he picks up the weapons over in the canyon wastes. Vayb offers to untie him if he'll show us how to get there. D'Arby offers to get John a drink, and Vayb leads him to the fridge. As the group strolls out, Otto raises an eyebrow and makes a comment about Vayb bringing people onto the crew.

> \[!Claire Note]
> oh my god, there is NO WAY we're taking someone like fisherman john with us

Otto gives John a little speech about how we throw crew people who are uncooperative over the side of the ship. Claire says that that's what we did to Red Ruin. Vayb adds that she was his lover.

In the meantime, Claire sends a reply to Plimbitt: I said what I said. It went well, but we discovered something about John. He replies that this "sounds serious. I can be there in a pod in 20 minutes."

> \[!Claire Note]
> HA! I DID IT! my idea worked!!!

Claire asks the party how to reply, and we decide to try to meet him at the Giant's Spine in the wastes. Vayb tries to rush the Ocean Court through the canyons to rush straight to the meeting location and get there (and set up) faster than Plimbitt can get there.

As Vayb pilots, a ping pops into his head: he senses incoming _danger_. This area has green mist choking the canyon walls. Crystal forms along the walls. Bombs explode in the distance, booming across the wastes. This is a testing site. The weapons are experimented with and enhanced. And he is immediately aware of the danger. In the back of his mind, the Ocean Court almost seems to warn him. Whimpering, and cries. Of spectres, lingering in the canyon. They stretch up toward the ship—and we see that they're not quite ethereal. Not quite ethereal. Modified, _irradiated_ from the weapons around. With his skill, Vayb narrowly dodges them. The Ocean Court turns sideways, avoiding combat with the undead.

Squall asks Pixie about the area. She says her history database says this place was a burial place, for people who died in the Cataclysms. But she doesn't know what's wrong with the spirits. She guesses they're irradiated.

Squall and Vayb question if there's anything we can do for them. Claire says there is—we're getting Plimbitt. Vayb says he wants to reverse it. Claire says that that's unlikely, when it comes to radiation.

Meanwhile, Otto strolls over to the brig with D'Arby. They chat about whether or not they should try to speak to/interrogate Becklinn. D'Arby points out how even John was reticent to talk, so she'd be more tight-lipped. Otto says he's not quite an interrogator, anyway, but maybe a shift in perspective is needed. He points out how intense and violent she was in that incident, and how she's not embodying the justice she's supposed to. But maybe there's something in there.

He goes to the brig and checks on the now-awake Becklinn. Becklinn is waking up. D'Arby asks if she needs any water. She side eyes him, and asks where she is. "Uhh... boat."

She comments, plainly, that we beat her. Otto replies that he expected more from her. She says she expected more of a fight back as well. Otto says that expectations can bite you in the ass sometimes. He says he doesn't blame her for her worldview, since it's not her fault.

She says, "you're from the same place as the hippo, aren't you? From the stars."

Otto confirms this but doesn't give up any more details. He tells her how he doesn't like hurting or breaking things, unless it's necessary—and she's the one who made it necessary. D'Arby translates that what he means is that we just want Plimbitt. Otto confirms with her that he was making weapons for her. He says he doesn't blame her for feeling it was necessary, but asks why she thought it was.

She asks if he's ever seen a dragon up close. Otto replies that she knows how we got here. Otto moves on to ask her why she's got everything here set up like a criminal organization... why all the
secrecy?

> \[!Claire Note]
> WHAT DO YOU MEAN HAVE I SEEN A DRAGON UP CLOSE YOU JERK 🤬

She finally raises her head and looks Otto in the eyes. "Assuming what you're saying is true, you of all people should know just how terrifying it is to be one on one with the beast. The teeth. The glowing eyes. Most of all... the fire. The thing that gives us life, from them, brings death. And there's nothing you can do about it. Most humans don't know that fear. We never had to worry about that. We had the one thing greater than the dragons—we had the gods on our side. The one thing bigger than a beast is a god... and when they left, our world went to chaos. Our world went to _hell_. Part of the reason things have been set up the way they have is... yes, I was aware that Plimbitt was on the run from some people. Supposedly you. But more than that, humans—the people of Kalaman—needed to feel like they were responsible for their own protection. Our city needed to feel like it was their own ingenuity leading to their survival. So I did what I did to create that illusion."

D'Arby admits that that makes sense to him, even if he doesn't like it. Otto sighs, and says it's interesting. That people want to survive, but... something about all of this doesn't seem right to him. Did the disappearance of their deities... "do you know why they're gone?"

She replies that humanity worshipped the gods for a long time. Fought by their side. So when there was peace, they thought that it would bring improvement. She explains that a long time ago, the king-priest, the leader of the humans, tried to become divine. And they left in punishment. She says that they've been clawing and _begging_ for solutions in the hundreds of years since.

She asks Otto why Plimbitt was the first to come to them. He's a warmonger, and an idiot, sure—but he's the only one from the stars who's HELPING.

> \[!Claire Note]
> ... is he, though?

Otto asks her what the next step is. Win the war with the dragons... what then? He tells her that what she's been living for is... flawed. That you have to live for more. More than war or violence. To be the best kind of person you can be. And sometimes that means more than just doing a duty. He pauses, thinks on his words, then asks if she really thinks what she's been doing will truly win the war.

Otto then returns to the party and reports the conversation to them. He asks us what we think we should do about this world.

This led to a long conversation about what to do with Krynn once we've dealt with Plimbitt.

> \[!Writer's Note]
> As the note taker, I was too engrossed in the conversation to actively keep up my note-taking. However, here is a summary of each character's stance:
> \-- Vayb believes that this is simply beyond our ability or responsibility, and that we should just take Plimbitt in and move on.
> \-- Claire is opposed to any breaking of the Prime Directive, as she believes that exposing these people to the spacefaring civilizations will only cause them problems. Additionally, she doesn't believe there's anything we can we materially do for them by ourselves.
> \-- Otto isn't thinking about space exploration, but he believes that there's something wrong in the hearts of the people here and that we should see if there's anything we can do to mend them—such as by finding a way to return this world's gods by restoring faith in them.
> \-- Squall's opinions are mixed. They believe in bringing Plimbitt to justice, that Becklinn should face justice among her people (not ours, notably), that we shouldn't be taking away Plimbitt's weapons now that they're already here. They believe that people like Darrett are the ideal person to be in this world. In their player's words: "Squall is pro-help, anti-intervention". That we should fix things if we have the power, but not take credit or openly break the Directive.
> \-- D'Arby was not in this conversation, as he was playing Solitaire and watching the prisoners, but in that time, he's decided that Becklinn does have a point, that we and the spacefaring world should help these people, and has made tons of kind-hearted promises that we surely cannot keep.

With the conversation as resolved as it can be, we bring up the question of what to do with Plimbitt. We decide to confront him directly, and as we get ready, credits roll on the episode.
