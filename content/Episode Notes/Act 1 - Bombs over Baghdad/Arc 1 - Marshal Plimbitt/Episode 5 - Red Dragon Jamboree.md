We begin the session where we left off last time!

[[D'Arby]], who has been spacesick, is handling it by hanging around in his cabin—trying to calm himself by going through a ritual of sharpening his knife, but lying on the ground as soon as the OCEAN hits any turbulence. He HATES the feeling of not being on solid ground.

[[PIXIE|Pixie]] asks him if he isn't feeling well. D'Arby grunts in response. After scanning, Pixie asks if he wants a puke bag or container to get rid of him vomit. D'Arby replies that there's no need—it'll be staying in. Pixie replies that that's good because she hasn't been stocked by the crew with puke bags yet.

D'Arby asks a bit about that, and Pixie explains that this ship was built with a specific purpose: to bring people to space for the first time. It used to transport fey from the Feywild to the Material Plane, acclimating and assimilating them to the world in the stars. While speaking, she started discussing the ship in the first person ("I"), then correcting herself. She apologizes for her weird language, since she's been trying to put her mind back together by assimilating some of the ship—eating ITS mind, so to speak. It's been inelegant, but it gets the job done, she says.

D'Arby says it's weird that she's been eating the ship. Pixie asks if she can ask a question. He notes that he doesn't think she can stop him. She says that's essentially true, unless he was to say no. She asks if he's truly never traveled before. D'Arby says he doesn't remember. The last few times he flew, he's been unconscious/in a cargo hold. She asks if he wants her to put him to sleep. He starts saying no, but she interrupts him and says she has to go—she returns to the main console to start explaining the information of our descent.

D'Arby fixes up his hair, drinks water, makes himself presentable so as to not show that he's had a TERRIBLE time, then joins the crew.

Pixie initiates planetary approach scan. Third from the sun. Historically, it's been shaped by theological forces, especially artifacts called Dragonlances. Divine weapons granted to tallfolk for combat against draconic entities. They were decisive in early conflicts—namely, the Third Dragon War. When a priest of the tallfolk tried to elevate his people, there was a cataclysm when the gods cursed him, and left the planet. This had terraforming effects. Currently in a conflict called the War of the Lance. The dragon queen(?) fights a war to prove her people's supremacy, and the tallfolk fight back. Both sides fight with primitive technological capabilities.

As she says this, we hear the sound of a photon cannon.

LET'S JAM!

The DM/Pixie introduces us to our ship's stat block and its mechanics. We have ballistae that do 3d10 radiant. Different parts of the ship have different HPs and ACs. Bully Points are temp HP. Items have Damage Thresholds (they take no damage unless the damage exceeds the threshold). We all have special actions we can take based on our ship roles.

Claire's actions (it's me writing this, so I'm gonna copy my features :P) are:

- Bully Points: recharge any component's Bully Points as an action
- Brace for Impact: grant resistance to non-magical physical damage to one side of the ship
- Fix It Up: repair disabled components with a DC 15 Intelligence check.
- Work the Sails: modify the ship's speed/maneuverability.

We roll initiative, as does an Adult Red Dragon and Adult Green Dragon. (Correction: _several_ adult green dragons)

> \[!Claire Note]
> this planet SUX >:(

Cheese plays [[Otto]] for a sec, and we discuss what to do/get involved. Otto would get involved (we assume), so we plan our turn.

[[Claire]] asks Pixie if chromatic dragons are always evil on this world. Pixie asks "what _is_ evil, really?", then concedes that yes—Krynn is, essentially, the origin of chromatic dragons as we know them.

[[Squall]] and D'Arby discuss switching around to maximize their attacks out of the starboard cannon. Our class features CAN apply to these guns, also.

(We figured out a rule: Captain always goes first).

Otto takes charge and declares a Fire At-Will. D'Arby and Squall fires. Squall misses a shot, then crits for 43 damage. A radiant blast PULSES out of our cannons, and the dragon looks at us in fury. A large howdah sits on the back of the red dragon, inside which sit many draconic humanoids. D'Arby blasts out a volley of 3 shots. Two land.

> \[!Squall Note]
> This is what I was made for. Literally.

The Bomb Squad takes its turn. Green dragons fly in, with bombs strapped to their chest. In 3 turns, they will bomb the city.

Red Ruin takes her turn. She dashes to fly straight toward us, preparing to do her breath attack.

The lightning cannons blast at a dragon ship. The tower to our west wave a red flag toward us.

In 2 turns, the white ship will blast the lightning cannons(?).

Pixie identifies a blimp/airship as a human command/communications relay. Otto hails them and asks if they can identify much about the dragons for us. The humans reply saying they're VERY aware of the dragons, and asks who we are. Otto identifies himself as Captain Octavian van Lux, and says we're servicepeople, and asks if we can provide support.

The human identifies themself as General Becklin. They discuss their defenses. The west tower is a defense matrix.

Otto tells them to get any information they can gather about Red Ruin for us, and says we'll try to clear the skies for them.

Otto tells the crew he thinks we've got the big one's attention, and suggests we focus the green dragon bombers. We send Claire flying out to try to fix the defense matrix, with Otto commanding [[Vayb]] to move the ship in range of the tower.

Claire casts Fly, then arrives on the tower, which is filled with thunderous synchronized wingbeats. Drakes burst up through the lower levels of the tower, furiously growling, ready for an attack. A crystal at the center is marred with claw marks, damaged by the drakes. Otto gets ready to hop on, next turn.

Squall fires the ballistae at the green dragons, and lands 1 shot. D'Arby misses his shot. The green dragon we shot is PISSED. It flies in and attacks our hull. It deals 15 damage, leaving it at 64 hit points. One of the others tries to take out the ballista. It hits the Starboard ballista, leaving it at 29 hit points left.

Red Ruin, pissed as she is, flies to the communications blimp instead. She's JUST out of reach of her breath weapon, so she had to dash.

On his turn, Otto commands a Fire At Will to have D'Arby shoot the already-injured green dragon. Claire gets on comms and SHRIEKS about the drakes, so Otto commands Pixie to repeat that the captain's orders are to Fire At Will until the ship has made some distance. Then he leaps off of the ship to defend Claire. He points his staff at one of the drakes and uses a secretive feature to try to get their attention.

A drake rushes him, and crit fails. A second attacks Claire and eats shit. A third attacks Claire and bites her for 9 damage. A fourth attacks Otto and misses.

Then we realize the players' turns weren't over. :P

Vayb moves the ship away from the enemy green dragons. D'Arby gets his permission to Fire At Will and shoots. He then pulls the pinball lever and locks in, using an Action Surge. 15 misses but 16 HITS. He deals 17 and 22 damage.

Squall starts their own volley. Misses both shots and we have an OOC discussion about the math of Sharpshooter when the shot does 3d10 radiant. Cheese starts Desmos calculations.

Claire succeeds on her carpenter's tools check to repair the defense matrix, then burns her last Astral Step to teleport and hover out of melee.

Moving to the enemies, the green dragons REALLY want to attack, but they have to fly back to get their bombs in position. Red Ruin BLASTS the hot air balloon with her fire breath. General Becklin screams out of the comms, and the ship takes 68 damage.

Someone else, [[Darrett]], takes over the comms nervously. Otto yells at himself to pull himself together. Darrett asks where to target. Otto tells him to focus fire on the bombers. Darrett apologizes for being so nervous, but squires aren't supposed to be on comms, but everyone else ran. Claire yells at him to stop talking and just SHOOT.  The lightning cannons fire on the green dragons.

Back to our turn, Otto mocks the drakes for taking on the lady because they're afraid of a "real challenge" and casts Compelled Duel. The drake takes offense and defends himself saying that that's sexist—the drake specifies that he's fiscally conservative, but he's socially liberal.

Otto commands Claire to hit the button on the dragon defense matrix. Becklin tells us to cover our ears. There's an EXPLOSION, and a noise pulses out from the tower. It rings through our ears, but it does far more damage to the dragons. The dragons roar in fury, their ears ring, and they shake. Two of the bomber dragons fall and SLAM into the ground into the field beside the town. The bombs strapped to their chest EXPLODE and take the dragons out of commission. The drakes are killed instantly too.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i did something!!!!

General Becklin shouts out some encouragement. Darrett shouts out that we're "fucking awesome". Becklin yells at him, saying that knights don't curse. Darrett apologizes and timidly corrects himself that we're "flipping awesome." Otto just mutters that they're making anyone a knight these days.

The ship is close enough for Otto to leap back and Claire to fly back to the Ace Nebula. Claire points at Vayb and shouts "LOOK AT THAT! I was USEFUL!"

Otto declares a Fire at Will for Squall, who shoots the last bomber out of the air. They take over the cannon from D'Arby. They roll over their chair, redo their calculations, remove their sniper matrix by telling Pixie to turn off the zoom, then BLAST the bomber out of the air. They get a direct hit on the bombs and the dragon EXPLODES.

Vayb repositions the ship and D'Arby takes a shot at the dragon ship. Crits for 32 damage! Second shot hits for 11 more.

That brings it to Red Ruin's turn. The DM asks if it's time to do this. Becklin says "wait, hold on! I think they might be—" And comm line 1 cuts out completely. From the gunners' vantage, they can see the Red Ruin (the rider...?) take out a wand, and she hits the dragon with it. The dragon, and everything on it, disappears.

"Hey, Squall, wasn't there a big ol' dragon there?" says D'Arby.

Darrett tells the lightning cannons to fire as the ship's breath attack blasts out. The cannons and dragon breath clashes, in a Dragon Ball-esque laser fight. The lightning cannon starts losing. Darrett says he can't hold it off alone.

Vayb grins and we ram into the dragon ship. The train gives off a CHOO CHOO! whistle. Claire flies out and hops into one of the Lightning Cannons blasting ANOTHER shot at it. The gunners prep the Forward Cannonade.

Red Ruin is eerily quiet.

The stalemate between the ship and lightning cannons is maintained.

Back to the Ace Nebula, Squall fires the forward cannonade. 49 bludgeoning damage BLASTS out of the hammerhead! They then swap to one of the ballistae and use their second Attack to take another shot. 25 radiant! D'Arby shoots another blast. 24 radiant! The hull cracks open, exposing the dragons within.

The lightning blasts make their way in, and the ship careens into the ground.

Claire flies back onto the ship and resets our hull's Bully Points.

Otto says we should land the ship and figure out what's going on. Claire points out that there's a dragon, and Red Ruin MANIFESTS back into our realm right next to us. The draconians start boarding the Ace Nebula.

On the lightning cannon's turn, Darrett asks what to do. Claire yells at him to just Ready a shot for when there's an opening. D'Arby tells Squall to get shooting; he'll buy them some time. Otto rushes in to fight the draconians too. Otto Misty Steps up to D'Arby. He welcomes the draconians to the Ocean Court Express, but tells them that they'll have to depart at the next stop.

Red Ruin tells us that if we give up, she'll have the dragon let go of us. Otto asks D'Arby, "What is our procedure for surrender, D'Arby?" D'Arby replies, "We're not much for losin', cap'n." Red Ruin growls out a "Very well..." and they set up to fight.

Claire braces the ship for impact, and Squall fires the ballista. The red dragon whales on our ship, but much of its damage is prevented by Claire's bracing.

Red Ruin takes a necrotic ray shot at Otto, and misses. He tells her that if she wants to be threatening, she really ought to be more—— and then she crits him for 23 necrotic damage.

Otto realizes that there's only open space behind Red Ruin. He breaks from the Draconian foot soldier and provokes an AoO, which ALSO crits. It does 9 points of damage, which ALMOST takes him out, but he's still standing. Otto casts Thunderous Smite. He deals 10 bludgeoning, 11 divine smite radiant, and more thunder. Red Ruin FAILS her save. There's a thunderous BOOM and Red Ruin is thrown back, off the ship and careens into the air as she screams and tumbles through the air into the ground!

She splatters across the ground, dying on impact.

D'Arby looks at the foot soldier, who swears revenge for killing his love—he means. Well, she didn't KNOW that but it was destined to be! D'Arby threatens, asking if he wants to do that from the brig, or just die here... The draconian takes offense, proclaiming draconians to be a proud race of CR... well, he won't tell us that, but!

D'Arby flicks his match in the draconian's face, and while they flinch, he slashes once, cutting the draconian in two. Claire flies in to try to reach Squall and arrives to find D'Arby killing the draconian.

The red dragon pops its head in and breath weapons the party. D'Arby is unconscious. Otto is unconscious. Claire dies outright.

> \[!Claire Note]
> (if she comes back) yeah this planet sucks so much. literally so so much.

Squall and Vayb are the only PCs still living.

Darrett fires his readied shot and FUCKING CRITS. Damn near finishes it off. Squall's homunculus nabs one of Claire's potions of healing to bring Otto back up. Otto Lay on Handses D'Arby to heal him. Squall shoots the dragon for 24 damage and gets one more HDYWTDT.

Squall has no idea that anyone is dying, really, because they're in the zone. They take the shot, which pierces through the dragon. They wave to Darrett and ask, "how is everybody—Oh shit."

The party gathers around Claire. A corpse. She's dead.

Otto contemplates the fallen wizard, whom he respects, but barely knows. Squall grieves, silently.
