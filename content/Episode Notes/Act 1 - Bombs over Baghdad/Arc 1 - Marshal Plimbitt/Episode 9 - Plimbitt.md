Episode 9 - His Most Distinguished Field Marshal Commander Brigadier [[Marshal Plimbitt|General Plimbitt]] III, Supreme Ordinance Officer of the Astral Fleet, Chair of the Giff Council

So... You wanna be [[Aces]]. Well, second thing you need to know: No bounty goes without a fight.

DM gives us a cool, suave monologue about how these guys always find somewhere to hide out, and summarizes our hunt of this big Giff bastard.

We resume in the Canyon Wastes, under the burning heat. The Canyons glow in an unnatural amber at dusk. Nothing lives here but things that shouldn't. Each of us are prepared as we can be.

Sprawled across the ground, at our meeting place is a factory—a mechanical foundry. The Ocean Court Express hovers above the meeting point. [[PIXIE|Pixie]] informs us all: "We're here, best for everyone to regroup." [[Vayb]] pulls off his helmet, hands over autopilot to Pixie. According to her sensors, Plimbitt is already inside.

Nowhere to run. This is when Aces shine.

> \[!Claire Note]
> let's do this. (๑•̀ ᴗ•́)૭✧

[[Otto]] gives us a recap of our plan. He scans the foundry for other entrances. There are no windows, just one large entrance—and perhaps the chimneys. There seem to be people, five or six individuals, waiting outside in some sort of line. He's able to recognize some of them—the guards from Castle Kalaman, from the warehouse—with their truth and lies gimmick. Then Otto notices why: a sign (as though anyone would chance upon it out here) that reads "mass layoffs, now hiring."

Otto double checks our routes in. Pixie chimes in and says she's linked our comms on the way in, and asks if we want to keep our prisoners in the ship (for now, we do). We set the sneakier PCs (plus [[Darrett]]) on the roof, and Otto + Vayb approach the door of the foundry on foot.

Otto approaches with weapons holstered, hands where they're visible, and a smile on his face. Vayb walks up with his look severe, eyes on the little group of guards in line, like Otto's security detail.

The guards see them and say "Hey, you guys are the guys who got us fired!" Otto confirms that they're not actively employed as guards. Vayb asks what their pay was. They say they were paid 1 silver an hour. Otto tells them that he has an important meeting here, so if they watch the door for us, he'll pay them for an hour of work each. He confirms that Plimbitt is inside—they haven't been passed the front desk, but they know he fired all of his staff. Apparently everyone was fired aside from his secretary. Otto replies that that is excellent.

They enter, and tell the gnome at the desk that we're representing Becklinn for her scheduled meeting. Vayb asks her if we need to call Becklinn up or if we can stop wasting time—since time is money. She concedes and says she'll ping Plimbitt that we're here. Vayb takes a moment to flirt with the receptionist (did he do this for a purpose or just for fun? I missed some context), and Otto gives us a call over the comms.

Meanwhile, on the roof, [[Claire]] has started intro-ing a vlog, inviting [[D'Arby]] and [[Squall]] in to ask if they're ready for their first bounty. Squall corrects that this is our second, but Claire and D'Arby say Starfist _barely_ counts.

We get our message from Otto, Claire looks to the camera and says, "Captain's calling." We set off. D'Arby watches the pattern of the steam vents, and then uses his ring of jumping to hop right through, taking a small amount of damage on the fall—though, due to Otto's distraction, Plimbitt doesn't hear him enter. Claire spins up, flying, then _drops_ straight through the vent her flight speed catches her before she takes damage. Squall drops with Vayb's hoverboard.

Otto and Vayb approach Plimbitt, getting a view of the HUGE dragon fount of molten metal. A huge hippo bomb hangs over the factory. On conveyor belts, metal wasps are brought out and begin to hum to life. Floating in a flying orb vehicle—like Dr. Eggman's ship—Plimbitt studies the production of his factory.

Otto strolls right in and waves. "Hail, sir!" Getting Plimbitt's attention. He jumbles in place a little, the flies the vehicle over to Otto, and one of us (whoever had the highest Passive Perception, not me) notices that the shield on his orb warps a little as Plimbitt flies over a steam vent.

Plimbitt asks who we are and what we need. Otto introduces us as the crew Ocean Court Express, bounty hunters. Plimbitt seems _shocked_, and backs up. Otto waves dismissively, and says "yeah, yeah, it's a whole thing." He explains that following Plimbitt was pretty easy, and we're supposed to take him in, but Otto saw his work during our mission and says he has a business idea for Plimbitt. Plimbitt raises a large white eyebrow, and tells him to go on.

Otto explains that all men are loyal to something. To Otto, he just wants to get paid. Plimbitt asks him to pause a moment and he chews out his secretary for letting an Ace in here—if she doesn't have a list of 10 people she prevented from entering here by the end of the day, or he'll consider that her resignation. Then he turns his attention back.

Vayb interrupts and says he's a little hard of hearing. He lights a cigarette, then asks if Plimbitt could come closer or turn off the vents. Plimbitt says he understands, the vents are impossible to turn off or it'll be a danger to everyone inside—so he lowers his vehicle and gets closer.

Otto continues, explaining that if Plimbitt comes in peacefully, he'll spring Plimbitt and split the money together. Vayb says we can fake his death, and then he won't have to worry about Aces come after him. Otto continues that he knows this is counterintuitive to his hope of staying free—but that there are two dozen other groups of Aces on their way (Vayb chimes in that he thought it was three dozen).

Plimbitt says to Otto that he thinks that's an interesting offer, and appreciates it, but ever since he was a child, all he has ever wanted was to cause as much destruction and conflict and explosion to lower life forms as he could. He LOVES what he does, and he will keep do it, despite the fact that _everyone_ wishes to stop him: the Elvish Navy, the Giff, the foolish workers, and... us. So, we should "leave." Otto shrugs, says, "Suit yourself."

Squall has been inching closer, Claire has been setting up a good camera shot (Vayb has been photobombing) and a good line of sight with Lightning Bolt. Otto speaks on the comms: "Let's jam."

And we jam!
Round 1 highlights:

- Otto starts, tells Plimbitt he might be one of the "dumbest things I've ever had the displeasure of witnessing," and casts his Hexblade's Curse on Plimbitt. He swings with his new magical quarterstaff and _crits_ with a Smite for a huge amount of damage.
- Vayb casts a _hold person_ on Plimbitt.
- Claire flies over, scrawls out a new spell circle and blasts Plimbitt and a wasp out of the air with her brand-new _lightning bolt_.
- Squall casts _heat metal_ on Plimbitt.
- D'Arby leaps down from the foundry and gets close.

> \[!Claire Note]
> BLAM! it _worked_!!!!

Round 2 and onward highlights:

- Vayb fireballs all the wasps and Otto, but Plimbitt's shield protects him from damage.
- Claire flies up to the dragon fount and jacks into the Weave.
- Squall whiffs both hits, but gets 3 damage in as a bonus action for free with _heat metal_.
- D'Arby uses his maneuvers to help the fireballed Otto move out of melee—but Otto politely declines. D'Arby's sword doesn't do much good, so he puts his sword away and tries to grapple Plimbitt.
- Plimbitt moves to run away, prompting attacks of opportunity from Otto and Squall—Squall's elemental damage does nothing.
- Plimbitt pulls out a grenade, says "taste this!" and throws a bomb at Squall and Otto. He shoots at Squall with his musket, but the heated vehicle prevents him from hitting.
- Otto looks at Plimbitt, says "aren't you forgetting about someone?" and uses Lay on Hands to heal himself up. Plimbitt says "What? That's bullshit!" He calls to an employee to see how he can do that, then realizes he fired that employee earlier.
- Vayb paralyzes Plimbitt again, then studies the shield he set up—he notices one design flaw. It could be shorted with water. He then uses his Telekinetic Shove to push Plimbitt and his vehicle above the steam vents, destroying the force field.
- Claire hacks into the ballistae around the foundry—they turn from a red to a pink glow and shoot out a critical shot (due to the advantage from the paralysis <3) at Plimbitt.

Something rings in Claire's ears, a ring that pulses, and becomes a static. It then changes, coalesces, and music echoes through the Threadspace. A skull icon appears in the digital space—a blue pixelated skull. Claire gets some strange sixth sense—someone else has entered the weave here. Another hacker.

The person speaks and says, "what's going on here?" Claire replies, "who the heck are you? I'm in the middle of something here." The person says, "Yes, I can see that," back to Claire. "You're interfering with my work." They begin to move toward the bomb node.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i'm worried about this. who is this person? and confused actually. i can understand war profiteering but why would they help a moron like plimbitt??

- D'Arby gets swarmed by wasps.
- Plimbitt stays paralyzed. Otto swings in with his staff, hanging off the hoverboard, and hits Plimbitt—he says "this is the first step in correcting your wrongs," and cracks Plimbitt across the face. Knocking him unconscious.

Otto has fought many people in his time. You can learn a lot about someone's strength or character in the way they take a hit. Plimbitt has avoided us, but past his weaponry, his foundry, his crimes... there is a fragile man behind it all. Otto sees only a look of fear and of weakness on the giff's face, and hears a scream as he knocks Plimbitt out.

- Otto rides the falling flying machine down to the ground, _misty steps_ over to D'Arby, appearing back to back, and helps D'Arby with the swarm of metal wasps.
- Claire sets up to defend the bomb node.

Inspecting the opposing hacker, Claire sees their hacker callsign: [[L1CH]]. She tells everyone "Bomb!! Everyone, get out now!!" L1CH says, "listen, I don't want to eviscerate you, or the hippo. Leave the hippo, and your crew walks out of here just fine." Claire tells them to back off, because we worked fair and square on this. L1CH sighs and says, "Yeah, that's a typical Ace response." They advance, beating Claire in the opposed check and dealing 14 psychic damage.

> \[!Claire Note]
> okay, SCREW YOU, l1ch. i don't want to get burned up again T\_T

Claire says "this person is good, grab me and let's get out of here!" Squall picks her up and they hoverboard out of the steam vents. Otto, D'Arby, and Vayb grab Plimbitt and run out on foot. The wasps attack Otto, but he _shields_ them effortlessly and says "Sorry, D'Arby's not the one you're dealing with now."

Back in Threadspace, Claire pushes back at L1CH and says, "how _dare_ you mess with us." She slams her digital avatar back into L1CH and succeeds at pushing them back. They pause and go, "Huh. Respect."

The rest of the group finishes their exit. D'Arby says, "Can you help with this guy? Also _we need to go_." To the guards. One replies, "we'd love to help!" Another replies, "we don't want to help." They help us.

On the way out, Claire—as much as she can in digital space—squints at L1CH. She claims the bomb node. L1CH sighs and says, "please don't." Claire activates it behind us.

> \[!Claire Note]
> that's a weird thing to say. was l1ch _bluffing_?

She returns to consciousness and the factory _explodes_ behind us, crumbling and destroying all of Plimbitt's work. The camera pulls back, revealing the monitor playing _Wildcard_ at the Laughing Beholder. Jack regales the audience with our story—congratulates the new blood on their good work, and tells us to "come get your money, you bastards!" Then he realizes we don't have a group name—so he rings us directly.

We step outside to take the call, and confirm our group's name in an official capacity. Vayb glances out and upward, gazing at the stars above the [[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]] and replies—yeah, we're the Ace Nebula. Jack relays that name to the audience and says he expects, and the audience can expect, great things from us in the future. Jack starts doing the outro, which Claire interrupts to plug her vlog channel—clairescorner. Jack asks if she's really plugging herself, and shrugs, telling the audience to check out clairesspot. She corrects him and says it's alliteration, how hard can it be?

Then, we went back to a couple scenes that would have happened before we left, even if the TV episode would have already ended.

Otto, for his part, returned to one of the people he met on his side quest—he tells her what we learned, what he believes about the gods and the hope of their return. She gives him a speech about how her husband always hoped there was more. Believed the gods didn't just abandon them, that maybe... they were watching. Otto tells her that maybe he was right. Maybe she can spread the belief herself. She says she hasn't seen much good, much power, come from faith. Then she asks if he has. Otto replies that we're people. Faith gives us all our power. She asks if she'll see him again. He replies that he isn't sure, but as long as he left things better than he found them... She replies that, alright. She'll have faith. Have faith in the gods. Faith in that she'll see them again.

Otto departs, returns to the ship, and punches Vayb for his poor aim with his fireball.

The camera lingers on Otto's friend, who stops a group of ruffians from roughhousing another NPC—she gets upset, smacks the ruffian on the head, and tells him to step away. During the altercation, a bit of a glow appears. Just a bit of divine magic. Unlocked on Krynn for the first time in... years. Everyone stares, agape.

The camera pans over to one strange individual. The old man who tried to sell Claire strange food (Fizban, though none of us know that)—looks on. He smiles.

And that's where we leave the episode for tonight.
