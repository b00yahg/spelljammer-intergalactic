"This is your Captain speaking: Pajama Party at 20:00"
-O

Booyahg delivers a monologue as our ship takes off, for the very first time.

[[Vayb]] is on the helm. His mind connects with the ship. He is one with the Ocean Court Express, it is an extension of his body, and he tilts it up and it SHOOTS into the sky. [[The Rock of Bral]] grows smaller and smaller behind us. We pierce through the sky, and then, suddenly, everything... connects (sick music change). Everything stabilizes and we find ourselves within a rainbow. Our train rocks back and forth like the waves of an ocean. We sail gently across the [[The Phlogiston|Phlogiston]].

> \[!Squall Note]
> Vayb has attuned to the ship. He has previously driven a bike of some sort. The feeling of mind attached to another body is curious. I hope it is not too disconcerting for him. I will take over for him if I must. My armor is not too different.

[[PIXIE]] says "Welcome to the Flow, guys!" and informs us that we should be able to use our comms to speak to each other. We repeat that we can hear her, loud and clear. Vayb asks Pixie to set up some tunes. Pixie says she'll find one. Then, she says we'll set sail for [[Krynnspace]]. She then corrects herself, noting that she needs the captain to say the phrase, so [[Otto]] declares that we'll set sail for Krynnspace. He also asks her to do us a lil status report.

[[Claire]] is taking aesthetic videos of the takeoff and space travel for her vlog.

Otto has put on his blue captain's coat, and starts a quick discussion about where, specifically, we're trying to route to in Krynnspace. We discuss going to a populated area. Pixie says she's routing us to Kalaman.

[[Squall]], Claire, and Otto discuss where/how he might hide—Claire points out that he is a giant hippo man. When discussing, Squall refused to pronounce giff and just spelled it out.

We take a moment to discuss whether or not Vayb can speak/is aware of his surroundings while piloting. Technically he couldn't, per 2e rules, but Booyahg thinks that's stupid so Vayb can telepathically project his voice over the speakers.

Pixie brings up the topic of whether or not we will be following the Prime Directive. It's illegal to inform groundlings of the greater space community. It's vague enough that it's not like we'd be prosecuted for one person knowing, but mass knowledge of space society historically causes problems in ground communities. Otto asks Pixie if she can find us a distant population center to claim to be from.

We then discuss how to disguise ourselves, and our illusory capacities. Claire has a disguise kit—and a few of the party have illusory capacities. Except Squall. Claire starts trying to figure out what she can do for Squall, and sets them up with a cloak and mask. She also designs a cute medieval fit for herself and films a Get Ready With Me vlog about that—and reluctantly dyes her hair blonde to pass more naturally.

> \[!Squall Note]
> Claire has given me a mask. I appreciate it. This is an odd moment.

> \[!Otto Note]
> Claire's espionage gear has many practical uses, but are any causes more just than looking good? She pulls off blonde fabulously, almost as well as I do.

Pixie presents information about the other location we could be from. Otto also approaches Squall about splitting our reward for the Starfist bounty.

We discuss whether or not Pixie should be paid her own cut. When Claire says she isn't part of the bounty hunting team. Vayb and her argue about this. He stops the ship and asks if she could fly the ship without Pixie. She insists that she can, so Vayb stops the ship and dares her to do it. She says she'll fly back to the Rock when we're done. Vayb says that wasn't quite his point, but Pixie does sort of teach us that the ship could fly _ineffectively_ without an AI like her. So Vayb sort of concedes the logic of the point.

We continue debating Pixie's cut of the money. Eventually, we sort of agree on setting aside a ship fund in Pixie's name.

> \[!Squall Note]
> PIXIE has requested a portion of the funds. She's pursuing her own directive now. I am partly proud of her, and partly afraid of her immediate choice to invest in crypto.
> Claire and Otto seem to oppose the addition of PIXIE's consciousness. I do not know why.

> \[!Otto Note]
> It seems that there are some growing pains in teamwork among this group. Vayb and Claire are the type to make sweeping declarations without consulting others. A ship faces enough obstacles outside without tension within. While I can respect a fiery spirit, compromises and communication are vital.

# The Sargasso Field

Pixie interrupts that we're coming up on a Sargasso field—she asks if we want a lore dump. We agree, and she explains that they're areas of antimagic around the Phlogiston, undetectable by sight. We can use gravitational propulsion rather than magic to fly, however. There are stories of treasure found in the Sargasso, given the amount of ships that crash in them. This particular one has four large gravitational objects. Pixie asks if we should pilot into it or around it.

> \[!Claire Note]
> so... turns out im. literally useless in sargasso. this is... a problem.

> \[!Vayb Note]
> Skill issue.

We bicker a bit while planning, and Otto whistles to interrupt us—he says that, no matter which course we take, we should have a specific plan in mind and work as a team, given the danger. Vayb has already decided he wants to go in, and Otto is fine by that but explains that it's dangerous and we should work together. Additionally, if there are dangers here, he thinks we need to remove them to protect Krynn.

> \[!Squall Note]
> I am not used to taking orders from an equal. I will learn.

> \[!Otto Note]
> The presence of a Sargasso field here was quite disconcerting. There seem to be gaps in the understanding of the implications. Badddd things hang out in these spaces, creatures that like to pry your mind open. Keeping a cool head is VITAL.

# The Octopus Spelljammer

A gravitational tide catches the ship as we fly into the pocket of Sargasso. We see a silhouette in the sun(?) and Pixie voices a warning: "Warning. Multiple Leviathan-class creatures detected." We fly deeper into the void, and massive iridescent scales the size of people float through the air. There is a large octopus-like structure.

Otto asks Claire. She recognizes the thing to be a human-made spelljammer. Due to the antimagic, we can't hail it.

^\[We discuss, ooc, a bit about how the movement in this space works. We do have _minor_ amounts of analogue propulsion that we can use to jump between orbit around each of the four suns. Then the discussion turns to ooc about whether or not Vayb's psionics are counted as psionics. The verdict is that: it should be treated separately (so he can cast here) but my (Claire's) opinion is that there should also be situations in the future where this special treatment should bite him in the ass.]

We approach the octopus spelljammer, and there seem to be humans on board that are beckoning us. Notably, each of them are wearing dragon masks. All of these are silver. Claire recognizes the masks from her terminal online-ness—a community called the [[The People's Order of Thought (POTs)|Pragmatic Order of Thought]]. Shortened to the POT. They're anti-slavers who fight for truth and justice. They always sign off their post with "We will remember. You will remember us. POTs."

Vayb seems to have some sort of conflicted feeling about this. Squall asks, but he denies that he's expressing anything particular.

When we dock, a woman greets us—Captain Nara—and tells us that we're safe and protected here. Otto greets her and tells her who we are, then asks what they're doing here. Nara explains that they're on a protective mission. There are solar dragons who live here, and they are investigating the Sargasso and trying to nullify it so that the dragons have their homes again.

Otto asks if she thought it was unnatural. She says that, no, it did occur naturally. And it is spreading, which could be natural too. But they are an endangered species, and they want to try to reverse the Sargasso.

We return to our ship to discuss this in private. Claire and Squall are confused about the plan to reverse Sargasso. There's no precedent suggesting that to be possible. Otto wants to help, since he thinks the spread is strange and potentially dangerous.

Otto returns and asks questions. Eventually we agree to do what we can to help.

Claire asks Nara if she can borrow a crossbow and some bolts (Claire can't do jack nor shit in antimagic). Meanwhile, Vayb has been flexing his ability to use his magic on her. She's quietly seething.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i literally hate vayb so much >:(

> \[!Vayb Note]
> _flicks cigarette_ Love you too, princess.

Vayb just insists that that's something people can do back home. Otto asks where, and Vayb explains that it's some podunk planet called [[Athas]]. Squall crosses their arms and doesn't voice their own conflicted feelings about the conversation.

# The Pufferfish Station

We go to the star with the other POTs station, through a debris field. Artifacts, items, and trinkets float through the space from crashed ships. Another space station here is shaped like a large pufferfish. We discuss going about collecting the treasure, but Otto seems to be more on edge and less carefree, so he insists we do that later.

We approach the second POTs station and dock. The captain here greets us with a similar speech to Captain Nara, but with some key differences: they HATE weaponry here (unlike Nara, who was totally for it) and they want to evacuate the dragons.

Otto explains that the other POTs people believe that they should suppress the Sargasso. The captain here is completely SHOCKED to hear this and says that the POTs mission is clear and he's gonna do such a flame war on the forums when they get back to magic. Vayb uses a telepathic Detect Thoughts to read what's going on in his head. Basically, this group has no leaders and operates on group consensus. He mentions a fight, and this guy seems to have just assumed he won the fight.

They'd both also mentioned something about a conflict between gnomes and dwarves, and when Vayb asks, the captain here goes into some speech about the historical context for the fight (he's pro-dwarf, Zara was pro-gnome), but Vayb only reads the surface thoughts: "Dwarves good, gnomes bad."

Vayb passes back to the party. Otto asks if they have a way to evacuate the dragons. The captain replies that, yes, they theoretically could, but the dragons refuse to respond. They seem to be engaged in a conflict of their own.

Otto also takes the chance to ask more about the Sargasso—the captain says there has been reports of it before. The research suggests there was a negative correlation between size and speed of spread. The Weird, the largest patch of Sargasso ever, has grown only 5 inches in 2 decades.

> \[!Otto Note]
> When it comes to authority groups, I don't think anyone is doing a bad of a job as POTS. And I've seen town guards get fooled by four gnomes hiding contraband in a fake cow.

# The Debris Field

We return to our ship to discuss. Otto suggests we grab some treasure and then go to try to talk to the dragons. We navigate to the debris field. A necklace, a staff with a crystal heart, a pair of wings. Paper floating about. Crates, wood, debris. Additionally, in a bizarre position, leaning on the crates, are a pair of solar dragons. They wave their hands in strange patterns, then speak in Common. "Fusion of the wind... GO!"

We get Vayb to use his telekinetic _mage hand_ to pull the treasure toward us. He acquires: a Necklace of Prayer Beads, Staff of Healing, and Wings of Flying. When Claire inspects them with Vayb's help, we do identify that these items have been deeply weakened by the Sargasso. The Beads have 3 uses left, the Staff has only 3 charges, and the Wings only give 20 feet. We'd need to refurbish them to fix them.

The paper floating about are drawings. Drawings of various individuals in martial arts fighting positions. They're poorly drawn. Stick figures.

# Meeting the Wyrmlings

Claire loudly yells out hey to the dragons. They hide and ask who we are. They identify us as fleshfolk (Squall agrees with this despite its untruth). They ask if we believe in the power of friendship. We agree, tentatively.

They seem to trust us a _bit_ about this. We try to get information about this. Vayb insists that we're good because we're a ragtag team who came together through the power of friendship.

They exclaim that we're "just like Uzmaki". "They live the lives of the sacred text!!" They conjecture that we might know the techniques. They ask if we know Uzumaki. Vayb says we haven't had the pleasure. They let us know where their leaders are—the elders are staying in the dragonfly by the white dwarf. They're guarding the relic. But their enemies have _stolen_ half of it.

Claire inspects them, and despite their size (they're nearly as big as the ship), she realizes they're Solar Dragon Wyrmlings—no more than four years old.

> \[!Claire Note]
> oh my gosh. they're babies this is actually so cuteeeeeee

Squall reflects on the names they used, and recognizes it from a retro anime called Uzumaki and the Flurry of Blows.

# The White Dwarf

We approach the white dwarf, around a crashed dragonfly spelljammer. A Young Solar Dragon lounges on a throne beside a collection of VHS tapes. The eldest tells us to approach and speak. Otto approaches and bows his head. He greets the elder one and introduces us and says that we've come to persuade them to evacuate due to the spreading of the Sargasso. The dragon asks why we would ask them to leave their home just while "they're waiting for Mother to return".

Otto explains that the Sargasso will destroy their home, then asks about their mother.

When the eldest was born 10 years ago, their mother left to get snackies, leaving them only with the holy text and their wits. Since then, they've studied the holy texts and learned by studying the martial arts of the holy text.

Squall asks if they're aware that their home is infested with Sargasso. The dragon doesn't know what that is. Claire demonstrates by producing the Staff of Healing topped with a heart. She explains that it's been sapping the life and power from the staff—it's been hurting the staff, and will probably hurt them.

Otto tries to double down on persuasion by pointing that good things don't survive in this space.

The dragon realizes that this situation is just like episode 43 when Uzumaki and Rina got their powers sapped. But the dragon explains that they couldn't leave now while they're at war with a group of heretics. It explains that part of the family has split, rallied around the youngest of them, the arrogant baby brother (NAME). It began on the 157th watch of episode 42, when Uzumaki and Rina danced.

Uzumaki and his rival Shadowsuke united briefly, and... the youngest and his faction believe that Uzumaki and Shadowsuke are meant to fall in love and end up together—but this one knows it must be Uzumaki and Rina.

# The Power of Friendship

Vayb began a speech about the power of friendship and understanding, which I need to transcribe later. But the speech fell on deaf ears. Because, although he understood... "they're the ones who started it!!"

Otto and Squall both try to teach the lesson of forgiveness by explaining with the example of the story. The dragon doubts the totality of his knowledge, and doubts himself. Otto and Squall give a speech about learning and the beauty of understanding. We all end up contributing to the speech and triple the group check's DC.

The dragon gets emotional. He gets confused and unsure. Then afraid. Then he accepts it. The feeling of not truly understanding the world is terrifying, but when overcome there is peace and joy. The dragon says that he will go talk to his brother, and try to respect his brother's... subtext. And besides... this will require MORE VIEWINGS—a prospect that excites the other dragons. The young dragon asks us where the location we suggested they relocate too is—we explain where it is, but also that there is a whole wide galaxy out there. They light their breath into space to signify the end of the feud, and the dragons come together.

> \[!Claire Note]
> no one on the web is gonna believe this :/

> \[!Otto Note]
> I'm not exactly sure what we just experienced but I love it! If these dragons reconciled with each other, maybe there's hope for our little group.

# Return to the Flow

The young dragon offers to fly us out of the Sargasso and he throws us out into the galaxy. Claire frantically tries to turn her phone on when we get out to video this.

Pixie turns back on and asks how our exploration went. Vayb asks if she knows what anime is. Her database has hundreds! She has thousands of TV and movies, though, so she has much less anime.

We tell her that we solved a conflict with the power of friendship. "Oh. That's nice, I guess?" Pixie says.

When we get ready to take off back to Krynnspace, Squall says that we should "take this Intergalactic." Pixie asks if this was a reference to something. They explain that Claire said it last time and they liked it.

As the episode ends, Vayb asks Pixie to turn on \[Censored] mode, and everyone shouts "NO! NO! DO NOT!" And the credits roll as we end the session. (Oh wait, no, there's more)
