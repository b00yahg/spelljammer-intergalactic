## Black's Jack Casino

Our group arrives at _Black's Jack Casino_, a bright and magical and flashy "temple" of vice. Nobles, retired spelljammer captains, and more intermingle here. In a private alcove there are private games of poker played. Greeting us from behind an extravagant red desk is a **gold dragonborn**—their scales encrusted with fine jewels.

He energetically greets us.

**[[Vayb]]** takes a moment to take in and enjoy the place. He stares at a swaggering retired captain, then nods to the dragonborn, and asks if they run the place—they reply that they're just a humble worker. They greet first timers and run tables.

Moving to business, Vayb mentions that we're here for the spellcycle race. Vayb is vibing with this dragonborn guy. The dragonborn confirms that we're a team and plugs us into his system. He asks us for our names—**[[D'Arby]]** says his name and emphasizes that the pause is needed, like the hyphen in Spider-Man. He asks **[[Claire]]**, who seems shocked that he doesn't recognize her. He does, however, recognize her name, as part of House Ainsworth, and comments that she's a celebrity. **[[Otto]]** gives his info too (Faisal had to be AFK for a sec and we skimmed this part).

The dragonborn explains that the mechanics are much like piloting a spelljammer. He explains that, in the race, pretty much all forms of violence and spectacle are legal.

D'Arby asks what to do if he doesn't know how to drive. In response he's told that he's either best waiting on the sidelines, or, well—there's a first time for everything! The dragonborn gets a goblin attendant to bring us to get ready—we get jumpsuits that can be illusorily changed to be whatever colour we want.

(Retconning, Otto comments about the fact that he's glad to be in better company, with the fancy dragonborn. He puts his full name, Octavius van Luxe, in the system.)

Claire sets her jumpsuit to sky blue with pink highlights. D'Arby sets his to match his normal colour palette. Otto picks white with gold trim. Vayb picks neon purple and blue highlights.

Vayb asks if we're ready, and notes that even with us all participating to stack the odds, he's gonna win. Claire replies, "yeah, right". Otto comments that he's happy for the spectacle either way.

D'Arby asks the group if they can teach him how to drive. Claire, Vayb (and maybe [[Squall]]), just go to get ready, but Otto takes time out of his prep time to get ready. D'Arby asks for a bike with training wheels.

We step out into the arena—scrying eyes fill the stands, creating an almost starry pattern (what a cool mental image, Booyahg). The stands are also full of a raucous crowd that roars in excitement.

Vayb walks out, puts out a cigarette and glances at the audience. Then he hops onto his bike. Otto bows charmingly toward the audience, commenting that he wishes he had a rose to throw to the audience. Claire steals that idea and prestidigitations a rose to throw herself. As she looks at the audience, she notices repeating patterns and ethereality—projected illusions to create an impression of a fuller audience. Claire chooses an illusion to toss her rose that will disappear at.

Otto casts a spell (prestidigitation?) to pop out golden fireworks.

The attendants come out and note that we have three regular spellcycles and one trainer. Then they bring out three Tron bike-looking spellcycles and one toy-looking trainer.

The other riders step out—casually confident and predatory in competitiveness. **The blonde** comments that she's shocked someone wasn't too scared to race against her and **Johnny**. "What are you, some kinda new group or something?" She makes a competitive declaration and asks "Isn't that right, Johnny?" Johnny throws a thumbs up. D'Arby throws one back to him. They nod to each other in understanding and respect. Otto says it'll be fun to have some competition. The blonde replies that she and Johnny are the eight-time champions so it's not really "competition". D'Arby says, "it took you eight times to become the champion?" She gets mad at this.

She gives us a line about "the claws". Claire asks, "did you spend all night coming up with that one?" The blonde shoots some jabs at Claire's clout chasing and mommy and daddy. (I've stopped being able to follow this over text, but I hope the recording will capture the exchange)

Johnny goes to offer a toothpick to D'Arby—the blonde yells at him for befriending the enemy. D'Arby, in turn, offers him a lollipop. Johnny takes an extra one for the blonde, in case she wants one.

Since D'Arby hasn't driven before, we're gonna follow his POV as he puts his helmet on. He puts it on and magic energy SURGES through his nervous system and locks in place. His vision blacks out and is replaced—the spellcycle, the dolphin underneath, almost becomes an extension of his body. He's never driven before, but he's moved his hand plenty of times—the feeling is remarkably similar. Everything clicks into place. The machine isn't _responding_ to his inputs. The machine has become him, and he has become the machine, and suddenly all his senses center around it as an extension of his body.

Vayb looks to D'Arby and gives him a grin.

The announcer starts announcing the main event—the race of a century, no rules-no holds barred. Introducing the "Grand Prowlers of the Night, the seven time champions"—Kitty and Johnny. And some brand new racers here to take on the champions—us!

## Tilly's Truck Stop

We head to _Tilly's Truck Stop_ to go get our new ship. A dwarf mechanic works on a ship. A redheaded woman pays more attention to her communication crystal than us. She boredly greets us, blowing bubblegum.

Once she knows that we're here for the ship, she calls for **Tilly**. Tilly asks her to show us to the ship. She replies that it's just too far. Tilly sighs and comes out to greet us after a moment.

Otto mostly takes point on talking business with Tilly. Tilly asks what we're looking for in a ship—Claire replies that we want one that costs 25k gold. Tilly points out that it's kind of old. Otto replies that he thinks that means it has a story to tell.

Suddenly a knock comes at the door. Claire and Vayb both turn over and cautiously look at the door. D'Arby looks through a peephole and tells us that there is a large man. Claire replies, "again?" D'Arby explains that he's not quite THAT large.

It is a hippo man in a green coat. A giff. D'Arby greets him through the door and lets him in.

He introduces himself with a **long title** (I will copy paste it later). Claire corrects his pronunciation of giff as "jiff". He goes on a speech about how people have his species' name wrong, and as those who aren't aware he forgives us for not knowing, but it is pronounced with a hard G.

He explains that his people keep a registry of information on the _[[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]]_ called "Giffopedia". We interrupt his explanation as he says his full title and discuss the title—which is apparently mandatory for him to say (and not write) for giff by-laws.

Eventually, we relent and register our ship:

Ocean Court Express: Ace Nebula

After he leaves, D'Arby asks if Claire is able to pull up Giffopedia—she tries, but it has an internal firewall. We debate hacking it, but deem it too dangerous to mess up.

This is about where we end the session.
