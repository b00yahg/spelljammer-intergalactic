The episode begins with [[Otto]]—he's just finished a definitely real, intense, emotional, great-writing-by-the-DM side quest, for sure. 100%.

He's sitting back and relaxing in the Ocean Court Express, watching the Space Chiefs game, when at a pivotal moment in the game... [[PIXIE|Pixie]] cuts in, saying there's an incoming transmission. He says he can't "leave those two alone for five minutes". The Discord "plunk!" plays as Otto hops on call.

He asks what's going on. We report what happened and that [[Darrett]] is missing. Otto asks why we care, and we tell him that Darrett might be reporting to his boss, and also that we kinda want to take him to space.

We discuss our plan to get there—[[Claire]] slaps a _fly_ spell on [[Squall]] instead of herself this time. Otto inquires about logistical/tactical concerns. When Otto says we "force our way in", Squall voices reticence to violence—Otto corrects that he never meant violence! Just that he prefers a direct route. We decide that Squall and [[Vayb]] are flying over. Otto decides whether or not to fly the Spelljammer over. The flying duo rush right over there.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i should try to use my magic on someone more useful than me from here on out.

The guards tense up as Vayb and Squall arrive from the air. It's the same two guards as last time. Vayb does a sick move with his hoverboard, points at them, and asks if he has Gregory's name right. Gregory sighs deeply. Vayb apologizes for before, but explains that he's in a rush and he has something to do in the castle. The guards say we have permission to enter.

Vayb meets with the inside castle guards and asks to meet with Becklinn. They ask if we have an appointment, and say that she's in a very important meeting. Vayb doesn't think they've been told much about the meeting—so he asks about Darrett, and learns he hasn't been back to the palace. So he reports that to the group.

Claire sends k1k1 out to search for him, and eventually she pings back somewhere near Castle Kalaman, only having found some shrubbery. Claire reports to the group, and we decide to meet in the location that k1k1 found.

Listening to the party bicker and get moving, [[D'Arby]] feels a little uncomfortable and unsure how he can help, so he decides to head back in to Civlar's. Civlar visibly flinches, and D'Arby reassures him, offering some apologies for the group's aggression and saying that he doesn't even do magic himself. Once he's sure the group is gone, Civlar agrees to talk to D'Arby. If D'Arby won't hurt him.

"I won't even move from this spot, big dog."

"Big dog...?"

"You know, like... cool cat."

"Uh. Okay. Sure."

Civlar asks to determine whether or not we were working for Becklinn. D'Arby says that we didn't, just that we kinda helped her out a little bit—and had one of her guys with us, but he went disappeared and that's why our party is out looking for him. Civlar comes clean that he's just a shop owner and an illusionist. He admits that he was approached by them to help disguise the source of their influx of weapons. D'Arby apologizes for him getting set up.

Civlar admits it was pretty cool at first! He was a hero of the town, got free drinks, and respect. But over time it's gotten hard to keep up the charade—and he's pretty good at charades! D'Arby asks who he worked for, whether it was Darrett, Becklinn, or whoever. Civlar says that the kid probably wasn't kept in the loop at all—it's definitely Becklinn's work. He would, like... pick up the weapons drops. They're dropped off at a warehouse at midnight. Some fisherman delivers them to the warehouse, he brings the weapons to the castle, he brings their payment to the fisherman. He's just the middleman.

D'Arby writes down as much as he can get about the information, since he's bad at remembering things. Civlar checks what he's doing, and asks if his name is on it. D'Arby realizes what he did and aggressively scribbles out Civlar's name. Civlar breathes a sigh of relief. He tells D'Arby that there's another midnight drop tonight, and wonders if maybe getting D'Arby's help would be cool, since they're heavy and D'Arby looks strong.

They continue chatting, and getting to know each other a little. Civlar says at one point that he thinks D'Arby's friend is a black mage—D'Arby says no, he thinks she's just a teenager. Civlar notes that black mages train very young. But they're both not sure why she doesn't wear robes, and discuss her pink colour scheme—maybe she's a pink mage? But there's no pink moon, so...

Meanwhile, the rest of the group converges on k1k1's location. Claire asks her where Darrett went. She leads us to a nook barely within the city's walls. We hear music from within the nook. We can distantly hear the clatter of the city, and the sea breeze. Nature, and the world, stand still. Light casts shifting patterns around us. Dragonflies fly around. This place is untouched by the progress of the rest of the city.

We push into the bushes and find a shrine. A wooden, weathered statue. A violin in hand pointing upwards toward the sunlight. We find Darrett by the statue, curled around a lute, playing music. Unaware of us, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2LR8YxdS0vQ">he sits and sings.</a>

```
The Father's face is stern and strong,
he sits and judges right from wrong.
He weighs our lives, the short and long,
and loves the little children

The Mother gives the gift of life,
and watches over every wife.
Her gentle smile ends all strife,
and she loves her little children

The Warrior stands before the foe,
protecting us where e'er we go.
```

Squall approaches, and kindly says that his song is beautiful. They ask what it is about. He says it's a song he learned a long time ago from his parents. About the pantheon that used to be here. He asks how we found him. Squall says that we followed him, and apologizes—says we were worried that he either got hurt, taken, or abandoned us. We didn't know. They ask what made him need to come here so suddenly.

Darrett says that it... is because maybe he couldn't turn us in. Or maybe he didn't want to. Maybe he needed to get away from us. He admits that he overheard us in Civlar's shop. Squall says we did. They tell him that he reminds them of themself at some point in time. They know it's difficult to choose a friend over duty—so if he needs time to think, we'll leave him here.

He takes a moment to think, then says that he lied to us earlier: he's been a squire a lot longer than he said he was. He doesn't remember a lot, but his dad was... apparently a painter. A good one. He doesn't remember much, save for when his dad sat him down and explained his gods to him. Worship is rare in this place, but it helped his dad. His dad described his god, and a power the god granted, through music. A power called Inspiration.

He tells us about when he met Becklinn, and saw the knights fight off dragons. And that that inspired him.

Squall says that she would do anything to protect her people, and that they don't fault her about that. Darrett says that whatever crime she might have done, she was doing it for them, and that that can't really be wrong, can it? It doesn't mean they're changing.

Vayb speaks up, and tells him that we're all changing. That you can cling to these ideals, but... the people you know. They're the things that change you. They're the ones worth fighting for. He glances to the rest of the group. Toward Claire and Squall. He sighs, then offers a hand to Darrett. He says it's okay to feel scared or bad about it sometimes, but at the end of the day you've just gotta pick yourself back up, keep going, and believe that the path you're on is the right one. He tells Darrett he's a good kid, and not to be too hard on himself about that.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i can't get a read on vayb. is he... being nice?? something is so weird about that guy

Darrett says that he wanted to be a knight, that he wanted to do the right thing. That he could bring hope back, even the hope the knights think is dead. That he wanted to prove himself to them. Squall tells him about how it's normal to have parental figures, guiding lights, heroes, that you'll look up to and learn that they're just a person in the end. And that you have to decide what kind of person _you_ are going to be.

> \[!Claire Note]
> what squall was saying... it's... kind of like my dad. isn't it?

The DM calls for a group Persuasion check from Squall and Vayb. Otto, for his part, hasn't met this kid and isn't as invested. Claire, for her part... is actually taking these words in, more than she is actually contributing to the persuasion.

Darrett tells them that he made an oath when he was younger. One that means everything to him. Not a knightly oath—but one he made to him (gesturing to the statue). He thought the knights were the way to fulfil that oath, but... they might not be. He takes a deep breath, and grabs Vayb's hand. He says he wants to help these people first, though. Squall asks if he's gonna come with us to see Becklinn. And beyond, Vayb adds. Darrett says he feels some kind of call, and realizes it might be further beyond the knights. He tells us that he thinks we should take some time to talk, make sure we're on the same page, and plan. Maybe spend some time at an inn he knows.

We discuss where/when to go, and what Becklinn knows and what we can do about it. Darrett points out that he was her spy, and that he's pretty sure he was operating alone. And with our current popularity/hero status, it's hard to get anyone to kill us.

D'Arby, who got lost looking for us, heads back to the ship, and arrives at around the same time as us.

Claire tries to get food on the way to the ship, and the only vendor around is a really weird old guy named Fizban. Claire is weirded out by this old freak, so when he trips, she just leaves him and leaves with her hand on her focus inside her purse. (Player note: I can't believe this XD)

At the ship, Darrett is surprised to see that our airship is _metal_, and is COMPLETELY caught off guard by Pixie. The group and Pixie banter a bit. Darrett steps on, taking things in, in awe. D'Arby says hi from his seat—Darrett asks if he's the ship. D'Arby just tells him to look to his left. They greet each other, Darrett tells D'Arby that he didn't turn us in, and they talk about how overwhelming everything is.

Darrett asks what is going on—if Jerrick was right about us. If we're Illuminati. Claire gestures him to the cockpit.

Meanwhile, D'Arby shows Otto what he found out from Civlar. Otto just stares at the paper, looks at D'Arby and asks how he got this. He catches Otto up, explaining how he learned all this by just... being nice and talking to Civlar. Then he tried to catch up, got lost, got a drink, and went back to the ship. D'Arby asks Otto if we're keeping the new guy. Otto asks D'Arby if he had siblings growing up. D'Arby says he doesn't remember. Otto just explains that sometimes, you know, your siblings find a dirty animal on the side of the road and want to keep it as a pet. D'Arby says it's awful to keep a human pet—Otto explains that it's just a metaphor.

Darrett heads back in and asks Otto if he's the captain, then geeks out about how he's, like, a warrior and our _leader_. Otto asks if he's good in a fight—Darrett seems to be unsure. Otto replies that "You have no idea. Perfect! You'll fit right in." Darrett asks Otto how this works. Is he our squire now?

Vayb butts in, saying that he's not anyone's squire. He's an equal member. Otto glances at him, says "right", and tells him he can explain what he knows, and we can square that up with what D'Arby learned. Claire and Squall are shocked that he actually got information. Squall asks if they need to revise their assessment of him.

Onto business, Darrett tells us that he doesn't think that assaulting the castle is the play—that he doesn't know how deep Becklinn is in this, but she's stubborn. He then corrects himself and says that she's dedicated. We turn to discussing [[Marshal Plimbitt|Plimbitt]], the source behind the weapons.

Darrett tells us that he knows she has a little device to directly reach out to this Plimbitt guy. We think that we could steal it, or crash the meeting to try to get a link on Plimbitt. Claire also notes what Jerrick knew and wonders if Plimbitt is on the dark side of the moon. We briefly consider rushing to the moon, but decide against it. D'Arby points out that we haven't seen any other hunters, and briefly the topic of how we didn't wear a disguise comes up—Claire takes a _lot_ of offense, considering she dyed her hair blonde for this. Ultimately, though, we decide to rest up and go to the warehouse at midnight.

We take a long rest before then.

Otto calls a team meeting to chat and catch up on details. He tells Claire that he's glad she's back, then asks if she saw anything while she was dead. She thinks, and only remembers the sight of lights and stars, and knowing she was one. Then he asks the group, even though he doesn't really mind Darrett joining, to ask him next time—Claire argues with him, pointing out that he stayed on the ship, but ultimately reluctantly relents. Vayb also backs Otto up, and offers an apology for making the choice without him. Moving on from that, Otto asks the party what the plan is for the meeting with Plimbitt/his men are. We discuss that briefly.

Jump cut to the warehouse!

We see two Solamnian Knights here, watching the doors. Otto and Vayb take the lead. Otto asks Vayb if he wants to "him them with the Inspector General approach." Vayb agrees, then realizes he has no idea what that is. When he asks for clarification, Otto just keeps on heading toward the guards.

They greet him and ask what they can do for him—Otto explains that he's here for the routine building inspection, saying he assumes that's why they're here: to let him in. When they ask if the Guild sent them, Vayb backs that up, and Otto says that Becklinn contracted it. The Knights get mad, saying the Guild needs to be a lot better about informing them. The duo says it's not their fault, and apologizes for the error. The guards say it must have been Darrett who didn't tell them. The four of them spend a minute shit talking Darrett.

The guards almost let them inside. But then they erupt with a "WAIT!" And the tension raises a moment. Then the guards ask: "hey, wait, we've been trying out, like... a routine. Would you guys give us your thoughts?" And they explain the routine of one truth telling and one lying—and Vayb and Otto tell them that that's a GREAT idea and that they're wasted on this job.

Once the guards are distracted, D'Arby uses his _ring of jumping_ to walk up to the wall, look at us, and then LEAP over the wall like a cat. To get Darrett in, we decide that he attuned the _wings of flying_ that we haven't decide which PC gets to wear. Darrett uses those wings to ferry us up.

We enter the deceptively simple warehouse's first floor, looking to be pretty barren of anything magical. We get ready to hide. Claire heads toward the split second level and casts _detect magic_. She finds a large metal _hippo_ that radiates magic in a back room. It has a red gemstone in it. It radiates evocation school magic. Claire isn't familiar with its nature, exactly, but she can tell that it's a bomb. We debate taking, disarming, exploding, whatever else, and Claire decides not to mess with it.
