# Session 3 - Ship Roles & New Rivals

Murdoc clarifies that [[D'Arby]] checked out of the race because the rest of us were not racing with _honour_. Shmamy insists that [[Vayb]] was; he was just winnin'.

## Ship Role Assignment

In the ship's cockpit, [[Claire]], Vayb, and D'Arby start sorting out who gets what ship role.

Vayb insists on being the pilot, so he gets to be the Helmsman.

Vayb and Claire note that [[Otto]] likes giving orders (and would be the least annoying doing so) Captain.

Claire, "as a trained arcane technician", says she'll be the Boatswain.

Which leaves [[Squall]] and D'Arby as the Gunners.

D'Arby asks about the pronunciation of "bosun". Otto remarks on the fact that D'Arby can spell, and suggests he stay on standby for "spelling orders" in the future. Claire is very confused by these people.

## Meeting **[[PIXIE]]**

Meanwhile, **Squall** arrives at _Tilly's Truck Stop_ from their own little adventure, and is pointed toward our giant flowery hammerhead shark train spaceship! Tilly notes that the rest of the crew is in there.

Squall remarks that that is "certainly an interesting boat." The door won't unlock for them.

**PIXIE** asks Squall if they want her to hack our facilities so they may enter. The rest of us, still discussing, see the lights flicker. PIXIE's voice echoes through the ship. "Data acquired. You may enter."

The lights turn back on and we run into Squall. Otto warns Squall about someone hacking our lights, but they confess that that was them. PIXIE introduces itself and explains that Squall offered it freedom in exchange for helping them out.

Claire is shocked that Squall would offer it freedom, but weird looks/judgment from the others make her think twice about making a comment about freeing an AI. Otto checks in with us about recruiting PIXIE. Then tells PIXIE that, in the future, it's less rude to knock before hacking in. PIXIE replies that it doesn't have free will so it can't choose to do that. Squall offers it free will.

PIXIE downloads free will.

Then she lights up the ship and introduces herself properly now that she is "free from that shell". She has a BOISTEROUS personality, refers to us as "you fucks", and asks if we're friends or enemies of Squall and if it needs to blast us.

The group reacts to this introduction. Claire asks if we gave our ship a god complex. Otto points the finger at Squall. PIXIE interrupts and clarifies to Claire (clairifies, if you will) that she needn't worry—there is no God!

Once the group processes PIXIE's arrival, we talk about what to do next—catch Wildcard and get our next bounty, we suppose? PIXIE informs us that Wildcard runs in half an hour, but before we go, it has a gift for us, and produces earpieces that link us (and PIXIE) together. D'Arby seems quite unnerved by everything going on with PIXIE.

PIXIE asks if we need anything else. Vayb asks if it has games—it proclaims that it can pirate SO MANY games. Claire points out that this is exactly what we just put Starfist in jail for. PIXIE proclaims it won't get caught, unlike Starfist. The party debates the ethics of pirating videogames for a moment.

Otto checks on D'Arby and pulls him aside to do some sparring/stretching.

PIXIE, unprompted, downloads a map of known space.

Lastly in getting everything in order, asks if we want to set its personality module. It has a few options: started on preppy, and includes a cool mode or (BLEEP). We ask for clarification on (BLEEP), and she demonstrates, activating the mode. She asks, sultrily, "what can I do for you, big boy?" And the group scrambles to get her to stop.

We turn back to discussing what voice to use, and settle on preppy or cool, but ultimately choose preppy. Group consensus was, as long as it wasn't seductive mode, it doesn't matter. Someone (it was either Squall or Otto) jokes that we now have psychological warfare to use on enemies. Claire says she'd rather self-destruct the ship than do that, and that devolves into a discussion about whether or not we should (ethically) let PIXIE install a self destruct module.

## Back at _The Laughing Beholder_

Once this is done, we agree on heading to the Laughing Beholder to catch Wildcard. Now, the bar is BUSTLING. Kobolds play cards, people smoking and telling stories of their last hunts, arm wrestles between an orc and a human cyborg in the corner. The air is thick with the scents of drinks and smoke and more.

Commercials are on the TV, but Wildcard will begin shortly.

[[Large Luigi]] greets us excitedly and congratulates us on winning the race and getting a ship. Vayb asks for a drink for the winner—Luigi offers to make something. We all sit down and order, claiming the one table that is free.

Otto asks Luigi if there are any other famous/notorious groups around here we ought to keep an eye out for. He replies that there are lots of skilled people here, including some advanced groups. Vayb replies that we're advanced, which prompts Luigi to sputter and say that he admires the confidence, but...

He offers advice about choosing the bounties.

He doesn't know much about the more advanced people we ought to keep an eye out for, but there was a new group that has been around lately—they don't seem to be here at the moment. But for the highest ranked group, he points out the Kobold Kolliders.

There's a ranking board that marks the best teams of [[Aces]], leveled at Jack, Queen, King, Ace, and Joker. The Full House. The Jack is a pair of mind flayers, the Queen is a group called the Lucky Bastards, the King is one person with a hook hand. Ace is a trio of goblins stacked on top of each other and wearing a trenchcoat and a bowler hat (the Stacked Beans). (I missed the Joker's description, oopsie)

Otto remarks shock about the mind flayers, and comments that they're evil and hive-minded, and should be exterminated. Luigi comments that things aren't so simple, since his kind is typically pretty evil too.

Turning back to discussion of the Full House, Luigi mentions that the Joker hasn't worked in AGES—since no bounty has topped his yet, he still holds the main record. You submit your bounties to be rated and a panel scores them (or something like that?).

(Prediction: Joker is the BBEG)

Otto stands up to talk to the kobolds. They're arguing about something, then notice Otto and call him over to settle a bet. He agrees, and they ask him to answer a question: The Joker. "Which one is the Joker? The dragonborn or the fox?" Otto says that his answer is that the Joker isn't really—it's just a play to make the Full House seem more impressive than they are.

The kobolds start arguing about how it's a complex system and that he saved the world!! They start yelling about how (or how it's impossible that) the fox(did I hear that one right?) is ratatouilling the dragonborn. Otto and Vayb double down and convince the kobolds that the Joker is a conspiracy. One of the kobold has a meltdown about it.

Otto shoots a look to Vayb, shocked that these are somehow the best in the bar. But he turns back to console them, explaining that he's happy they're freed from the illusion, and that the ladder they want to climb is actually smaller than they think. He says they should celebrate the revelation over drinks.

He settles down and introduces himself. The lead kobold says his name is Honda. Otto introduces Vayb too, who just coolly smokes. Otto ends up joining them to play some cards.

Back at their table, Claire and D'Arby chill while Claire records a vlog and we wait for the show to start.

Behind them is another crew—a werewolf wearing nothing, a human on their phone and not paying attention, and now sitting on the table... a plasmoid, whom Squall had met during their little misadventure.

They had stepped outside to solve a dispute (the werewolf says he had to pee, and the sea elf tells him to settle down), and we took their seat when they were.

## The Greenhouse

The group starts discussing what to do next, and we head back to our ship.

Vayb asks PIXIE what she can get us on this guy—and Claire asks if she can hack into Giffopedia. She says she COULD but she needs some extra help from a hacker and likely some hacking into a physical terminal in a giff base... and wouldn't you know it, Lord Greeble went to a base on the Rock!

Otto suggests we scrape public information on Plimbit first. The group discusses this, and PIXIE interrupts that she doesn't have any connection to the Worlds Wide Weave (shout out to Cheese for naming this), but there is a database in a place called the Greenhouse.

Squall, who's been there before, leads us to the place.

Massive crystalline spires reflect prismatic displays out of them. Bioluminescent flowers glow. Spiral staircases connect different levels with different biomes. Despite the beauty, the space feels quite empty. Our footsteps echo through the chambers. Only one other people waits here—a half giant, on their knees and tending to a garden.

Squall recognizes themself as **Sally**—who'd handed them a pamphlet on Squall's side adventure. She greets us and smiles. She speaks very slowly. Squall introduces her as someone who introduced them to the P.I.S.S. The group takes a moment to note that that spells "piss". Sally prefers we spell it out.

She and Claire both start talking at the same time because she speaks so slowly—and then Sally keeps trying to defer to Claire, who waits awkwardly and then starts at the same time as her. Claire gives up and lets the others speak.

Otto takes point on asking about connecting to the Worlds Wide Weave. Sally yells out for a doctor named **Hickory**—replies by yelling and asking "is it my bitch of an ex-wife; she already has my soul _and_ the kids, what more does she want?"

Hickory then comes out, carrying a shotgun, and greets us. Realizing we're not his ex-wife, he asks what we want. Otto explains that he was told Hickory could show us to a database. Hickory checks that we aren't just trying to look at porn.

We explain that we're Aces and doing a "history project" of sorts.

We make some conversation and eventually Dr. Hickory lets us access the terminal.

Meanwhile, Vayb had snuck off to go sit among the plants and... almost cry. Sally finds him and asks if he's okay. "Kinda a loaded question." "I have, a loaded, answer... hahaha. hahaha. hahaha." Vayb takes a moment to formulate his words, then asks why this place is so beautiful, but... "ya know, some people never see somethin' like this. That's just not right."

Sally doesn't quite know how to answer, but is happy to likes it. Vayb supposes that there's a lot of light out there in all that dark. Sally replies that this _is_ the Rainbow Ocean. She asks if he's a groundling or spaceborn. He replies groundborn—he's from _[[Athas]]_. She recognizes it and smiles, then explains that "this place, is for, you... we preserve, wildlife, from many, different worlds, long after, they are gone." She points him to some purple spiky flowers.

It's a plant that is... _phenomenally_ rare on his world, as most are. He's never seen it before, but he does recognize the flower as being from his world.

Vayb asks if he can take a cutting of this. Sally says that she has one rule. If you take from this place, you must also give back. She agrees, but only if he promises to take a flower from another world on his travels. He does, and he takes a cutting then rejoins us.

Back to the main group, we start researching. Otto starts asking around about whatever he can on search engines, forums, and more—getting Claire's help to automate the process. Despite profanity sent our way, some people do like the cut of our jib and provide us with information. We get two major pieces of information.

From a user named **Renegade888**, we learn: they remember seeing a red ship, giff military with a large cannon, heading into deep space of the Gemini district.

Another user named **[[L1CH]]** remembers seeing a ship flying in a weird pattern nearby(?)

D'Arby takes point on sorting through tips. Squall and Pixie help him out. D'Arby recognizes that Renegade888 has a first grade reading level and is likely false information.

We gather that the red ship would be a giff bombard, floating toward a backwater planet called _Krynn_. We search through the data and confirm: Plimbit, aware of the bounty placed on him via Giffopedia (assuming he still has access), would be able to track what the giff are saying about him. Additionally, if he was selling weapons on Krynn, the _[[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]]_ would be a close place to do space crime to acquire the weapons... which confirms he is, in fact, based on Krynn.
