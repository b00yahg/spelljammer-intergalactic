As we begin the session, [[Shilear]] is abustle with preparations. [[Shaw]] is giving orders.

But the Ocean Court has done some preparations of their own, so we flash back.

As the ship, [[PIXIE]] greets [[Claire]], who wants to do research. She finds a mess, with [[Darrett]] in a pile of energy drinks, furiously typing on the computer. Claire remarks how she's shocked (and impressed) he's able to use one of these. He says he set up a profile like she told him to, and PIXIE explains that Darrett has been arguing with people about the nature of divinity on Krynn.

He's been furiously debating with a guy called AsrielTheAllKnowing who's been telling him that his dragon queen is Tiamat, and he goes on a big, passionate rant about how this guy is talking BULLSHIT and that even though the guy says he's an "expert on Krynnology", Darrett literally IS FROM THERE.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i'm so proud of this guy, already picking fights on the worlds wide weave.
> they grow up so fast (߹ ᯅ ߹)

Claire and PIXIE encourage Darrett to insult the man's mother. Darrett decides to leave and calm down. Claire walks past all the energy drinks and gives him a cup of very sweet, very caffeinated iced coffee, to "calm him down".

With the computer under her stewardship, Claire researches Dragon and [[2NGEL]]—finding that his past has been scrubbed from the internet, often finding only a single pulsing green skull icon. But, she does find one thing: a record of the purchase of his destroyer from The Arcane.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i swear i'm gonna find that stupid l1ch person.......

Claire uses a spell slot to cast _infallible relay_ to call the Laughing Beholder's phone line, and speaks to [[Large Luigi]] about Dragon—the DM makes a promise to RP this between sessions since we're not gonna get to 2NGEL tonight.

Back in the present, Shaw shows up at the ship and whistles us over. He tells us he needs help making decisions about the defense of the town.

Apparently, the Mueller Twins, who we hired a couple sessions back, are huddled around a safe that they found for us. They're trying to break it open with a psychic blast to no effect. They found it buried here, Shaw thinks it's Void Wrangler cash, perhaps?

[[D'Arby]] congratulates the kids on their work. They ask if we think we can get it open. [[Vayb]] asks Claire to detect magic on it. [[Otto]] decides to kick it, and chastises us for losing time. Vayb questions him, pointing out that we have a day to work and Claire's gonna ritual cast _detect magic_. Otto says "ten minutes?? I don't have time for that!" Claire remembers that this is something her father has said HUNDREDS of times throughout her life.

Otto and D'Arby successfully kick it open, finding: 1 potion of healing, 1 potion of greater, 1 ointment (potion) of heroism, and a potion of flying. We divide the loot among the party, except Claire who already stocked herself up on potions.

Vayb and Shaw discuss the tactics of the fight. They decide on a place to put a bomb trap down that we can lure the Wranglers into, among other things.

Before the fight starts, the orc fella who fought with D'Arby at the bar walks over and gives a rehearsed apology about managing anger and drunkenness, about how D'Arby didn't deserve it, and how the town is grateful that we're helping. D'Arby gives a speech about how it's water under the bridge, and the orc replies that he loves D'Arby. D'Arby changes the subject and asks if he can help with moving boxes.

Shaw asks if we have any words for the town. Vayb drags him up. Vayb begins a speech. "Friends, Shileareans, countrymen." He gives them a speech about how they're a tough and proud people, and they ain't gonna stand for someone kickin' them. Vayb points to his companion, and says that Shaw is an outsider, like himself, but he made himself a defender of this community. How Shaw is gonna continue leading them all, and Vayb nods and shoves him up forward to give his own inspiring words.

Shaw cocks an eyebrow at the comment that he'll keep leading the town. Shaw steps up and begins his own speech, about his past in the Elvish Military, that he's met good and strong people across his life. He says there's part of him that wants to say that he's the worst of them. And it's probably not true, but standing here in front of these good people, he... (The DM cut out and didn't want to re-improvise his speech, but he summarized it for us)

D'Arby steps up and encourages everyone to protect their lives more than the buildings of the town.

Otto steps up, and says simply: "The Wranglers think this town belongs to them. Prove them wrong."

It is high noon, now. The town holds its breath, a quiet wind whispers through the empty storefronts. Those who can fight stand ready under the scorching sun.

An army of Void Wranglers crests the hill. They surge forward "like hungry dogs straining against invisible leashes." They hoot and holler, firing phaser blasters into the air. One holds a cannon. An obsidian tank rolls behind them. Blackeyes tears through on a spellbike.

Blackeyes shouts out for Shaw, saying he's sure everyone here is ready to wrap this shit up. He asks them if they're gonna be stupid, or if this town belongs to them.

Shaw roars back in defiance, and the Void Wranglers cheer and whoop, pulling their weapons and firing.

Vayb, who's linked us telepathically, tells D'Arby (who has the detonator for our explosives) to get ready to fire. D'Arby tells Claire to count him down.

She focuses, watches, and finds a good moment. She gestures a countdown: 3, 2, 1... "Boom."

AND BOOM!

Four Void Wranglers are thrown into the air, dead instantly.

> \[!Claire Note]
> BAM! _nice_.

_**[[Lets Jam - Ship Combat Mechanics for D&D|Let's Jam]]!**_

Shaw gives us advice, pointing out that they're rigging a tower in the back graveyard, to take down their tank, and then asks "what the hell is that?" as D'Arby recognizes the sound of Stabbin' Steve screaming in the distance.

Then, suddenly, music blasts out over the scene... we look up, and find a Space Bugatti in the air. Our rival group of [[Aces]] has appeared! The sea elf prince shouts "What's up, homosexuals?!", before getting corrected about how that's not quite appropriate. He says Common is weird.

> \[!Claire Note]
> right after pride month??

It seems our rival Aces, along with a group of others, have signed on with the Void Wranglers.

Shaw heads off alone, D'Arby goes to free Stabbin' Steve, Otto and Vayb head to the barricade to help the townsfolk, [[Squall]] takes up a position as a sniper, and Claire returns to the Ocean Court to try to deal with the others/the infernal machine tank by yelling at Darrett to get on the guns.

Starting at the graveyard, the Void Wranglers excitedly turn toward the lone D'Arby, when one of them is suddenly sniped and killed by Squall. D'Arby says "yeah, I did that with my _mind_!"

Back at the barricade, Vayb and Otto begin a skirmish with the Void Wranglers.

Otto swings at one of them, who shouts "parry!", but Otto sees this obvious parry coming, feints, and whips his baton around to hit him in the ribcage. The Wranglers fight back, and Otto critiques their poor footwork. That said, due to their success, they start cheering themselves on as the best of the best.

This brings it to Vayb, who finger guns, fireballs them, and clears half of them immediately. Otto and Vayb do an intimidation routine, and their morale breaks. The Wranglers there run away, despite Blackeyes' protestations (followed by a storm of repeated curse words) over their comms.

D'Arby's intimidation attempt is less successful, but Stabbin' Steve is happy to see him—and is promptly yelled at by his boss about it. An electric shock runs through Steve as the machine powers on.

D'Arby moves, so fast he trails a cloud of sand behind him like fog. He swings twice, and the sand clears. Two bodies drop to the ground. Then he action surges. Steve's captain PANICS while he types on the machine.

Claire runs into the ship, yells at Darrett to ask if he's ready to hop on the guns. He shouts "FUCK yeah!!!", and she tells PIXIE to turn targeting on that infernal machine for him.

Clair rams the Ocean Court Express right into the Bugatti for lots of damage and some minor damage to our ship (despite Vayb's condition for her using the ship). She and the sea elf captain argue over their comms—and she tries to get him to think twice about tussling with the Ocean Court Express. She demonstrates their guns by having Darrett fire upon the infernal engine. Darrett, who is caffeinated to high hell, is manic and laughs like a lunatic while he shouts to Otto in excitement.

Claire's intimidation is unsuccessful on Blue, who laughs and says he would never surrender. But his crew, persuaded by Claire to cut their losses, turn on their captain—telling him it's not worth the amount of the bounty to win the same amount back, and that knowing the Ocean Court they're accidentally gonna kill Shaw anyway.

They put a sock gag on their captain's mouth to "mute" him, then fly away.

> \[!Claire Note]
> gotta hand it to them, they're iconic for that

Claire tells them to "subscribe to claire's cornerrrrr~" as they leave.

From the ship and Squall's vantage point, we can see that Shaw has been captured by Blackeyes, who has him bound in a hold person device—and he's taking his bike toward Vayb and Otto.

The captain in the graveyard unleashes a powerful blast from the arcane tower, which hurts D'Arby, Squall, and the Mueller Twins with its magic.

Squall takes a shot at Blackeyes and knocks him off of his bike! Most of his army is running, afraid of how quickly their haphazard assault is being foiled.

Blackeyes gets up, ranting in a rage about how stupid we are, about whether we think 2NGEL is gonna take this calmly.

Otto interrupts, chastising him for breaking the deal: his men attacked us when we handed Shaw right over to him. Blackeyes asks if we really think 2NGEL will believe that—Otto replies that 2NGEL will believe whoever's strong enough to win.

Blackeyes' ranting turns into screams as his nanotech arm takes over his body and transforms into a mech suit.

We have all gathered when the fight begins.

The infernal machine runs over D'Arby and almost gets Claire. She webs up the machine to the best of her ability.

Vayb and Otto take on Blackeyes. Blackeyes rants about how "strong 2NGEL has made me", and how weak we are in comparison. Otto replies that borrowed power isn't real power.

When Otto attacks Blackeyes, he grins, shouting that "FINALLY!! You're taking me seriously!! Now we can have a REAL duel!!"

Then, of course, he realizes what a real duel with Otto looks like. And Otto kicks his _ass_, quite handily. Otto dents the metal chest plate in, grabs it in his off hand, and then RIPS open his armor, dragging him out of the nanotech mech suit. Otto's eyes glow with power. He puts his baton hand around Blackeyes' neck, tells him that "you picked this fight." He brings his shield down on Blackeyes' face.

Blackeyes' eye rolls out of his bashed-in face. It begins to _beep! beep! beep!_ and then explodes! Otto leaps out of the way, but the bomb incinerates the body and destroys all the evidence.

D'Arby climbs into the webbed-up machine and takes control. He pumps the brakes, stopping the infernal machine.

We free Shaw, who looks at the ashes of the Void Wranglers' leader. The townspeople cheer and embrace each other, free, and proud, knowing they will not bow to any of their threats in the future.

Shaw looks at us, nods in respect, and congratulates us on victory. But, he tells us, we haven't won just yet. He looks up toward the stars, and says "the battle isn't over yet."

And the credits roll!
