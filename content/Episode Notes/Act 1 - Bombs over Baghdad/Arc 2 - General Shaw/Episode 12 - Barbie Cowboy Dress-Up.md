Continuing his interrogation of our prisoner, [[Otto]] circles him—cutting an imposing and threatening figure. He tells the pirate captain that he wants to know "everything" from these people—who they are, who's in charge here, everything.

Captain Bloodflow curses Otto, but explains. They settled in this area because it was "easy pickin's", for the relatively small size of their gang. Their leader (not him, notably?) chose to settle down here. There's towns here, and they sell something valuable, real valuable. But, considering how backwater this place is, they have little protection—which Bloodflow's gang offers, for a 30% cut of their earnings. In return, the gang doesn't kick the locals' shit in.

Otto insults their ways, and moves on to making a deal—how we might get in contact with their boss. Bloodflow explains that he's currently "planning" in their base. Moving on, Otto asks what can be learned about this sheriff. Bloodflow doesn't know much except for that he's the reason they're not making money anymore—anyone who doesn't pay gets threatened (or their houses burned down, if they're still adamant about not paying). In a recent such attack, this guy suddenly rolls in and starts shooting them with elven-grade military equipment.

Otto implies that we can maybe solve this problem for them, if he'll get us in contact with his boss (and return our hostages to boot). Bloodflow replies that "you think you're the first person to try this with Blackeyes? Fuckin' [[Shaw]] did too!" He recounts how Blackeyes, in response to Shaw pulling a similar stunt, he went to meet him one-on-one. "If you start puffing your chest like Shaw did, you're gonna end up in the crossfire."

Bloodflow sighs, and says he'll try, but warns Otto that Blackeyes has a temper—he had Bloodflow on guard duty like he was precisely because of his part in the Shaw situation. Bloodflow says he'll do it but he needs our word that we won't kill him—[[Vayb]] gives him a threatening look. Bloodflow panics about how "that's not an answer!" and about how he's afraid Vayb will eat him brain or something. Otto suggests he better get to work before Vayb gets hungry. Bloodflow then sets up the meeting.

Bloodflow is handed his communicator. Eventually, Blackeyes returns an affirmative, with a couple demands: we do this in their base and that we bring something valuable. It's a sort of "tradition" of Blackeyes', to collect a lil trophy from people. All his gang has done it.

Otto replies that we're not born yesterday; we'll go to a neutral meeting ground, and we're not bringing anything. In return, Blackeyes concedes that we'll meet in the canyons between his base and the town of [[Shilear]].

Otto convenes the group to discuss what we wanna do—the gist was we wanted to meet Blackeyes tomorrow after having some time to scope out the area and see how the locals feel about everything.

We head into the town of Shilear, and suddenly a voice connects to our minds via a _message_ spell, asking if there are any carbon-based lifeforms out here, as he senses. He asks who we are. Otto introduces us as the Ocean Court Express. The man says Otto speaks real fancy and asks if he's a lawyer—Otto replies that we aren't. The man expresses disappointment, citing that he needs help with divorcing his wife. In any case, we park our ship for a fee of 2 silver.

We disembark and meet the man. We introduce ourselves and talk about our trip over. Among other things, he asks us if we plan to steal his screws. Otto replies that he does. The man (Lobe Ludwig, apparently) says that he's gonna have to kick us back onto our ship, then. Otto points out he hasn't asked why. Lobe says he thinks "there's no moral justification for stealing a man's screws, but go ahead." Otto says his business with Lobe's screws is to _screw_ his wife. They laugh about the banter.

Moving on, Otto and [[D'Arby]] ask for directions to somewhere we can hang out (D'Arby's pointed to the Last Drop Saloon), as well as anywhere we can get some less ostentatious clothes—[[Claire]] scowls, Vayb dislikes this idea too. Lobe does agree that we look like "princes and princesses". D'Arby asks Claire if she heard that Lobe called him a princess. Lobe says D'Arby looks like the kind of person he'd marry his son off to.

Vayb walks ahead, looking out across the asteroid mining town, lighting a cigarette—he can look out over the town and see and appreciate the scrappiness of this town... life clinging to a rock floating in space. The idiosyncrasies, the craft and resourcefulness, of the unsleek life this far off in space. Buildings made of the hulls of spelljammers, sheet metal cobbled together, the rough and tumble people.

The party walks into frame behind him, yapping and breaking the brooding vibe he has going for him.

As we enter the town, people give us strange looks.

As we walk and are guided by Lobe, Otto asks him what the need for a full TOWN like this is, rather than just automating it. Lobe explains the need of human supervision and a human touch in the mines. Otto asks about why they _live_ here, though, full time. Lobe explains that it's a little odd, but a lot of people... well, he explains his story: that he used to be a navigator for a major gnome ship, but eventually he got sick of going from place to place... being undermined and restricted by the laws there. For instance, the elves have laws against building hydrogen weaponry! "If a man wants to build a hydrogen bomb, he should be allowed to!" In either case, he explains, that this place offers a fresh start. People get to _take charge_ of their lives here. Otto muses that "this rock isn't much, but it's yours, eh?"

We arrive at the general store, an old squidship that has been carved out into a store. The tentacles opened up to make the door. People mill about the shop, gathering things in their hands and heading toward the counter, where a broad-shouldered man with tiny spectacles looks angrily at a book. Lobe excitedly greets the shop owner, who sighs and asks what he wants. Lobe says he's just showing us around. The man puts on a more composed front, slicks his white hair back and breathes to try to calm the flushing of his face, and asks how we're doing. Otto introduces himself and says that we're doing terrible, with the state of our clothes for this rock! Vayb ignores him and starts looking around for a poncho.

Otto and the shop owner discuss clothes. In the meantime, D'Arby leans over to [[Squall]] and muses that it seems our tour guide isn't very popular. Squall does concede that his marriage disintegrating probably happens for a reason.

D'Arby asks the shopkeep what kind of clothes he recommends for him—D'Arby gets an AWESOME red-black cowboy fit. Otto picks out an outfit with a black duster. Claire picks out something simpler, a chic fit with denim overalls and a cowgirl hat. Vayb finds a purple poncho that fits the clothes he already has on. Vayb plops a bowler hat on Squall. With the shopkeep's help, Squall gets a green vest to wear instead of their suit.

While we're gonna be rung through, a customer objects, saying they were getting ready first—and making the man leave us and focus entirely on them. Otto feels something is off, and notices a purplish glint to the kid's eyes: he's using psionics.

Otto tries to interject, asking if he's gonna be dealing with us so we can pay him. The man hesitates. Otto inspects the kids, who are about 17, who are carrying candy bars and other snacks. D'Arby moves in, throwing a casual arm around the kids and asks if they need help paying for stuff. This manages to intimidate the kids, who skedaddle and leave. D'Arby and Otto follow, and the kids yammer about how they didn't mean anything by it. D'Arby says he's not an authority figure—Otto cuts in and says he kind of is, but he has no intention of getting them in trouble.

One of the kids says "so you ain't gonna tell Momma?" D'Arby points out that now we know they have a momma. The other kid yells at him for it. Otto launches into a speech—accidentally calling them "y'all" and sighing in embarrassment about picking up the accent.

Otto eventually presses them into talking—the twins admit that they have a "talent". Otto replies that he knows what psionics are. Otto points out that kids stealing and getting in trouble is a common thing—and a sign of boredom. He suggests remedying that by offering them a job to spy around the town for some money. Nothing illegal, but they'll watch out for us. D'Arby offers them one gold piece as a _forward_ payment—if they get something good, we'd give them more.

As they go to depart, the kids end up pointing out that their mom runs the inn and they can maybe get us somewhere to stay for cheaper.

[[Darrett]] catches up to us, and Vayb insists that Darrett go get a better outfit. He awkwardly points out that he's working for us but he hasn't, technically, uh... been paid. The group gasps, realizing we forgot, then Vayb just hands him 50 gold. He goes to get himself a cool fit, "just like D'Arby's." Claire interrupts, and styles an outfit for him herself, one that gives him a cowboy look with a bit of a cape about it to preserve a knightly aesthetic.

The group then splits up about town: Claire has half of a plan herself, but most of the party goes to the Last Drop Saloon. The saloon is a rowdy, classic Western bar. Like horses would be, gigantic hamsters are tied to posts outside—their riders are inside. As the party arrives, an orc throws a woman out into the streets. The orc yells at the woman, Jessie, for trying to cheat with fake cards. She says that "you can't blame a girl for tryin'!" and punches the orc with an extendable prosthetic arm.

Otto and D'Arby move in to break up the barfight. They pull the pair aside and try to learn what happened. The orc says they were playing cards, and then he looked over and found that her deck was half aces! Jessie spits that if it took him that long it's his fault. (The orc is named Todd) They also meet an elf named Eliza, who chastises the pair (friends of hers) for not being able to make it 30 hours without fighting.

Settling down at the table for some cards, Eliza makes conversation with the party and asks who we are and what we're here for. Otto explains that we're just drifters passing through. They chat a bit with Eliza and learn that she's acting as the deputy while the sheriff is off on some business.

Claire takes Darrett with her and uses _disguise self_ to look like she's from Krynn. The sheriff's office looms over the middle of town—a weathered sign depicting a badge swings over the door. Dust settles on a rocking chair on the front porch. Wanted posters hang on the outside of the office. The only one we recognize is of Bloodflow, who is currently tied up in our ship. His bounty is worth 2 copper. Everyone else numbers in silver-to-low gold.

Darrett asks what we're up to here, and Claire spins him a story about how we just left our backwater and we need to see if the place is safe to settle since we heard stories about a sheriff dealing with the gangs around here. We approach the front door and see a sign that says "OUT". She considers breaking in.

She looks around and sees an elven woman across the street who could see us, but she moves into her apothecary shop and heads upstairs. Claire picks the lock and tells Darrett to watch out. She checks if he knows any magic—and he admits something to her on the condition that she keep it quiet: old Krynn traditions used to channel godly magic through their songs and now his songs are channeling some magic. He says that everyone else does amazing stuff with their psychics and batons and he felt like his wasn't very use Claire tells him how that's actually really cool, and points out that he might be his world's first cleric in ages.

Feeling a little more confident, he tells her that he does have a song that notices magic. One such spell is laced into the door—Claire realizes it's an _alarm_, but he does also illuminate something from upstairs. Claire can see enough from the aura that there's elven grade military equipment here. And maybe a magical chest?

She is tempted enough to cast _dispel magic_ on the _alarm_ and creep inside, pulling out her camera to record the place. She finds a relatively unadorned, empty, and worn-down first floor. She heads up the stairs, not rolling amazingly on stealth, if it matters. She heads upstairs to find a bedroom, sheets rough and wrinkled, like the bed's occupant has been tossing and turning. There's clothes scattered. Bottles around the room. A closet, which has weapons inside it—an elvish military rifle adorned with ruby-red accents, a pair of fancy boots (metallic with a gemstone infused onto it), an ironed elvish navy uniform (a general's outfit), a small box with a small screen device inside it attached to a headband, and something Claire recognizes as fairly cutting-edge tech produced by the [[The Sindiath Line|Sindiath Line]] and Ainscorp—an "ethereal blade". (Essentially a fucking lightsaber, that's so cool) Claire inspects the chest and determines that it probably has an _alarm_ up. She thinks it isn't worth burning _dispel magic_ on, so she just snaps more pictures and heads downstairs.
