The DM performs a wonderful narration for the Last Drop Saloon as the party steps into the swinging saloon doors. [[Claire]] hangs back and records the party, who wants to aurafarm as they enter the saloon—[[D'Arby]] swings open the doors and step on the LOUDLY creaking floorboards. Everything—the chatter, the piano, everything—goes completely silent as he enters. D'Arby strolls toward the bar and waves a casual 'hey' to everyone. He apologizes for the loud creaking, and finds that no sound leaves his mouth.

People in the bar wave for him to get off the floorboard, and some technicians come in to fix the _silence_ emanating from it.

[[Otto]] takes the lead entering following D'Arby, looking proud and cool—the orc employee waves him away from that floorboard. Otto strolls around it, and apologizes for inconveniencing him, then helps pull him off the ground from his kneeling position. The orc tells everyone not speaking in the bar that "this isn't the first time this has happened, y'all go back to fuckin' off and making me money!"

He greets the new arrivals, then heads back to the bar and polishes his whiskey glasses.

Around the room, people are gathered around tables gambling around glowing cards—a kobold, a mountain dwarf boasting to an uncaring audience, and many more gruff misfit and drifter types. D'Arby apologizes to another employee for making noise—Otto tells him that's 20 pushups, and refuses to elaborate, so D'Arby finds a corner and drops down.

Otto and [[Vayb]] head to the bar. Claire and [[Squall]] follow. Otto asks if we should split up and explore the place. Squall says they don't like the idea of splitting up in an unfamiliar place. Otto says that's exactly the timing for splitting up and exploring—to which Claire points out that this saloon is one single room.

Vayb reaches over the bar and grabs a bottle of alcohol. He grabs a green-tinted bottle, throws 5 gold on the table, and pops it open. The orc crosses his arms and frowns. Vayb asks if that doesn't cover it. The orc replies, "that'll be 6, partner." He seems to be annoyed that Vayb just grabbed the drink.

Otto sidles over, rolls his eyes, and tells Vayb that "your charm never ceases to amaze me, Vayb." The pair both pour a drink and down it... Turns out it's lime juice. The orc bartender laughs about it, then apologizes for fucking with Vayb, and goes and grabs some real alcohol for everyone—he says the 6 gold will more than cover it.

A local woman at the bar laughs and tells us that we "haven't been here five minutes and're already giving me a good laugh!" Otto replies that "you'll have to see how much we're capable of in an entire evening."

This kickstarts a conversation getting to know her. Vayb lets her know that we're just drifting through with the winds—that we're [[Aces]]. The bartender and lady ask us if we're here for work, and they try to downplay that we're actively here on a job, saying that we're just passing through and as Aces we're kinda always on the job. The bartender shrugs and says he hopes the job won't have us going too far from here and into the Weird.

Claire strolls around and gets her bearings around the area, finding that D'Arby's wandered off on his own. He stands up from his pushups and strolls over to some people playing cards, where there's an open seat. He asks them what game they're playing, and it's some kind of card game where you play hands of monsters and attack people—it sounds like some kind of mix between Poker and Magic :P

D'Arby settles in for a game and plays the game with a cool cat poker face. He plays well and wins, with the kobold griping angrily about losing. D'Arby offers him his money back—which the kobold accepts and then calls him a sucker.

Once that table refuses to play with D'Arby for being too good at the game, Otto joins that table and calls Claire over to record him joining a drinking game and arm wresting competition. Eventually a group forms to watch and bet on this event.

Otto biffs it, and gets pinned—he tries to bluff that he wasn't cued into it properly and didn't realize he was supposed to start. But, he's remarkably deceptive, so they restart the match. The opponent is against it, but his sister encourages him to go again after a proper countdown. They go again... and Otto gets handily pinned again, much to his embarrassment.

(While I, the notetaker, had failed to pay attention, and Squall bet ALL THEIR MONEY ON THIS... ... ... but had not specified who they were betting for. And obviously the strong guy is the obvious choice :P)

To resolve the high amount of betting as Squall bet a ton of money—the gal running the bets suggests another bet, double or nothing! However, Otto manages to get D'Arby tagged in instead, since he's tough. Whatever the specific arrangement ended up being, both Vayb and Squall ended up going ALL IN on their bets. (Vayb has every intention to use a _subtle spell_ to cheat)

Otto tries to convince the gal running the bets to limit the betting to 500 gold. Squall tries to convince her not to—Otto tells them to just shut up and bet 500 gold. Squall tries to convince her to make their own side bet where they can go all in.

D'Arby almost gets his shit wrecked in the arm wrestle, but has a vision of his imminent defeat and a psionic spell secretly assaults his opponent's mind—Vayb casts a hidden _silvery barbs_ and D'Arby performs a WILD defeat.

The girls in the betting pool look stone cold pissed, but Jane pays up and hands the group the money they earned.

Vayb sidles up to the ladies and offers to buy them a drink for their troubles. Squall heads up to him at the bar and somehow this devolves into them having a one on one conversation, telepathically, about whether or not Squall is a virgin and if they've had the virtual/robotic equivalent of sex with [[PIXIE|Pixie]]—kind of ignoring the girls he's flirting with.

Otto stops by the bar to ask Claire what she's been thinking—and she agrees that it's weird that these people have so much cash on that. Something is a lil suspicious.

Vayb, Squall and the gals go to dance.

Claire sits down at the bar to try to work the bartender and see if he can tell her much about the elf guy in town, the new sheriff. The bartender tells her the sad story about the troubles with crime here—how these bandits came, and how they killed the old sheriff. Then one day, [[Shaw]] stepped up. He'd been a quiet drifter for a while, but when the pirates came and threatened the dwarves, the kids we'd met at the general store tried to stop the Void Wranglers, and Shaw shot their gun hands clean off.

Blackeyes came for some retribution later, and Shaw made a declaration and that as long as he was standing in it, they wouldn't pay anything to the pirates anymore. Since then, Shaw's been trying to clean up the place more—among other things, he's turned the kobold from the bar, who's a pickpocket ever since he got left by his Witchlight Carnival, back to the straight and narrow by employing him for his talents to be used productively around the town instead.

Claire asks if Shaw's asked for anything in return, and it seems he hasn't. She jokes that that's very generous for an elf. The bartender agrees and says that he knows for sure that Shaw's not part of that Elven Empire on account of that—though he hasn't really asked much about Shaw's past because "there's some things you just don't make a fella talk about if he don't want to."

Meanwhile, D'Arby gets some gossip from the miners and corroborates some of that impression about Shaw—as well as the impression that Shaw will be outnumbered by all these Void Wranglers. And if that's the case? Well, who knows. Maybe he calls it quits then, maybe not. Maybe he dies.

Eventually, the evening winds down and the night falls. We set up an _alarm_ on our ship and on our rooms at the inn.

We don't hear someone enter the inn, and sneak across the floors. A masked individual with a white and blue mask and a long ponytail holds onto throwable blades. They're surprised by the CLANGING of our alarm bells going off. D'Arby wakes up. The individual slides back, taking out a naginata polearm.

Otto tells the group to take this person alive. Vayb throws a _drop_ command at them. Claire _blesses_ the party. In response to the command, the ninja shouts "WAIT! NO!" and drops a kunai that explodes on himself, Vayb, and Otto when it hits the ground.

Ganging up on him, we press the advantage significantly and kick his ass. Claire finishes up the fight by teleporting over to him and conking him over the head with her magic crossbow. The mask cracks open and breaks. His suit goes flat in colour, and he melts into a puddle of water; the assassin is, or rather was, a _simulacrum_. We did not get a proper glimpse of what was behind the mask as he melts.

Otto asks what the hell that was. Claire pokes around in the puddle. Otto points out that the ninja seemed oddly fixated on D'Arby, who—despite trying to think—doesn't remember any reason he'd know this guy or have made him want to kill him. He wonders if it was slime, but he wasn't the plasmoid from the bar...?

The party discusses what to do, but ultimately decides to discuss it in the morning once we've had some rest.
