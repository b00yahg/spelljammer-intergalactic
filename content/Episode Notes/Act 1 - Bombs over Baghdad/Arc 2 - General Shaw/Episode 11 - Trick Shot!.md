We begin the session in the stars. [[Vayb]] chats with [[PIXIE|Pixie]] while he flies and they're curating a playlist together. Pixie tells him how she's searched the web and found something like 3000 songs to start considering, and asks if he wants her to play them all at once.

Pixie finishes setting up comms permissions for the captain—Faisal has been given muting permissions on Discord to reflect this (incredible).

Eventually, something comes into view out of the [[The Phlogiston|Phlogiston]]. An eerily still collection of asteroids, save for clusters of sparkling lights across the surface of the rocks. Pixie tells [[Otto]] that she's plotted a course to the city, which Otto tells her to relay to Vayb.

Pixie isn't sure how to bring this up, since it hasn't happened before, but we have an incoming signal from one "Deathspider" 1000 feet from us. Otto says he's glad that name is so welcoming—very reassuring. Pixie corrects that that's the ship name—the person's name is Bloodflow. Otto has Pixie patch all of us into the comms, startling [[Claire]] and [[D'Arby]]. Otto tells us that it seems we have a welcoming party, and to stay sharp! Then he accepts the call.

"Ocean Court Express, to whom do I have the pleasure of speaking?"

An individual in a fur coat with a massive, gnarly facial scar appears. They seem to be in a dark room, with a couple lackeys who seem to be watching. The Deathspider's captain speaks. "Oi! What's another ship doing around these parts?"

Otto sarcastically thanks him for introducing himself, then delicately explains that we're hunting a fugitive.

In the meantime, D'Arby strolls to his battle station. He passes Claire, who's rushing to hers while trying to finish her makeup.

Captain Bloodflow of the Deathspider steps closer to the camera and takes a look at Otto, who can in turn see him better. Bloodflow seems to be a well-built drow. He introduces himself as the second in command of a gang and the captain of the Widowmaker. And we're in his territory.

Otto says he's sorry, he had no idea, given the _zero markers_ they'd put up. Bloodflow tells him this is one and to get out. Otto replies that it's not a big deal, we just wanted to make a quick stop, maybe spend some money that'd end up in gang hands. He doesn't blame Bloodflow for wanting to defend himself, but we're nothing like the vagrants he assumes are normally in this area.

Bloodflow considers what Otto has to say, then says "500." Otto asks if he has 500 gold worth of anything. "Your lives." Otto sighs and explains that he's not interested in intimidation, he's proposing a transaction.

Bloodflow thinks about it, then ups the offer to 600 gold. Otto says that sounds great, why doesn't Bloodflow come onto the ship to "collect it yourself, asshole." Bloodflow says "ah, yeah, that's be great—ah. Ah, c'mon," as he realizes what Otto's actually saying.

A Deathspider ship starts to orbit around an asteroid a thousand feet away, starting to move toward us.

D'Arby boards, and swiftly cuts one of the pirates down the middle.

Outside, as enemy ships close in, Vayb rolls around our ships to place their captain's ship between them and us. Squall blasts away. Claire hops on a gun and misses.

The fighter ships shout another "Trick Shoooootttttt!!" over the comms. The captain goes "Jerry, I swear to FUCK—" and the shot hits the Bloodflow's ship.

Otto and D'Arby handily take out Captain Bloodflow, and Otto tells D'Arby to try to get all the other pirates to stand down. [[Darrett]] runs in, ready to fight, declaring that it's "Darrett time!!" And then realizes he was late. D'Arby tells him to just round up all the weapons.

Despite the losing battle, one of the fighter ships shouts out another "TRICK SHOT!" and _finally_ succeed at landing a shot by ricocheting it off an asteroid. Squall crits and obliterates that ship.

Otto and D'Arby start rounding up the few stragglers to take as prisoners. D'Arby plays blackjack with one of the prisoners. As we get the prisoners settled on the ship, Vayb pulls us in to the asteroid's city.

A mishmashed, ramshackle little outpost clings to the dusty surface of the asteroid. Pixie highlights The Cradle on our navigation system, a crappy little dock. To the right of where we pull in is a freighter converted into the Last Drop Saloon, its cargo doors open. Even from here, we can hear the sounds of laughter, shouts and arguments, and tavern clamour. A general store glows with magical light. Citizens move between buildings with cautious efficiency. To the left rises a watchtower, made of ship salvage, sensors sweeping the area and catching our ship and the drow ship we have in tow.

We have arrived in the settlement of [[Shilear]]!

Otto looks to our group and says, "Well, team, that was a fine job we did back there. Let's keep it going for next time."

Regarding the prisoners, Otto asks Vayb if he wants to do an interrogation. Vayb says he wanted to hit the town, but he decides to help out anyway.

Vayb and Otto drag the captain out. Vayb smacks him over the head and asks what his name is. Bloodflow weakly repeats his title, says he works for the Void (somethings?). Vayb says "oh, right," because he had explained this before, but Vayb explains that he's gonna forget again later.

Eventually, the boys get him to tell them his boss works at a base nearby. Otto asks if he wants to return there—Bloodflow says he wants to, so he's ready to cooperate. Otto tells him that, in that case, he'll tell Otto all about his gang, how they operate in Shilear, and what he knows about the town and about one [[Shaw|General Shaw]]. And, if Otto is satisfied, we can discuss terms about his release to his base.

About Shaw(?), Bloodflow identifies him as "the fuckin' sheriff" who "kicked Blackeyes' ass". He showed up very recently, allegedly.
