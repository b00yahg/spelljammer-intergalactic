We step out into the swirling neon chaos of the Blue Moon Station. It's familiar—holographic ads, the hum of Spelljammer engines, and more. But we get something new: Downtime. Jack will be giving us our paycheque for the bounty shortly.

[[Darrett]] is overwhelmed by all the noise and lights. He sees a squid ship fly overhead—and asks if all the ships look like sea creatures. [[Claire]] says that they don't all, but most look like some sort of creatures. [[Vayb]] points out that some look like normal ships. Claire thinks it's boring. Vayb thinks it's useful for laying low—Claire concedes we could have used that on Krynn.

> \[!Claire Note]
> it really puts things into perspective seeing someone like darrett see all this for the first time. oh! that gives me an idea!

Claire tells Darrett that, tomorrow, she's going to take him to experience the joys and luxuries of space. She asks if he's ever been to the spa. He doesn't even know what that is. She says that's _perfect_. Walking ahead of the party a moment, Claire bumps into a giff wearing a green coat.

The man apologizes for running into her, but he was looking for us, so he asks if we're the members of the Ace Nebula. He, like the other giff, has a long list of titles. Essentially, though, he's the executor of Plimbitt's will. And [[Marshal Plimbitt|Plimbitt]] dedicated all of his remaining possessions to anyone who bested him in combat—we blew up most of his items, but we got anything he had on the [[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]].

That contained: 4 grenades, 2 magical schematics, a melee arcane amplifier, and a deed: the Starcade. We have a Bastion now!! Apparently, before getting into warmongering, Plimbitt tried to pursue a secondary passion: video games. It failed because he wasn't a good gamer, nor a good businessman. But the business remained in his name and transferred over to us.

We cut to the Starcade as Vayb throws open the doors so we can see it all at the same time.

The Starcade sits in an overlooked corner of the commercial district. The building sits beneath an unlit neon sign, whose tubes depict a Spelljammer flying over an arcade machine. The building is overgrown. A large metal door sits at the front of the building.

> \[!Claire Note]
> what a hunk of junk!

[[Otto]] asks if any of us have any, er... entrepreneurial spirit. He doesn't know what to do with this. The group concurs to some interest; [[D'Arby]] would just vibe with anything, Vayb thinks it could be useful. We quickly procure a lock for the doors for 10gp. D'Arby signs the deed.

The locksmith comments that the place is really dirty, but he has friends in the Refurbishing Guild, who can fix it up in a week for some price. D'Arby and him talk about getting the place furnished. D'Arby goes to sign the contract with this guy—Vayb steps in to check the contract (he can barely read). He tries to just get us some time to read over the contract. D'Arby says that the locksmith wouldn't steer him wrong, but passes his Insight check: he might be trying to steer us wrong.

Otto sideeyes Claire and [[Squall]]. They don't have much input. Vayb tries to emphasize that we're hotshot [[Aces]] and we can track him down if he fucks with us. The guy fesses up—if we sign, this guy and his boys get free video games for all time. We hesitate. That's it? D'Arby signs. He says that if they're there all the time they'll bring us the _best_ sofas. The guy agrees. They ABSOLUTELY will.

With our arcade plans settled, we move into the rest time!

Vayb asks Squall to spend some time with him, since they haven't really had the time since they left [[Athas]]—and they're gonna go to ~_the Casino_~. At Black's Jacks Casino, the pair are handed complimentary martinis, and Squall takes in the new surroundings. They're familiar with the concept of gambling but it's a very new environ for them.

The gold dragonborn attendant greets Vayb again, welcoming back his favourite Ace—Vayb says he's sure that he says that to everyone who comes in here. "Only the hot ones," replies the dragonborn. The dragonborn uses his breath to light up a crystal and start a wheel as the duo sits down to gamble, and says he's happy to get some games firing off at this table. Vayb telepathically suggests he use their winner status at the derby to get some attention.

Then, someone appears at the table, greeting Squall excitably—it's Sugar! (The plasmoid artificer from the rival gang of Aces) She asks if Squall "missed me?" Squall replies, "like a hemorrhoid." Then explains that they don't have hemorrhoids. Sugar asks what Vayb and Squall are up to here, sloshing over with a drink in hand. Squall asks if she can even drink—she can! She can incorporate it right into her slime, apparently.

Vayb and Squall buy 1000gp and 978gp worth of chips before starting the game. They settle down at the table, and the attendant sets up an antimagic shield. Squall bluescreens and drops to the ground. Vayb starts yelling at the casino attendant, who nervously apologizes, saying it was casino policy. Sugar jumps in and starts yelling at the man too—collectively, they convince the attendant to not put up any wards. Vayb telepathically says he respects her game and offers to cut her in, asking her what she's doing here—she tells him not to worry about it.

Over the games of poker, Squall triples their money, and Vayb loses half of his.

Squall approaches Sugar afterward about the bet we made with her, and almost gets taunted into a new bet.

Meanwhile, Otto sees Claire getting ready for a night out and becomes _very_ intrigued by the possibility of fame and sponsorship. As she launches into her vlog, Otto ends up trying to take the tablet and run with his own introduction to the video. Claire critiques it for being hammed up—in this kind of content, audiences want _authenticity_, people who feel like you're a friend not some movie star. He replies that he _is_ a star.

> \[!Claire Note]
> otto is a puzzle, honestly. he seems like such a self-righteous paladin, but he's lowkey a diva on the inside. i've gotta learn more about this guy

As they get into it, they explore audiences reactions to the team: they've sweet bean-ified Squall, discuss about whether or not Vayb and Claire are dating, people love D'Arby but some people are trying to investigate if he's worth canceling, and many audience members ask if Otto is single. They go out and try some food and other businesses around the market, show them off for the vlog, then talk to owners and stuff and try to make deals. Otto, for his part, just gets random people off the street and asks if they want to be famous.

We get a few contacts: Succlesups (a vendor of flavoured potions), InstantHe11per (because 1 + 1 is better than 1: a mental health delivery service that sends you a robot who gives you words of affirmation), and RiteWay (a beauty care product line) with a contact woman named Ashley. Also, [[Large Luigi]] seems interested!

Otto's efforts find a man named Tom: a "self-preneur", who sells invigoration supplements called Natural Wake. Otto thinks this is such a great product. Otto offers that, if he partners with us, this supplement will be a hit—not just on the Rock of Bral, but all the spheres! Otto pitches him a plan: advertise them as the official supplement of the Ocean Court Express, and if people take them, they'll be able to be Aces themselves. Tom asks if, hypothetically, he had 10000 cases to move, how would he do that?

Otto inspects the pills. He has no idea what they are. Nor does Claire. He starts pitching a particular distribution model: a Triangle of Success. Otto purchases 1000 units to distribute himself.

Claire meets with Luigi to discuss his idea. He pitches canned Laughing Beholder drinks and—inspired by his ideas—Claire pitches highly caffeinated energy drinks: LuiG-Fuel. Luigi makes the deal with one caveat: legally altering someone's name to Firstname LuiG-Fuel Lastname. Claire volunteers D'Arby. With the deal cut, we move onward!

> \[!Claire Note]
> yay, i can for sure use this profit to make sure my stay out here is extended!

D'Arby's first big activity is to go check out Meat's Silver Cart of Wonders! Claire, planning to do some magic item crafting, tags along to Gaspar's Reclamation. D'Arby approaches Meat, who has an earbud in and isn't paying much attention. Meat runs his cart right over a guy's foot as he rushes over to speak to D'Arby, who waved. Meat says he wasn't sure if he read the vibe right, but it looked like D'Arby was coming to him so he came to D'Arby faster.

As they begin talking, Meat briefly vanishes and freaks out D'Arby. He rushes back to the cart and D'Arby—apparently his Wild Magic teleported him. Meat greets Claire and the two guys ask what she's up to here: she's here to see Gaspar to work on some magic items. Meat says that Gaspar's a super nice guy. D'Arby says ohhhh, that's where this is! He's been looking for a bathroom for thirty minutes since he's all full of Icee. Meat says he gets that. He'll usually just pee himself when he needs to. D'Arby hesitates and says, "That's... I don't—I don't... think that's good, man."

Meat launches into a sales pitch about cheap, slightly useful magic items and his business model: where everything is 100 gold and you don't need to choose. They have a discussion about the choice to make everything cost 100 gold instead of those "99.99" stuff since it's so confusing. D'Arby comments that we got a ton of money from taking down that hippo, so he might as well. Meat says he thought about being an Ace but wouldn't be good about it. Claire tells him that her friend Otto is selling some supplements to help with that. D'Arby draws a Double or Nothing—a coin that can use an action to double or negate (50/50 odds) a declared effect.

Intrigued by this, Claire caves and rolls an item herself. She pulls: Krenko's Calling Card. This card can be used to summon a goblin for 1 hour or until it drops to 0 HP. As an action you can tap the card to make all goblins you control fall prone and stop moving for a turn. At the beginning of your next turn, your goblins multiply.

Over the week, Vayb, Otto, and Squall also go to check it out. Vayb gets a Ring of Kickass Fuckin' Flips. (Reminder to copy paste these descriptions here) Otto pulls the Pocket Spa. Squall pulls the Tome of Todd. And Vayb spent 100 gold on Darrett to get him a Gluttonous Fork.

After D'Arby and Claire buy their things, D'Arby says "Hey, thanks for... this. Whatever this is." to Meat, then asks about how he said he's always poor and never has money. D'Arby offers a possible job to Meat—maybe we could see about getting him a stall at the Starcade. Claire wanders off to go get her work done. D'Arby tells Meat that he's so lost and needs directions. He turns around to find that Meat is gone.

And we cut to Otto and Darrett doing some training! Otto brings him to the training space in the ship and asks for his assistance. Otto shows the space to him. Otto uses it most, but D'Arby also spends some time there. Otto insists upon Darrett how our work is dangerous, and it's important to be ready. He realizes Darrett is stuck in a salute, and tells him to be at ease. He inquires about Darrett's minimal training in Krynn. Darrett says he _was_ trained, but he does, like... know how to use a sword? Otto asks if he thinks a sword would be enough. Otto says that a scholar once said that a man can be a poet or a swordsman—and he liked Darrett's song, but it feels a little greedy for him to try to be both, so he's gonna run a test. He challenges Darrett to land a hit on him. Darrett stammers and says "what?" Otto says he doesn't judge the party for wanting to take him along but that Otto wants to make sure people aren't getting hurt. Darrett is confused and says that Otto is leaving himself open.

"Oh? Is that what you see?" Otto says, casting Shield of Faith and taking the dodge action. Darrett takes a swing but easily fails. He grabs his sword, and his early form is quite good—not perfect, not as good as Otto, but solid. It's a fencing form that Otto is generally familiar with. But, as Darrett starts his swing, Otto clocks his immediate loss of balance. The moment Otto and Darrett lock eyes, Darrett's fighting instinct is immediately overtaken by concern and fear. Doubt.

Otto steps around Darrett and knocks his weapon to the side, effortlessly. "Surely that's not the best your training has resulted in. Again." Darrett tries again. He swings wide and almost falls over

Otto teaches Darrett that we're putting our lives on the line—if the crew wants him to watch our backs, how can he expect us to trust him if he can't land a hit on someone who's not even trying to fight back. Darrett stammers and seems on the verge of tears. Otto tries to inspect what, precisely, is the issue here: if the block is mental or physical. It's _definitely_ mental. The two spar for a bit longer. Darrett gets increasingly frustrated. Otto kicks Darrett's feet out from under him. Darrett begs him for another chance. Otto says he's seen enough. Darrett stammers that he can do this, he's just having a bad day.

"I can do this, Becklinn!!"

And there it is. Otto says it's not his form, it's his mind. "What did your old commander do to you to make you like this?" Otto reaches to help Darrett up. Darrett takes his hand and says that she didn't do anything. Or she did... everything? He says she made him believe he can save the world. Ottos asks if he thinks he can. Darrett says he always did, but then you know what happened to her. What she did.

Otto leaves him with a bit of advice. That he's more than what Becklinn did, he's a good man, but responsible for himself. Otto reassures him that Becklinn's actions are not his, and that he'll have to keep moving forward under his own power. Darrett is quiet for a moment, and says that Otto wants to know if he can handle this—and that's fair, he is the captain. But the answer is that he can. He'll learn. But the truth is that he's terrified—that he wants to be part of this crew, and see the worlds, but there's something else in him too. Something else that is scared. That he's not gonna be able to fight with the vigor that we do. Scared that he'll be nothing but a poet. He says he thinks Otto deserves to know that before he makes any decisions. Otto gives a soft chuckle and says he isn't planning to make any decisions like that. He says that Darrett is made of good stuff—and not everyone can land a hit on Otto regardless. But Darrett needs to just keep working. Darrett asks if that means there will be more practice.

"Unless you have a problem with that."

"No, Captain!"

And they agree: drills, every dawn, until Darrett's nice and ready.

Cut to Vayb, at the Garden! Vayb goes to the greenhouse, which looks exactly as lively and green as when we were last there. Vayb pauses, on his way in, and puts out his cigarette before arriving, even if it was absently and recently lit. Sally isn't there today, nor is Doctor Hickory.

Vayb puts his hands in his pockets and just takes a peaceful stroll through the Garden. His psionic senses pick up the presence of an individual. Like someone is watching him. He can't see anyone. An arm suddenly GRABS at his waist.

He pulls away and sees a little kid! 5 years old. With a hand-knitted frog hat. Vayb cracks a smile as he pulls out of his defensive stance. The kid says "you're it!" and sticks out a forked tongue. He runs away, then is caught and scooped up by Hickory. Hickory greets Vayb, and the two chat a little. Vayb's mind drifts off as they exchange pleasantries. He's just taken aback by how cultivated and green and full of _life_ this place is, incomparable to the greatest gardens of Athas. He thinks of the ocean on Krynn and all the _**water**_ on that planet.

Then his mind flashes back to a horrible memory—of terrible confinement, his brain pulsating, almost _squirming_ inside of his head, until suddenly... he starts to hear a sound, of drumming machinery, and is in a place of darkness. He realizes he's trapped in this vision. Part of him wants to scream that this is over and that he got out! But part of him is... afraid. What if he didn't? What if he's still here. He screams.

Something answers his call—a creature with four tentacles. Massive tentacles, the size of Vayb himself. He tries to recoil from this being, as little as he is physically capable of. He's frozen in place as the tentacles slowly creep toward him. He sees the tentacles, their slime, their inkiness, the reflective void across them and he can see himself in them. Something touches him——

And a hand is placed on his shoulder in the Garden. He jumps in terror. He looks up and sees Sally. She apologizes if she startled him. He stares at her for a moment and then gathers himself. He pops his collar, regains his composure, searches for his cigarettes in his pocket—though he remembers where he is and puts it away. Sally inspects him, gets a grasp on what's going on, and tries to soothe him. She gently reaches her hand out and beckons for him to grab it.

She tells him to take a deep breath and says she's going to clear his mind, if that's okay. He says that's not okay. He thinks he doesn't want to be in his mind today. Then he realizes. He didn't get a flower. On Krynn. He sinks to the floor and starts to cry.

Sally reassures him that it's alright; there are lots of places with flowers. Vayb replies that there are lots of places without them. Sally says that's why they collect them here. She advises him to stay present in this moment. He's here in the Garden, and there are lots of flowers around him.

She offers him a present, and places something in his hand. It's a packet of seeds. She explains that, according to the packet, the flower has four petals and glows. She says she knows the job has us traveling a lot. He and our group are gonna see the universe. He won't always be able to bring back flowers. And that's okay. But, if he's willing, maybe we can plant them in places where there aren't.

Vayb fights back tears, grinding his teeth. He holds the flowers reverently, and forces himself to look at Sally. He breathes out a ragged breath. And nods.

"I'd like that. Thank you."

As he departs, Vayb feels something, present, squirming, lurking, in his mind. It's still there, but for now it slowly fades away. And Vayb is officially multiclassed in Warlock!!

He composes himself, leaves the Garden, and heads back to the ship, straight to his room. He places the gift somewhere safe.

And credits roll for tonight's interlude!
