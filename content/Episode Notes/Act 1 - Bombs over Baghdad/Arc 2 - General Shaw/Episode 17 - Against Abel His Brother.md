We set off into the skies above [[Shilear]], where 2NGEL's destroyer ship looms—a huge roving fortress of a Spelljammer that resembles a heavily-armored whale. As we make our approach, its mouth gapes and a metal pod flies toward us. It hails us.

[[Otto]] glances at the crew and makes a check in to see if we know the plan. [[Shaw]] pipes up that he's in the understanding that we're using him as bait. There's some hesitancy and uncertainty among us. Shaw mutters that he can't believe he's putting his life in our hands.

[[D'Arby]] says he doesn't know the plan, but he usually doesn't; he just stands there and kills when Otto tells him to. Otto says that's the right spirit. [[Vayb]] rolls his eyes, says "fuck you," and accepts the call from the probe.

On our feed, a visual comes up of a zombie, who asks if we have the prisoner. Otto replies that we do, and smacks Shaw both for show and the fun of it. The zombie replies that "Lord [[2NGEL]] awaits us". We spend a few moments riffing on this and making fun of the title. The zombie says that it just does what 2NGEL says. Otto says we're gonna have to start calling him Lord Captain.

> \[!Claire Note]
> zombie servant? lord 2ngel? can this guy get any edgier?

Otto tells the zombie that we'll meet on our ship—"our hostage, our ship". The zombie starts to disagree, but 2NGEL takes over, and allows it.

The Ocean Court docks on his destroyer, and we meet him face to face. He melodramatically monologues to Shaw, then tells the zombie to bring him to the brig. Then 2NGEL invites us to drinks to talk and share our reward.

Vayb strolls up and sucker punches Shaw—throwing an insult his way—and in so doing, he secretly hands a dagger to Shaw.

2NGEL takes us to a grand parlor room—it has a piano, a huge painting of space whales, and various other fancy furnishings. Some of us sit down, [[Claire]] goes toward the piano, and Vayb and [[Squall]] split up to lean on opposing walls.

2NGEL, instead of credits, drops a bag of gold on the table in twice the value of Shaw's bounty. He also tells us that we've done so well, too, that he wants to treat us—and opens a crate of weapons he has prepared for us. Squall gives the weapons the ol' artificer's once-over.

They inspect the guns, and 2NGEL asks if we want to try them out. Otto agrees, shrugging. 2NGEL gives a whistle to his undead servant, who brings in a red-headed young woman—one of the hostages, bound and gagged. 2NGEL puts an apple on her head. 2NGEL tells us this is one of his favourite games of skill—taking turns shooting targets off her head, taking more distance each time.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i. want. to kill this guy.

In spite of Otto's reassurances to us that he's got this, Claire interrupts and convinces 2NGEL to use a different target. Otto asks if she's gonna volunteer. Squall interjects and volunteers themself, with no hesitation.

2NGEL hands a rifle to Otto, who takes it, mimes lining up a shot—then takes the rifle, swings it like a baseball bat, and breaks it over the grand piano. The rifle shatters.

2NGEL, who clearly expects something out of us, is taken aback by this, but just kind of waits to see what the hell we're gonna do next. Otto cheekily says that his hand slipped, and "out of curiosity, are you a kind of dragonborn that can breathe in space?".

2NGEL cocks an eyebrow, and asks if Otto is threatening him. Otto replies that he's not doing so directly, but points out that we've "brought back more of your possessions than just the admiral". Otto asks Vayb what it's called again—and considering he means the bomb, Vayb objects to this telepathically, so he mentions that we have the datapad. But, he says, he doesn't wanna interrupt the merriment yet. Otto telepathically objects to this, so Vayb, Claire, and Squall pivot.

The three of them pressure him about how this is a NEW negotiation for whatever's on there—and whatever it means to him. 2NGEL strolls over to the wall, snaps his fingers, and a pistol appears in his hand. He says we're bold, and suggests maybe we can talk. But "enough of whatever the fuck this is".

Otto strolls into the chair 2NGEL was in, and talks business. He tells 2NGEL that we want information. 2NGEL concedes some ground and offers to answer questions.

We ask where he got the ship—he says he bought it from the Arcane, for way more that we make now. He used to bounty hunt back in the old days, when there was far more money going around.

Otto asks where the King is—2NGEL says "the mind flayer? Oh gosh, those fucks live a long time, so... that one's probably out there somewhere. Last I heard he was headed out to the Weird for something." He says they haven't talked since 2NGEL was still in the business, but that the King had some space taken up in the Crown Skull.

> \[!Claire Note]
> hm. this information is going to be useful at some point, i'm sure of it.

Squall asks how he got out of the business—he considers how to answer, and while he does Squall says something about "you know us robots, we stick to one thing forever and don't change it." He narrows his eyes in disgust about that. 2NGEL finishes his thought, though, and rants about how he hunted during the Inhuman War, how the Navy created WARRIORS and how he was the best of the best. But when the war was at its end, they MADE PEACE. Just accepted the surrender, and the recompense. He says that they gave him a sword, and put a crown on him for it, and then they tried to chain him and use that sword on him. He says that his removal from being an Ace went rough.

Vayb asks if he has beef with the Elvish Navy. 2NGEL scoffs and says "No! _No_. Not at all." He says the Navy fucked themselves over, but in doing what they did to him, they did what they had to to win, and he gets that. He has no beef with them. "That being said, they couldn't put me down." He says it's just business.

Claire says he's just some kind of relic of the old war—"so what does that have to do with Krynn? With [[Marshal Plimbitt|Plimbitt]]? What was the point of that?" 2NGEL explains that Krynn and Plimbitt was quick cash and space for study. But he has rackets _all over_ this galaxy. Some are important, but some are just part of the machine that is 2NGEL.

> \[!Claire Note]
> this guy thinks he's in charge. hah!

When we try to prompt him for more about the plan, he laughs and mocks us for, "what? Trying to bargain for the plan? WITH the plan?" But Claire and Vayb turn it around by pointing out it's not our fault he didn't get what he wanted out of his deal—that for us, it's "just business", to throw his verbiage back at him.

Claire wrangles the advantage back in our favour by saying that, to her, she doesn't actually care about HIS plan. Because at the end of the day, he doesn't seem like the brains behind his plan. She asks who [[L1CH]] is. Vayb then slips the dagger in by backing her up and using the name we found online: Theo.

2NGEL raises his eyebrows and says he hasn't heard that name since he left his home behind. L1CH, he explains, is an associate, who handles the tech of the operation. Vayb casts a subtle _detect thoughts_ and gets one clear surface thought "I'm gonna fuckin' kill her." To Claire's gut, he seems to believe what he says about L1CH.

He seems surprised that we found this information, and says that he's impressed with us finding this, since he thought L1CH scrubbed it along with everything else. He thought it "got wiped out along with my hometown."

Vayb asks about this, and he explains that his town was a rough place—the kind of place even a dragon wouldn't stay. His mother got out early, but he and his dad—a priest of Ilmater—stayed. Master Torvin ran the place, kept all the trade routes, the debts, everything. And the debts, when they couldn't be paid, well... he took things as collateral. Their children. Theo's dad, "bless his heart", took on everyone's debts for himself. He got sold into slave labour, basically, in the mines, for the debt he carried and forgave. His dad said the suffering brought him closer to Ilmater. When his dad eventually succumbed, he got out, and he became an Ace. He became _strong._

Returning to L1CH, he explains that she's a great technowizard, but that she's just that. Very very useful.

> \[!Claire Note]
> underestimate a technomancer, i freaking _dare_ you.

When discussing L1CH, Vayb did get a surface thought or two: that he's selling the gold he's mining here to her, and the connection of her to prison—and to salvation. When we mention the grand plan, he does have one thought about the goal: End the war.

Claire asks one last question of him: Do you think your dad would be disappointed in the person you've become?

And he looks her in the eyes and says "I know for a fucking _fact_ that he would be... And that's how I _know_ I'm on the right path. My dad was a fucking loser."

Vayb takes this moment of distraction to push deeper into his _detect thoughts_, trying to find L1CH. He sees an image of an individual, shrouded in darkness. Detail is hard to glean, but he can make out a skeletal mask with bright red eyes. She wears clothes of this era, baggy pants and a crop top. Behind the clothes, though, are tons and tons of wires. The wires embedded into the body are all plugged in to the technology around. There are no lights except the computer screens around her. Completely isolated.

She looks up.

And the image of her disappears.

Vayb pushes into Project Cain next: he sees a scene, a conversation between an Elvish official, 2NGEL, and L1CH. The elves doubt and are terrified by Project Cain. 2NGEL backs up his data and his idea. How something like this has NEVER existed before. He gives a speech about how this is the Elvish Navy's answer to the Scro—"do you think they never had any failures when they created their Beast?" The elf (Sgt. Pepper) belittles him, complaining about how impossible this is to cover up: "we even had to get Wildcard involved!" The elf then complains about L1CH's presence, and her "hacking stunts". 2NGEL defends her as part of the team. The elvish woman asks what to tell her superiors. L1CH speaks: "That you're closer to winning the war." Pepper gets frustrated and leaves the call.

2NGEL flips a chair in fury. L1CH tells him he's panicking, and reassures him about how nearly impossible it is to decrypt her coding language—2NGEL freaks out at the implication that it COULD be decrypted. She reassures him that the Eternal Codex is safe in (place?) in [[Lil' Avernus]].

> \[!Claire Note]
> and there's a lead, folks!

Then 2NGEL gets word that the "[[Aces]] are back with Shaw". This happened MOMENTS ago.

2NGEL then processes the realization that his thoughts were read: "What. _the fuck_ was _that_?" Vayb gives a shit-eating grin and says that we can trade information for information. 2NGEL narrows his eyes in fury, and Vayb can read his surface thoughts: he's going to try to kill us.

He draws his gun.

_**[[Lets Jam - Ship Combat Mechanics for D&D|Let's Jam]]!**_

Otto says that things seem to be getting too hot and heavy right now. In return for keeping his end of the bargain, we'll fulfil ours, and "just leave this here". "Sound kosher?" And he leaves a minor illusion of the tablet on the table.

2NGEL sees through the ruse and says that he'll get his "information for information." He uses his Action Surge. Nanobots cover his face from his hat, and it converts into a gas mask. He releases a red gas from his grenade, which bursts into the air. Everyone except Claire fails their saving throws.

Vayb attempts to kill Squall, Otto has enough wherewithal to Lay on Hands himself a cure.

He catches up to Claire, and the duo formulate a plan: Claire's gonna get the hostages, Otto will try to deal with this situation here—then we all get to the Ocean Court and drop the bomb.

Squall, on their turn, looks to Vayb and says "you couldn't fix me." Their face is a black mirror, with no emoticon. They slip into the mist and then disappear.

D'Arby has one hated target close to him, so his bloodlust, thankfully, is leveled at 2NGEL.

Unfortunately, 2NGEL is VERY skilled, and downs D'Arby before leaving. The Cain gas keeps its effect up, and though Claire passes, Vayb then fireballs her and Otto, dropping her unconscious. Otto retaliates against Vayb, nearly dropping him with a crit and smite. Squall emerges from the fog attacking Vayb, dropping him.

Vayb's fireball blows open the windows, though, and the gas clears from the air.

Otto, his face furious, gives one hit point to everyone. We then heal ourselves as best we can. Claire casts _locate object_ targeting the dagger Vayb gave to Shaw. Otto casts _find steed_. He grabs Claire (who D'Arby had scooped up in a fireman carry) and the two mount the horse.

While we make our way to Shaw, Claire uses the comms to have [[PIXIE|Pixie]] and [[Darrett]] prep the ship to go. And Vayb adds to not let anyone on—unless it's us. Squall notes that maybe it's best to shoot us if we're alone.

We find Shaw in a cell, and a bullet flies through the hall toward us. There is a keypad on the cell door, which can use a fingerprint scan or a password. We enter the name "Theo" and the doors open.

> \[!Claire Note]
> oh thank GOSH we found him

On our way out, Claire uses another _locate object_ on 2NGEL's hat, and we discover he's in the next room over. Full sprinting, rushing to escape, on horseback. 2NGEL pursues—on all fours, a draconic roar echoing through the halls of the ship.

Looking back at 2NGEL, Otto says "better luck next time," and tells him to "run away and heal up", casting _command_. He sniffles and winces at the magic, and the _command_ takes hold.

We arrive at the ship, and start taking off—Pixie having readied our engines, thrusters ready to burst.

Claire asks Vayb if we're dropping the bomb. He tells her to send it.

She keys into the bomb, and as she sets the timer, she feels something shifting. A system, previously dormant, rests in the bomb. A green skull appears in her mind. Ringing fills her mind. The whole thing freezes.

A familiar voice echoes. "Ugh, you know what? This is what he gets for making his password his actual name..." And we drop the bomb. Take to the air. A beat passes.

> \[!Claire Note]
> okay. yeah. this dynamic is going to be _interesting_ to pick at.

_**BOOM!!!**_

The bomb explodes in 2NGEL's ship.

Barely making it out of the ship, licking our wounds, Otto says it's a good thing he gave us the money already. (cue laugh track)

Then we turn to discuss what the plan is, with Shaw. We offer him the choice, but he agrees with Otto and Claire that leaving him on Shilear would be dangerous—for him _and_ for the town. He's willing to spend some time at the Starcade on the [[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]], but assures us he won't overstay his welcome.

But, getting Vayb's detected information out in the open, we realize that the Eternal Codex is in Lil' Avernus and that we might not have time to drop him off—2NGEL was about to go there, and we have a SHORT advantage while he recuperates from the bomb.
