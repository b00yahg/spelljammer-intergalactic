# Pixie's Guidance

### ATHAS

- Psionic-dense world, completely isolated from the wider cosmology. No spelljamming infrastructure, no portal network, no divine presence.
- Magic on [[Athas]] functions through "defiling," which drains life energy from the surrounding environment.
- **The vessel buried at Site Null is the only known entry/exit point to the planet's surface.**

### [[2NGEL]]: TRANSIT METHOD

- **2NGEL's ship maintains a stationary position in the Athasian upper void**, cloaked behind a sustained illusion field that mimics the local starfield. From the surface, it is invisible.
- To reach the surface, 2NGEL drops. He seals himself in a reinforced descent pod and falls through the atmosphere on a ballistic trajectory to a **fixed landing zone in the open wastes.**  The pod is shielded against thermal stress but is not magically propelled. It is, functionally, a rock with a man inside it. **From the landing point, the nearest settlement, the city of Balic, lies a couple of hours' march to the east.**
- **2NGEL's standard approach is to head west on foot, make contact with his intermediary, and proceed to Site Null from there under escort. The journey from the Silt Stalker ranging grounds to Site Null takes several hours through open desert.**

### SITE NULL: LOCATION

- **Crashed Illithid vessel buried approximately four meters beneath sand and shale in the southern scree wastes, southwest of Balic.**
- Open rocky badlands transitioning to deep sandy waste. No roads, no trade routes, no settlements within half a day's march in any direction.
- **The site sits within territory claimed by the Silt Stalker tribe**, a nomadic elven raiding clan split into three sub-clans: **Fire Bow, Fire Dagger, and Fire Sword.** They are hostile to outsiders but **will trade if the price is right.**

### Local Contact

- **2NGEL maintains an intermediary within the Fire Dagger clan, identified only as "the Runner.". As he travels into their territory to the west of the pod landing, the runner quickly finds him**
- **The Runner manages supply movements, initiates return psionic beacon, provides advance warning of Balican patrols, and ensures Silt Stalker raiding parties avoid Site Null.** Payment is in **water and obsidian.**
- **The Runner knows the exact route to Site Null and has made the trip dozens of times.**
- - 2NGEL uses a **spoken passphrase with the Runner** to confirm identity at the waystation contact point. Prior intercepts suggest **he rotates it on a lunar cycle tied to Athas's moon Ral.** **The Runner will not engage without it.**

### SITE NULL: Entry Points

- **Primary: Port-side hull breach.** Main access, partially reinforced with off-world resin compounds. Descends into the mid-deck.
- **Secondary: Ventilation shaft approximately 40 meters northeast of the main entry, concealed beneath a cairn of stacked scree stones.** Narrower. Leads directly to the lower deck near the biopools.

### 2NGEL: Status

- **He has missed a scheduled check-in as of this report. This is due to the capture of his ship by the Ocean Court Express.**

### Andropinis

- **Sorcerer-king of Balic**, 2NGEL's primary research partner on Athas. One of the most powerful beings on the planet.
- **Andropinis visits Site Null on a predictable schedule: every 7 local days, arriving at midmorning, departing before dusk the same day.** He does not stay overnight. **He does not deviate from this pattern.**
- **His arrival and departure are instantaneous. A portal manifestation has been observed at a fixed point near the primary entry, consistent with an arcane teleportation circle.** The origin point is presumed to be **somewhere within his palace complex in Balic**, though this has not been confirmed.
- He travels with a minimal escort — **two to four templars who remain at the surface entry point.** They do not enter the facility.
- **Communication with 2NGEL has lapsed.** Andropinis has not received a relay transmission in some time and **may assume the project is on hold.**
- **Whether he represents a potential asset or an additional threat depends entirely on how he is approached and what he is told.**

# Session Notes

We begin with a flashback sequence. [[Squall]] has to change their uniform.

Privately, in their room, they infuse a _bag of holding_ in order to retrieve their old armour from where they previously stored it in a pocket dimension. They slowly remove their robotic outer shell, replacing it with an old one—built with limited technology, before they knew about the world in the stars, created out of the stripped carapace of an ankheg. The chitin holds... memories.

[[Claire]] bangs on the door, telling Squall it's time to go. They open the door with their armour, a wobbling goop beneath the visor (made out of the bug's mouth), and then takes on Claire's face. She compliments the new makeover, but Squall points out it's more of an old look. Claire "empathizes" by pointing out that she wouldn't like wearing her own old outfits.

Then, they rejoin the group. [[Vayb]] silently judges Squall's choice to use Claire's face. As does [[Otto]].

Then, we cut back, with Claire's message: "We're comin' for ya, 2NGEL!"

The pod hurtles through the atmosphere, the inside turning dark as the outside of the pod catches fire. Metal groans, something in the wall cracks. The pod begins to break. Air jets inside, smoke begins to fill the air. Vayb guides us to a rough landing on the Athasian surface.

"Keep your breakfast in, man. Have you never flown before?" Otto ribs Vayb. Vayb pointedly ignores him while he opens the pod. Claire and [[Darrett]] laugh.

Athas reveals itself beyond us. A wooden post sits 60 yards from the pod, a body impaled to it, a creature consuming the corpse from within its ribcage. A silt drift covers a tipped-over wagon. The desert around is nearly silent.

Something moves way on the horizon, but it disappears before we can process it.

Vayb steps out of the pod, kneeling in the red dirt and sand, and grabbing some of it between his fingers. "Just as I remember it..."

The dark sun has risen above the horizon, but it's still fairly early in the morning. Vayb points in the direction of the sun and says "Balic's that way." He points elsewhere else and suggests we go see the elves. He asks what the group thinks.

Otto and Claire defer to him. [[D'Arby]] agrees with him, but gives him a look to see if he's doing alright. Squall says they don't want to go to Balic.

Vayb pulls a cigarette out and goes to light it... then realizes he'll have to defile to light it, so he puts it to the still-smoking hot pod and lights it there... where he finds that 2NGEL had a stockpile of water—enough for 2 full days worth for one person.

Vayb ensures everyone gets their waterskins and such ready, and distributes the stash. Notably, Claire does _not_ have a proper adventurer's waterskin: she has her bottle of boundless (iced) coffee for drinking—which is a lot less helpful on Athas.

Vayb gets the party moving, but hangs back a moment and takes a look in Balic's direction—his expression betraying that he's not quite sure if he's made the right choice sending us this way. D'Arby, the only one of us who notices, makes a point to give Vayb a solid pat on the shoulder at some point while we walk.

We go check out the impaled body first thing. It's leathery dry, carries 7 obsidian chips, and seems to have been killed by a knife—carving meat off of its bones. Darrett grimaces in horror.

Vayb identifies this person as probably having been killed by Silt Stalkers. Darrett asks about those. Vayb explains that they're people who live in the wastes, with a very harsh lifestyle.

Ahead, in the Sea of Silt, there's a thin gorge between walls of salt. As we cross, D'Arby spots people in cloth wrapping (mummies?) riding huge lizards—which he points out to Vayb. Vayb says he can talk to them. We approach, waving the blue side of Otto's cape to get their attention.

They catch the sight, snap their gaze right up towards us, and look at us with bright gold eyes.

Vayb calls out "peace!" in Elvish. They start shouting, asking "City dweller! What have you done?!"

Otto looks slowly at Vayb, then back at the rest of us.

As he does, there's a pressure beneath his feet... a hum. The silt begins to move. 20 yards ahead, a ripple of sand begins to move, the size of a small house. The salt cliff faces begin cracking. There's the scent of burning iron to the air.

The elves don't bother responding to us, dropping low and taking battle stances.

A huge creature emerges, a reptilian creature with loose skin that makes "wings". Powerful. At the very least, it bears a striking resemblance to a dragon. Drakes, as Squall and Vayb would call it.

We start to run.

The first of the elves vanishes. In his head while he tries to goad the drake, Otto gets a telepathic message from the elf, asking him what he thinks he's doing. "Watch and learn, friend!" He simply replies.

We continue running, trying to figure out who can take to the air.

Otto looks at the drake and says "I take it back, Vayb, you're only the _second_ ugliest creature on this planet!" He tries to goad the creature, and flips over it.

The elf asks if he intends to be some kind of "sacrificial lizard."

"I have no intention of dying here! My crew are simply soft of flesh."

"If you perish," the elf replies, "wave your cape for me so that I know where I might find your corpse to harvest your water."

Otto and Vayb distract the drake while the rest of the party flees, and eventually they _burst_ away on their flying magical gear and outrun it. We gather again as a group.

On the way back to the party, Vayb chastises Otto for "getting cute with it". Otto tells Vayb that he acts this way because it sets an example for the party: if he looks like he feels he has things under control, it makes the party feel more confident.

Vayb meets Otto's eyes, and concedes. "You're right. I have my own way of doing things too, and... with the rest of them aside, so they don't worry... I need you to know that this thing isn't coming out of my head. And I need to know that that's okay with you, because I don't want to lie to you. And maybe you had that thing under control, so I'm gonna need you to trust that I have this thing under control."

"Why do you need it so much?" Otto asks.

Vayb admits that maybe he _could_ try. That guy on [[Lil' Avernus]] said he could try.

Otto asks why he doesn't want to, then.

"It didn't choose to be there," Vayb says. "It... it feels like a part of me. Like... everything I went through, I went through had to be for something."

Otto is quiet for a moment, and stares off at the sky. He sighs. "Oh, Vayb. I don't relish being the one to tell you this, but... sometimes the horrors we face simply _are_ for nothing. We withstand them, and move on, and deal with the consequences. You still have the consequences within you, and you may have to deal with them later, but... you know where I stand." Otto then rejoins the group.

He's all smiles when he returns to the group. To Squall he says, "I thought the creatures here were supposed to be dangerous!"

Otto then feels a blade touch his neck. A whisper now, audibly: "Hands up, Defiler. Don't move."

Otto misty steps away.

The elves ask our business here, calling us sorcerers.

Claire says that we're here to kill a _much worse_ Defiler. "You're welcome," says Otto.

The elves accuse us of involving them in squabbles between the sorcerer kings.

We're not working with the kings, says Otto. He and Claire also insist that we're not sorcerers.

As I let the party engage with the elves, I (Clio, the notetaker) unfortunately have to leave, but the DM summed it up as follows:
