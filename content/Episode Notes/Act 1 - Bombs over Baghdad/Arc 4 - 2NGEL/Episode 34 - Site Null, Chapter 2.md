We open the session to an episode of Wildcard, which tells us of Dragon (now [[2NGEL]]) and Quickdraw at a big mission in a crashed nautiloid, bantering as they fight wave upon wave of intellect devourers. The Elder Brain, deep inside, wakes.

(GET THE PROSE FROM THE DM :3c)

We return, to [[Vayb]] and [[Otto]] watching the poor soul in the lab.

Vayb looks at it, thinks about lighting a cigarette, but decides not to defile. So, he leaves. Otto looks at him, trying to determine how he's feeling. Vayb wears a wry smile, but it doesn't reach his eyes.

"Why do I get the impression you know what this is, Vayb?"

"Oh, he was me."

They lock eyes. Vayb shrugs,

"What's a guy gotta do to get a light around here, huh?"

Vayb looks to [[Squall]] as he leaves Otto. They look to him, trepidatiously ask if he needs... then they trail off. They look visibly stressed.

The group readies to move on.

Meanwhile, Darrett's been organizing the bodies by the wall. [[Claire]] asks what he's doing; he explains that he's preparing a little funerary rite—("why?")—to guide their souls where they're meant to go. Claire asks why, and says that she assumes they're not going to somewhere nice. When [[Darrett]] asks why, she gestures to the ship, says "not somewhere nice", and shrugs. She turns to rejoin the others. Darrett nervously follows.

The squad moves on.

We inspect an odd apparatus able to fit a head in it. Squall determines it to be some sort of brain "scanner". Like a brain "printer" that they used to make Squall?

Leaving that behind, Otto and Vayb both voice that they were thinking of destroying the thing. But they ask Squall for their thoughts first. They're uncertain, so we simply depart.

We descend down some stairs to find a room flooded with a violet goo in a moderate layer. A single legionary is in the goo, wearing boots.

Otto says he thinks he should interrogate this man, as the only lone humanoid we've seen. He approaches. Through the interrogation, he determines that the guard is watching the ship's "engine". Its _heart_.

The group considers trying to liberate the legionary if possible. Vayb wants to bring him with us, Otto thinks it's far too much of a risk—but he's conflicted.
