This is an informal partial session ran so that our lovely Shmamy got some time to do his [[Vayb]] roleplay.

We begin by discussing what's been going on with the characters. Vayb's fucked off from the party a little, maybe tries to get to know Echo a bit—maybe play some video games with her and [[Darrett]] and make sure they're okay.

How many days was it before Echo decrypted the files? Well, she revealed things in waves. But, the files, which concern Vayb and [[Squall]], were decrypted about halfway through the week. Echo reveals the files to everyone via text. At that time, Vayb looks over them briefly but he doesn't really read so he doesn't pay much attention to them until the party can inform him. Though he's a little concerned about [[Athas]] being mentioned.

Squall and [[Claire]] are at Gaspar's as they work on some magic craftswork. They read through the files, and Claire slowly looks up toward Squall. They stand right up. They think about where Vayb told them he went—and remember that he said he's away gambling.

Claire stops Squall and asks what is going on, and if they're okay. Squall calmly says they're unsure, but they must find Vayb. Claire upcasts a _fly_ to get them there faster. They zoom right on past the greeter at Black's Jack Casino and find Vayb with a bottle in hand at a slot machine.

He seems unbothered, just having some fun. Sugar's behind his back, watching and chatting with a drink he bought her in her hand. She has her hand morphed into giant crossed fingers, cheering him on. Displacer beast... displacer beast... beholder. The pair curse.

They greet the suddenly-arrived casters. Vayb asks what's up. Sugar says "Not that I care, but should you be flying in here?" Claire says "yeah, yeah, doesn't matter. Buzz off." Squall's screen hides a little grin emoji.

Vayb tells her that "we'll pick this up later, babe." Squall's thoughts are derailed by "babe"...? Vayb shrugs, asks if there's something wrong with that. Squall replies that they can't forget that she hurt [[PIXIE|Pixie]]. Vayb blows some smoke and admits that he didn't know that. He agrees to talk to her about it and pats Squall on the shoulder.

Claire interrupts and tells him that we need to go back to the ship. He tells Sugar that he's gotta spend some time with his friends and invites them to gamble with him. She and Squall reiterate that we need to _go_. He asks what's got them so tense. Squall starts explaining more delicately, but then Claire shows him the page about himself—which he slowly reads. So we agree to go back to the ship.

At the ship, Vayb puts on the Spelljammer Helm asks Pixie to telepathically relay the information within the files to him mentally. Claire skims through it with the rapid processing of the Reality Chip. She paces the room impatiently and anxiously. Squall freezes and reads the information from their suit.

Vayb has been still in the helm, but forget the cigarette in his mouth, so it's slowly burning in his mouth. He's white knuckling the helm's chair.

The cockpit is quiet except for the routine sounds of Squall checking in on Vayb's vitals through Pixie—and Claire slurping iced coffee through her boundless bottle.

Finally, we finish reading. Vayb gets disconnected from the Spelljammer. He shouts in pain as he realizes the hot cigarette has fallen into his lap. He punches the console, swears, and crashes out a bit. Squall reaches out, stops themself, and then approaches—kinda just hovers around Vayb.

Vayb pulls out another cigarette, fumbles a couple onto the floor. Then he notices Squall. He starts, then pulls them into a hug—he's tearing up. He's not in control, though, and drops his freshly-lit cigarette.

Squall asks him if he saw the biggest implication in the notes. Vayb just apologizes to them for what happened. He slumps down into the seat.

"You were part of my team. I got you caught in this. I took your life from you."

Squall replies that they're not the same person, and he gave them life in this one. That person is long-dead, and them being a machine is not his fault; it's Andropinis'.

Squall says this isn't what they realized. "Vayborexes," they say, their voice finally breaking. "We have to go home." They exit their suit, and hug him. "I don't want to go home."

He agrees. They never wanted to go home again. Vayb punches the table again. "He's a fucking monster... but he has to be stopped." The two express their fear about what's going to happen.

Claire interrupts, telling them that they've gotten really strong. And if they're going back to Athas, we're going back with them. As a team.

Vayb agrees. "We're a kick-ass team." It's just that... "you don't know what it's like there, Claire."

Claire, the Ainsworth, almost gets offended—but as her experience has helped her grow... she pushes down the offense. Additionally, the Chip, in the back of her mind, fires synapses. Information. She sees bright yellow. Rocks, stretching up into the sky. A burning desert, one she can almost feel it, the sky sizzling the skin. Then, she sees a void. A pit of darkness, despair, and radiation itself. A dark sun which burns the land, like a black hole sucking up a dying earth.

The burning is what lingers. Claire flinches, like she's been hit. Squall's defensive energy takes over, and they ask Claire what's going on. She explains that she felt it. The Chip knew, for a moment.

"Well, then you can imagine a lifetime in that place," Vayb says. "Imagine that." Then he begins t o tear up further. "With psychopaths like him. My own fucking mother—" He breaks. "My own fucking mother, sold me for doing the right thing."

Squall replies that the three of us can all understand horrible parents. This gets a laugh from Vayb. Claire tells Vayb, earnestly, that she's "so sorry..."

He replies that he doesn't want her pity. Squall reacts, shocked. Then he says that he appreciates it anyway.

The two comfort each other... agreeing that they have each other's backs. That they're partners—brothers.

The three discuss what the files mean. What to do. Claire insists that we have to stop them. Vayb's point of view is more nihilistic, beaten down. But Squall agrees, that Vayb's team might also still be there. Vayb wants to agree, but he has no idea what they're capable of. What Andropinis can do—that he was worshipped like a god, that his power isn't a charade.

They talk about what little they do know of Andropinis. They saw him teleport. He broke their minds without even breaking a sweat. But Vayb doesn't know much. Nothing that will be useful for us. Claire says she knows.

He and Squall open up about how much they can't forget him. About how sometimes Vayb still thinks he's there. How Squall feels him when they fight.

At the shop in [[Lil' Avernus]], Vayb says, they scanned his brain. Andropinis wrote in his brain. Put magic in there. Powerful magic, to bind the thing—the tadpole—in there. "He could probably just flick his finger and—" Squall finishes the thought, that Andropinis could reverse it, just ceremorphosize him immediately. Vayb says he doubts he could be of any use to us.

Squall says that they know Vayb is strong—and that they wager that when Andropinis flicks his fingers, Vayb never goes down without a fight and maybe he controls the tadpole. Claire says she'll do some research. She's sure that someone knows something that can help.

Vayb and Squall talk, agreeing that they _have_ to go back to do the right thing and deal with [[2NGEL]], no matter how much they want to leave it behind. Vayb is just genuinely really unsure of whether he can do anything against Andropinis.

Claire relays a family saying: "Either you think you can, or you think you can't. Either way, you're right." Squall relates to a different saying from their father: "Don't think, just do," but they agree it's not quite the same.

Then, Darrett enters with a chipper expression. He says he got a message from Pixie and asks if he can read it—then sees the mood in the room. Claire plans to fill him in later.

Vayb says he's gonna go on a walk. Squall asks if he needs them. He says he doesn't _need_ anything from them, and reassures them about the stuff going on and how he'll be okay. But he'd be happy for the company of a friend.

Claire interrupts and asks if she can post the files. Vayb thinks solemnly, asks he to scrub their names and not implicate our crew, but agrees that the world has to know about 2NGEL. He thanks Squall and Claire, then gives them both a hug. He tells Squall that they're beautiful in this form.

Then he heads out of the ship, lights up a cigarette, and walks out in to the neon-lit streets of the [[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]].

After he's left, Darrett asks if everything is okay. Claire and Squall fill him in—about small concerns about [[Otto]]. He says that, if you've seen the stars, one little planet can't be a problem. Claire grins, punches his shoulder, and says "that's the spirit." Squall moves to give him a hug, then questions whether he'd like a goop hug. Darrett replies that Squall isn't stranger than anything else he's seen these days.

Darrett says that it looks to him that this means 2NGEL has no where else to hide. We can go to Athas, and that we'll "fuckin' kill 'im." Squall replies that they'd like the same, but we should defer to captain's orders. Squall can see death in his eyes as he says this.

Darrett says he can leave. Squall and Claire encourage him to stay if he wants. Besides, says Squall, "I'm quite fond of your company."

Squall asks how Darrett would feel about going back to Krynn. He's unsure, he admits, but he's grown a lot and seen a lot. He'd certainly be comforted knowing he could leave when he wanted. And he could maybe beat the shit out of Becklinn now, and maybe that would feel good.

Squall is glad he's so confident, but admits that Athas... might be worse than Krynn. It's "less Becklinn," Claire says, "and more a dragon."

Darrett points out that we did kill a dragon the first time he met us. Squall says it did cost us Claire (much to her discomfort). Darrett says she came back.

Squall asks if she thinks she could take a dragon now. She stretches, cracks her knuckles and answers with a response that Squall can tell is "programmed": "An Ainsworth never loses twice."

"You know," Squall says, "we're more alike than I thought." Claire's surprised, but the two do agree that they're both sort of flesh and sort of computer now.

Claire then departs, heads off toward the ship's liquor cabinet. As she pulls it open, her flip phone rings: it's her father.

She pauses, frozen. Swallows. Then...

Declines.

She pours a shot, throws it back, then does a lil jog-in-place dance to ready herself. "Let's do this." She heads out into the Rock of Bral to prepare for 2NGEL.

Her father leaves her a text message. A congratulations on her most viral post yet.

And we end our little RP here.
