At the end of last session, we discovered that [[2NGEL]] was pumping Project Cain into the cathedral called the Church of the First, where the peace delegations between the elves and the Scro were being held.

Through the portal we can make out cool light, not of [[Athas]].

As we breathe and prepare to enter...

The DM asks us a question. What do you fight for?

[[Vayb]] has his Deck of Many Things in hand, riffling the deck idly.

[[Squall]] steps through the portal readily. They fight because they have to. Because others can't, and they can't. Because they were made for it. But... deep down, they're with a group of people they trust to do the right thing. And it's fighting for them, and for that.

[[D'Arby]] steps forward too. He fights for all the people who don't have whys—who would not have a choice BUT to fight if this were to happen. For them, he steps through.

[[Claire]] pulls her camera into selfie mode, poses, and says, "This is it, guys! For all the marbles!" (Finish this!) If she were asked why she fights, why she's an Ace, she would answer that it "makes a great story." And this is not untrue, but deeper down, on a level she's not yet conscious of, it's about a certain kind of story. In this world, there is a lot of noise and bullshit and manipulation and exploitation... and what Claire fights for is the _truth_.

Vayb finally moves. He's been working through a lot of thoughts, deep down in the back of his mind, about this. About coming back. Lately? It seems the answer is for freedom. 2NGEL and Andropinis... they take. For their own gain, they steal the freedoms of others. Of himself. And he'll ensure it won't happen to someone else. But deeper down, this is also... what he wants to _do_. After trying to run away from this place for so long, there's nowhere else he'd rather be right now. Fighting for what's right here, with his friends.

[[Otto]] steps through the portal because this is his _purpose_. This is the culmination of everything he's grown for all his life and he cannot conceive of doing things any other way.

[[Darrett]] joins us, and we emerge on the other side of the gate. Beyond an archway, we know that 2NGEL awaits.

Otto looks over the crew, thinking about how they matter so much to him now. That they've taken up the place of a different pillar in his mind. "I have full confidence in all of you, but in the event that things go wrong... it has been a pleasure." Claire tells him to save the sad stuff for when we're done. He replies that moroseness is always best now, before the fire and lightning. Claire concedes that, and says that afterwards will be time for partying. Otto agrees.

Squall says they don't see a world in which we don't win. It's 6-on-1, and we have something he doesn't. And as Darrett begins to finish their thought by saying something like "family" or "the power of friendship", Squall says "we pissed in the Project Cain goop."

We walk through the arch into the cathedral. The pews have been broken by roots forced up through the stone, cracking them. Ahead, a pale light resolves into a window of white fire. A symbol glows there, in the shape of a 3-pointed star.

There are people—corpses—in the pews. From all across the Sphere: soldiers from Krynn, devils from [[Lil' Avernus]], elves.

We look to where the roots lead. They reach to an altar, with a candle atop it. The roots, as well as the black tentacles of a nautiloid, wrap around it.

The tentacles, Otto determines, is the endpoint of the tentacle pumps of the Project Cain goop. And the star, it's the symbol of the Juna. The beam of light is holy protective magic, but constrained. Then, tearing through the quiet...

**Bang!**

A gunshot tears through the cathedral, sparking against Otto's armour. 2NGEL hops down out of his hiding spot. He attacks with a surprise round.

_**Let's. Fucking. Jam.**_

2NGEL takes another shot with his gun, missing again. One last shot, finally hitting Otto in the shoulder. Then he tosses the gun aside, draws his blade, and charges forward.

"Where's the foreplay?" Otto asks, disappointed.

2NGEL glares at Otto, the look on his face clearly communicating that he is finally _done_. No more talking.

We begin with the Cup Ramen Gambit: Claire casts _sickening radiance_ atop 2NGEL (and unfortunately D'Arby), pulling a sun out of digital space and taking a selfie.

Otto's eyes glow gold and runes light up across his armour. A pulse of golden light explodes out from him, blessing D'Arby and Squall. And some of those same runes appear on 2NGEL. He hovers in the air dramatically.

2NGEL burns a Legendary Resistance when he fails a saving throw. He turns to D'Arby, speaking for the first time. "I'm pretty fuckin' sick of your party messin' with my plans." He attacks, then uses movement to rush toward Claire.

Darrett panics momentarily, then runs forward. He doesn't charge 2NGEL, though. Instead, he helps Claire by casting _invisibility_ on her.

Squall continues the Cup Ramen gambit and casts a _heat metal_ spell on 2NGEL's gun.

(Not taking down the turn by turn, but we engage in a fun combat back and forth until...)

As D'Arby wails on 2NGEL with his nunchucks, we hear a _crack!_ noise. 2NGEL has (black tentacles??) come out of his body.

He yells at the bodies in the pews, which come to life. "DO NOT LET MY WORK GO UNFINISHED" He commands them to start doing something to the trees.

We have 2NGEL under a Hold Person spell, and Otto lays into the paralyzed bounty hunter for critical Divine Smites.

We chew through ALL OF HIS HIT POINTS before he can take a single action in Phase 2. Ahahahahahahahahaaaaa

Project Cain fills the air as he waves his hands and activates the nautiloid pumps. We have our masks to protect ourselves... but 2NGEL inhales deep lungfuls of it.

As we fight, a voice echoes through our comms—in little bits and pieces, but nothing clear. [[PIXIE|Pixie]], radioing us. She says something about. Athas-- flying-- fast as-- can. Atop the altar, there is a communications jammer. But, we get bits and pieces, and know that the Ocean Court Express is nearby.

Claire destroys the last remaining undead and hacks the communications blocker, turning it off. Pixie connects and says she's here. She says she can't really help with combat unless she were to blow all of us up with 2NGEL and the church. But... she can fulfil a different request.

She tells Claire to look at her tablet, where there is a request: WILDCARD would like to share your broadcast. She accepts. Jack's voice echoes through the cathedral, infuriating 2NGEL. The booing toward 2NGEL and the applause about us grants advantage on our next d20 rolls.

"Smile, captain!" Says Pixie to Otto. "You're on camera!"

"Good thing I'm already shining."

Otto then considers that 2NGEL is empowering himself with the poison that is Project Cain. So Otto steps forward, uses Lay on Hands, and _heals_ the poison. The focused aggression fades somewhat, and there is...

Fear in 2NGEL's eyes, for once.

We hound on him until he starts to scream and writhe, yelling in terror. He presses on something and there's a beep noise.

Jack apologizes to viewers, saying there's some sort of disturbance in the broadcast.

A voice comes from the broadcast. "Sorry folks! I know we're all excited to see this guy get beaten to a pulp... but we interrupt this broadcast with a message from a very special sponsor!"

"[[H4G]]??" Says 2NGEL. "I knew it! Let's get these jackasses and--"

"Oh, we're not here for you. You just smile for the camera while they take you in and... try to choke on your blood while you're taken in—the people eat that part up."

Something (magical?) pulls 2NGEL away from us—a green glow in the tech in his outfit.

"What the hell? I want to talk to [[L1CH]] now!"

"Alright, alright. If you insist, man. I'm not here to hog the spotlight, I'm just emceeing this little shindig here."

H4G then introduces for the audience: the one, the only... L1CH!

Otto steps forward and tells H4G that it seems like he IS trying to hog the spotlight—but without showing himself. "Who is this?" He asks.

L1CH starts talking, introducing herself to the audience. "Unlike the man before you, I did not set out to become this. It's something that has evolved. As these things do."

There's a ring on our phones and communication devices. A simple text that appears and says "Not yet."

A shimmer appears in front of 2NGEL. A shield wraps around him.

"Some of you who have dug in the Weave know my connection to this man. I will not pretend that away. 2NGEL is my experiment. A thesis made flesh for the world to see. I found this man with nothing and I gave him everything, then watched what he built with it. He became a monster. A waste." She explains that he is a natural product of this world. How he ignited wars people barely cared about. In a world like this, she says. He was inevitable.

"He is this world's violence, incarnate."

She says the war could have ended with a long grind. The Scro have the resources. The Navy has the time. But she doesn't have that time, so she created him. Held up a mirror to the world. And now he's fallen to it. She ended the war.

She leaves the audience with a message: do not forget it, do not swallow it. Remember. Speak, and listen for it. Some people who share her resentment for him might tell you you can break the chains. But her truth is this: you must remove the arm.

Then she turns to Theo. She chastises him for having all the tools in the world in front of him, and all he did was make a bomb. He did not fail in some grand, epic way. He failed in a million small ones. He just failed. "You had every opportunity to succeed, but you didn't."

Then she addresses us, telling us that she doesn't intend to steal our victory against us. That we have every right to end this. She will let us handle 2NGEL.

Otto steps forward. "You mistake nihilism for wisdom."

He exchanges words with H4G and L1CH briefly.

We surround 2NGEL.

We discuss what to do.

Claire turns to Vayb and Squall.

Otto endorses killing him, or keeping him alive for information.

Squall looks at him and says that they've dreamt of this for far too long to pass up on the opportunity. With 2NGEL's own gun, they shoot him in the head.

There is fury, a reaction from the crowd. Soldiers march in, speakers blare thunderous applause.

Jack talks us up to the audience, wondering what we even call the five of us now that there's no Joker. (Claire hijacks the broadcast saying that _surely_ the five of us are the Royal Suite.) As Jack reads off other news we learn about: a missing person search for Echo, [[Shilear]] opening its doors to refugees, other changes left in our wake.

And...

Wildcard is going on hiatus, as they're retooling their image. But it gives the audience, they say, to regroup, heal, visit family, deal with unfinished business, and "fuck it, start a hobby!".

We see visions of the party. (Ask the DM for these?)

And that brings us to the...

_**End of Act One!**_
