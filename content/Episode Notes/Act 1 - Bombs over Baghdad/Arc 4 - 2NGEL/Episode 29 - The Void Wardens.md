## Notes

The Ocean Court drifts through the [[The Phlogiston|Phlogiston]]. We sail across the stars of the rainbow sea.

[[PIXIE|Pixie]] speaks to the team: "Approaching uncharted territory soon. Estimated arrival time: 2 hours."

While we're getting ready, [[D'Arby]] approaches [[Claire]], finding her dyeing her hair blue. He awkwardly suggests that maybe we need to be careful about keeping an eye on our party whenever it's split. She agrees, and they quickly discuss who's best to watch who: Claire will make sure she always has eyes on [[Vayb]] and D'Arby will make sure he sticks around [[Otto]].

On the way out, D'Arby asks Claire why she's using a spell to dry her hair—since we have hair dryers. "Where's your wizardly showmanship?" she asks. D'Arby mutters "wizards..." and leaves. Claire rolls her eyes and murmurs "fighters..." under her breath.

Claire then departs, plops k1k1 on Squall's shoulder and says they're gonna be watching the digital ferret. [[Squall]] asks if there's a reason. "Yes," says Claire. She refuses to elaborate, and leaves. Then she settles down in the cockpit.

Meanwhile, Otto retreats to his room to read a secret transmission from his bosses. The transmission tells him that now is the time to open a briefing file he hasn't been allowed to see yet. The code is 34-86-91-BLOCKAGE. Otto removes a false bottom from his drawer and procures a device. He inputs the code.

It says the message will only be displayed once:

### Message

SITUATION

#### 1.1 Background

The CRIMSON SPHERE is a sealed crystal sphere located far off established space lanes. It is one of several illithid containment sites tracked by the Intelligence Directorate as part of routine aberration monitoring across the Known Spheres. Approx. 2,000 years ago, illithid forces (referred to locally as "the Invaders") penetrated the Spherewall during a period of large-scale warfare on the sphere's primary world. They arrived with fleet-strength assets and colonization infrastructure. Local defenders engaged and contained the threat but were unable to eliminate it.

#### 1.2 The Seal

Prior to the invasion, a local warlord-sorcerer designated RAJAAT had anticipated external threats to his sphere. RAJAAT appointed twelve military commanders designated VOID WARDENS, equipped them with ziggurat-class capital ships, and tasked them with sphere defense. The VOID WARDENS waged a centuries-long campaign against the Invaders. When their defensive posture became untenable, they executed a denial operation: creation of a dead zone designated the BLACK GULF between the inner system and the Spherewall. This severed Invader supply lines and reinforcement corridors. Side effect: the operation sealed the sphere entirely. All parties, including the VOID WARDENS themselves, were locked inside. No natural portals have formed on the Spherewall since. The seal remains intact as of last assessment.

#### 1.3 Current Status

- VOID WARDENS remain active. They continue counter-Invader operations and maintain armed patrols at known vulnerability points on the Spherewall exterior.
- Surviving illithid elements are assessed as diminished but persistent. They operate from dispersed positions in the sphere's outer voids using primitive bone-and-chitin craft.
- Illithid attempts to establish footholds on the sphere's moons have been repelled by VOID WARDEN forces on multiple occasions.
- The situation has not required Order intervention to date. Simply monitoring

#### 1.4 Unconfirmed Reporting

Fragmentary intelligence (CONFIDENCE: LOW-MODERATE) suggests a crashed illithid vessel may exist on the surface of the sphere's primary world. The Order cannot confirm whether the vessel's biological systems remain active.

OPERATIONAL GUIDANCE
The following hazards are documented for operations inside the CRIMSON SPHERE:

- **METAL DEGRADATION.** Passage through the Spherewall via portal or physical interaction with the sphere carries a known risk of transmuting metal objects to stone.
- **NO COMPATIBLE REPAIR FACILITIES.** Ships from outside the sphere are virtually unknown inside. If your vessel sustains damage, assume no repair capability exists within the sphere.
- **HELM DISRUPTION NEAR PRIMARY WORLD.** The primary world's planar isolation causes spelljamming helms to lose functionality in the upper atmosphere. Orbital operations are viable. Powered descent is not. Any surface insertion requires a non-spelljamming method.
- **PSIONIC SATURATION.** The primary world and surrounding wildspace are saturated with ambient psionic energy. Crew members with psychic sensitivity should be briefed on potential interference. Natives to [[Athas]] are assumed to have countermeasures naturally inate inside of them.

STANDING DIRECTIVE
**Under no circumstances are the illithid elements inside the CRIMSON SPHERE to be allowed to breach containment.** The seal must hold. If your operations inside the sphere compromise containment in any way, the Order considers that a failure of mission. Any actionable intelligence gathered regarding illithid activity, the status of the crashed vessel, or any party exploiting illithid technology is to be relayed to the Intelligence Directorate at the earliest secure opportunity.

COMMANDER'S NOTE
The Order trusts your judgment, Brother Otto. Complete your mission. Make certain that when you leave, the seal holds. Nothing gets out.

## Notes, Cont'd.

Otto asks Pixie to broadcast a message: "Everyone gather around, it's time to discuss our plan."

Otto glances at Claire's stuff set up for painting her nails and tells her that we need that space for a more important discussion. She rolls her eyes and says he's being presumptuous, but gathers her stuff. Squall wanders in, having prepared little snacks that they're feeding to k1k1.

"Helpin' good people not enough for you?" D'Arby asks, oddly earnestly.

The topic turns to Darrett's computer terminal—and suggests we take that away from him. Darrett points out that that's our pinnacle of modern technology, isn't it? We discuss disguises (which will be done off-camera), and not openly carrying metal. Claire says that important metal implements go in the Bag of Holding.

Then, the Phlogiston goes grey. A strange hum ripples across the ship, up through our legs. We see a dark, red crystal sphere ahead of us. We see shadows moving, sliding along the curve of the wall. Too angular to be natural, too still to be ships in motion. Our lights flicker.

When they come back, the sphere wall seems to have stopped moving. The shadows are gone.

Vayb scans the surroundings. He sees that humanoid (larger than humans) beings that have grabbed our ship with gravity. Vayb calls to Otto: "We've got company!" And he angles the ship away as best he can.

Pixie identifies their location. Otto confirms they're not a ship or something we can technologically hail. Vayb reaches out psionically, but his telepathy's range is too short. But he senses a connection form anyway—coming from outside.

We all get this connection, except D'Arby. We hear a ringing in our ears, and a voice echo: "What strange wyrmlings have entered into the protector's zone?"

Otto replies, saying that these wyrmlings are the Ocean Court Express—and that he presumes we speak to the Void Wardens? The beings point out that the shark is metal, not living. Otto replies that it is in fact 100%, genuine metal. And that we're inside of the ship.

The Void Wardens manifest in front of the ship: eyes emerging out of nothing first, then gather as magical, glowing humanoids made of energy. Their wings seem to wrap around the ship. Inside the light, there is a chrysalis in the core of the projection. Inside, there are humanoid bodies.

Vayb stops the ship.

We discuss entry. Their goal is to keep power out, and keep power in.

Focusing on them, a few of us notice _cracks_ in the chrysalises. We realize that 2NGEL must have attacked them for entrance.

Otto entreats their sense of responsibility. He says their goal is to prevent power from entering or leaving, but their duty here prevents them entering and excising it themselves. That we're here to help. Otto names the threat they watch: the illithids. They agree.

"We're here to help you do your job," Vayb adds.

As for whether they can trust us—as they waffle on this—Otto tells them to take one look at what he wears. To see what that identifies about him.

The Wardens agree, and venture in to read our minds.

Vayb casts _intellect fortress_ to hide his illithid connections. The others attempt to willingly let them in. Except D'Arby, who keeps his _ring of mind shielding_ on.

Claire—not even wholly intentionally—thinks of Project Cain. What we know, what it felt like to fall victim to it.

Squall's only thought is: "I don't want to go back I don't want to go back I don't want to go back I don't want to go back."

Vayb forcefully projects his desire to find and kick 2NGEL's ass.

The Void Wardens acknowledge that we fight for a different cause—the invasion of a toxin, not of the flayers. They describe what they saw.

Otto what he does best: pushes our luck—asking them to help. That inaction is complicity.

They will grant us the ability to remove the foreign toxin, but when we're done we will leave and take all of our kind with us.

We need to fire our magic into the monolith, as precise as we can make it.

It doesn't look alive like any other worlds we've seen. No blue, green, white. Just... rust. Mountain ranges claw upwards. In the atmosphere, we see the Merkabah, hovering.
