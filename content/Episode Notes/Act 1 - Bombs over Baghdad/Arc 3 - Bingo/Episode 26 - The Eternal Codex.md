We begin our session flashing back to a scene our paladin and fighter didn't get to have. The camera returns back to the Ocean Court Express, where D'Arby's been summoned to talk to [[Otto]].

Otto says he hopes he didn't take [[D'Arby]] away from anything important. D'Arby replies that, nah, he was just doing laundry. The two discuss the difficulties of washing the scene of dragonfire and the (presumably) blood of illithids out of their clothes.

In any case, Otto explains, he wanted to ask D'Arby about something. He displays a clip of our fight with [[2NGEL]]—as Pixie's been recording from our helmets. Otto presents a specific moment in the fight, and asks D'Arby for his opinions on 2NGEL's fighting.

The two agree that they're concerned about the ease with which 2NGEL fights. How he moves faster than most would with that huge glaive—evidence of extensive practice. Ordinarily, Otto explains, he'd try to use the bulk against them, but his planning and simulations aren't quite working. He asks D'Arby how D'Arby would prep.

D'Arby replies that he doesn't really think this is the kinda guy we can't fight fair. "I don't think he was even taking us seriously. He's got all these tricks and doodads." D'Arby says he's not really a prepper, so he's not sure. Rely on [[Vayb]] and Claire's magic, maybe?

Otto agrees, but says there's only so far you can plan on relying on others for. He's trying to plan assuming the least; that it's just him and 2NGEL. But 2NGEL is just a mortal, so there has to be a hole in here somewhere.

What Otto's missing, he surmises, is practicing against true lethal intent. He closes his eyes, inhales, and opens them again. They're glowing gold. Combat is about the flow of energy and there's a certain force that he doesn't have the ability to work with.

But, if D'Arby studies 2NGEL's moves, and attacks Otto in earnest... the two can work on trying to counter 2NGEL together. For the past few nights, they've been practicing together like this, in quiet.

Then, we cut back.

The DM monologues, reading a letter, signed by the Curator.

```
To Sir Aldric Vane,
I have received your petition.
I have read it in full. I have considered its terms.
The Argent Order has long held my attention. Perched in your silver towers at the edge of everything, you have stood sentinel against the writhing dark. Gods of Protection speak them with something more. You have my respect.

Mankind was made for a thousand purposes. I, only for one. When the spheres were young and separate, so too were the gods. Isolated. Fragmented. Then the Juna sailed the stars. Then the elves. Then mankind, spreading like fever through the void. And it became clear—painfully, inevitably clear—that unity was required. The pantheons that once stood alone now move as one hierarchy. One design. Every angel in every sphere serves a single function within a pattern so vast your mind would break perceiving its edges. We do not choose purpose. We are purpose. One thread in an infinite weave. Immutable. Unbreakable. No angel deviates. No angel can deviate. This is perfection.
Mankind cannot participate in such design.

They cannot fathom that this hunger—this grasping, endless reach for control—is the flaw stitched into their very making. You have fought well, Sir Vane.
Bled well.
Now rest. The greater design will continue without you.
You cannot live in the world and be its protector. You are the world. I am its lock.
```

Then, we return to where we left off.

Otto decides to go ask the owls to direct him to where he'd need to go to help with solving the attempted break-in. The pair say they should go see the Curator. Otto replies that the Curator would be there and he wants us to help.

Otto keeps going, doubling down and wielding his charm with Squall's _guidance_ to convince them that the Curator contracted us to help with security and we're auditing and need to make sure that defenses can go to all the wings quickly in the case of a breach.

After giving us our directions, the owls wish us luck and go back to eating.

Claire asks how that even worked. [[Squall]] replies that Otto is a great liar. Which he gets miffed by, saying his actions are rooted in honesty and justice so no matter what he does it's not a lie. Otto grins, clearly not taking himself seriously there.

However, we pass an Empyrean Iota, which begins to flash. Squall and Otto snipe it, popping the iota instantly.

Within the security check-in, k1k1 deftly runs past the eagle and hides under a table. The eagle flies over, bangs into the table, and paws under the table with his talons. But, this may not last for long. During the whole kerfuffle, Claire murmurs that this will make _very_ fun content.

"You're here to break her out?"
