We begin the session with a speech, in time past, delivered by Ainscorp to pitch Mammon's Vault.

After this, we cut to [[Shaw]] and [[Darrett]] waiting in the Ocean Court, above the neon streets of [[Lil' Avernus]]. The camera cuts down, into the city, showing [[Bingo]] in a street light in the early morning. He approaches us, and asks what the plan is for him and [[Candy]].

[[Claire]] confirms that Bingo's guy is still good to help. He confirms that he does, so we talk about getting him in through imports, and keeping him on standby there—with Candy on standby on our ship with Darrett and Shaw.

We wrap up with Bingo, who asks if we need anything else. [[Vayb]] asks if they'll have drinks inside. The group assumes no because they're angels. He points out that they work with people, to which Claire replies that they work with narcs. Vayb grins and punches her on the shoulder, then tells her he's happy she's learning "you're right, narcs _aren't_ people."

We enter the vault building, to a fortified security chamber—where we're greeted by a giant eagle. The eagle asks our business, and lets us in. On the way in, we pop Vayb's telepathic link and discuss our plan in meeting with the Curator. Vayb and [[Otto]] want to see what the Curator wants and see if we can convince him to part with the Codex once we figure out what he wants.

We come face to face with the Curator: a huge man whose skin is multicolored as though he was made of [[The Phlogiston|Phlogiston]]. His long hair, we realize, are not quite made of strands of hair, but rather strands of _text_. His wings shimmer and seem to hum with notes of music. His eyes seem to know infinity. He looks at us.

"You are... the Ocean Court Express?" He asks.

"Last time I checked," replies Otto.

The Curator introduces himself, and says that he figured he should conduct this meeting himself. He explains the history of the entrance/guest wing, and how he planned to meet with us because—although the [[AAPD]] stressed we had some kind of urgency, but didn't elaborate on what.

Vayb says it's a matter of life and death. We explain that it isn't INSTANTLY urgent, though, and keep making conversation. Otto asks if the Curator has a name, out of "politeness". He explains that his essence isn't like our mortals'. His existence and purpose have the same purpose and his title IS his name. He explains that he finds something like what we might call _pride_ in organizing and protecting secrets like this.

Otto asks him a question about his appointment to this role and the way his nature connects to this. He explains that structure is important to a pantheon; everything they do has order to it. A large pillar that stretches all the way to the TOP!!!!! of perfect order and unity. Each god, and their holy army, uses their skills to create the most effective pantheon hierarchy. There is a chain that leads him to be the leader of this place, but he answers to Oghma, the god of Knowledge himself. This place is run through HIS will.

Otto says he appreciates that illumination, and that that's sort of what this is about. The Curator isn't the only one who answers to a higher power. "We—or, well, I answer to a higher power."

This interaction is cut off by Vayb getting annoyed by the topic and starting to light a cigarette—before being rebuked and told to stop by the Curator. He stares in defiance, but then meekly puts away the cigarette.

[[D'Arby]] takes the chance to interrupt to ask if there's a bathroom. He's directed to a hall a couple lefts down and through a portal, where there'll be a portal to the right. The Curator sends three celestial things made of Celestial text to accompany him. D'Arby says he's gonna go ask some question first, and approaches Abraham the giant eagle. He startles Abraham, who nervously agrees to share some of the spicy corn puffs he's eating.

The Curator apologizes to Otto for the interruptions. Otto says it's not a problem. They're interrupted again by Vayb fidgeting, in need of a smoke—so the Curator casts a spell and cures Vayb of the shakes. Vayb reacts pretty annoyed, asking if he asked for help. Both of them see confused by each other.

The two start to bicker a little. Otto jumps in to try to calm things down, explaining that a mortal's struggles are just as important to them as their accomplishments. The Curator apologizes for his inconsideration, and casts a spell again. Vayb gets his nicotine addiction back.

We move to a more private meeting room to keep talking with the Curator—a fancy place with a fountain filled with something that isn't quite water, as well as a bar with refreshments and snacks (cheese, crackers, wine, etc.)

Otto finally gets to begin his line of conversation. He explains that some mortals do choose to follow higher powers—and that these callings are what brings him here. Before explaining, he asks what the Curator knows of the world outside the Vault. Not much, it would seem—he knows what the things brought into the Vault tell him. Of the mortal struggles and what they need to be saved from: fiends, aberrations... each other.

> \[!Claire Note]
> so you partner with the sindiath line. sure.

Otto and the Curator having a conversation about morality leads the Curator to confirm (in front of us!) that the two of them are from the same world. The Curator ensures he knew about the story of Karsus—the disaster of Karsus' Folly. The Curator explains that intelligence can lead to disastrous consequences and evils, even if Karsus had altruistic goals (to save his people). Otto replies that the Curator is wrong—true altruism would mean Karsus wanted to save EVERYONE. It is the seeking of power that is evil.

The Curator concedes the point. Says that Otto speaks absolute truth. And, he guesses, that that seeking of power is what we seek to prevent. Otto nods, and explains that someone has been going through the stars, accumulating power for his own purposes—and that Otto's divine mission is to stop that. And that we'd need an item from the Vault for that.

The Curator laughs, and says he expected that. That we'd be delusional enough to believe our hands are the right place for these cursed items.

Vayb and Claire criticize the Curator for his connection to the [[The Sindiath Line|Sindiath Line]]—Vayb says that he's been set up to watch these items for the Line, that he's been kept blind to the outside world. That his knowledge, the ultimate goal, is being impeded by this task.

Claire interrupts and says we need to stop arguing morality. What does the Curator have to stop the might of the entire Elvish Imperial Navy when they decide it's no longer convenient for the Curator to be watching these items? The Curator says that it would be foolish to challenge the entire divine hierarchy that exists behind and around him. For now, he will keep doing his job because he has all the tools to do so. Claire can only laugh at his stupidity.

Otto then says that the Curator has failed at his job. His job is to protect the _worlds_, not to watch these items. By being so focused, he's already failed.

We cut to D'Arby, who talks to a guard by the bathroom area. He asks about the celestial iotas that are following him, and whether or not they're gonna watch him while he pees—since, he says, he "can't perform in front of an audience." There's not much that the guard can do.

D'Arby then pulls out a cigarette to limit test the iotas. They blast it out of his hands when he's bringing his lighter to it. D'Arby then shrugs, does his business, and then keeps examining the area around him.

D'Arby returns to the room as the Curator is beginning to admonish us for believing he might relinqui—

Then... a booming alarm goes off.

D'Arby puts his hands up and says he swears he flushed.

"This is not you. There has been a breach."

The Curator then says he's initiating his divine mandate on us: we must stay put. Claire and Otto begin to argue. Otto explains that he will not let the Curator refuse Otto's own divine mandate to do good. Claire insists that this could be the people we were warning him about. Claire casts _guidance_. Otto puffs his chest, cloak billowing, and exudes radiant pride.

The Curator calms somewhat but tells us to stay put while he surveys the situation. He bids Abraham to watch over us.

We establish a telepathic link and discuss our next plan: we agree to try diplomacy with Abraham first.

Claire tries to catch him up on what we were telling the Curator, and that they're being used by the Sindiath Line. Vayb gives a speech about the morality of walking the line between order and chaos to do good.

D'Arby can see some trepidation inside the eagle's head: fear of the competence or possible lack thereof of the Sindiath Line, some sort of doubt in the organization of the Vault that he's been stuffing down. D'Arby puts a hand on his wing and says he can trust us with this.

Abraham thinks, then sighs. He says he was TELLING Din that this place isn't uncompromised. That he's had dreams that something like this is happening. He sighs, then tells us that if we see any dangers, we need to not hesitate to get rid of it, as he thinks they should have been doing the whole time.

Abraham then flies past D'Arby and eviscerates the Iotas behind him. He nods to us and says "I didn't see you."

We then leap into action, rushing to find Bingo. Otto rushes into the room, catching the attention of a giant owl. We enter combat. [[Squall]] OHKOs it with a shot from their stun gun.

We search for the box Bingo is supposed to be in, marked with a red dot. We open it up and do our best to catch Bingo up on what's going on; asking him to help. He casts _pass without trace_. We move on, teleporting out to the docks.

There's a large owl there, patrolling the area. Vayb sneaks along, inspecting a minecart on glowing rails. Enchanted, he can see, to follow the will of celestial beings. It won't work for him. There's a telescope, though, which Claire uses to look for other entrances. Comes up with nothing.

But... as she looks around, she does see a single tiny vehicle lurking in the shadows of Lil' Avernus' cityscape, close by. An AAPD vessel. She informs everyone, then we double back.

In another room, there is a commissary. Vayb enters, looking around, and is spotted by a pair of giant owls. One reaches for a _sending stone_. Vayb casts a psionic _suggestion_ on that one to Jedi mind trick him into believing we're not a threat—and just bluffs and gaslights the other one into saying we're chill.

Then... we hear something over the sending stone (rockie talkie, as Shmamy called it :p).

"Threat neutralized."

We bluff to the owls again, and they fly off.

We roll credits, unsure of what the hell is going on with this alarm.
