**Note:** We had the wonderful artist DiceDoll vibing and listening in on our session tonight—and she produced the lovely sketches of the game's moments that you've been seeing!!

[[Vayb]] continues flying us through the Flow, in the Aquarius region—on the way to [[Lil' Avernus]]. [[Squall]] has been despondent and unresponsive, their display typically blank.

As the session starts, we cut to a conversation in the ship's control room.

[[PIXIE]] asks us a question—confirming that our mission is to rush to Lil' Avernus to find this Codex to unlock the tablet before [[2NGEL]] catches up. [[Otto]] says she's missing the part where we kick ass and look great while doing so.

[[D'Arby]] asks us to clarify what we need the Codex for. He asks if we need it to get some junk. [[Claire]] explains that we have the junk we need, but it's scrambled, and this will unscramble the junk we have.

We take a moment to clarify our character's states in the aftermath of the 2NGEL encounter: Claire is lounging around in pyjamas and has not done her make-up for an uncharacteristically long time. Otto, for his part, had spent a few hours stewing and then bounced back with the same energy he always has ("Coping mechanisms? What are those?").

> \[!Claire Note]
> how does this guy look so perfect??

Regarding Lil' Avernus, Pixie asks how we intend to present ourselves—as Lil' Avernus is occupied by beings from the Outer Planes who have fled the Blood War and is thus primarily occupied by devils, demons, and the angels that "guard" the area and keep the law.

Otto tells Pixie that the angle is simple: offer our services to the angels to "cleanse" the city of troublemakers. Claire interprets this as way too genocidal, considering the fact he's a paladin but he's shocked at the implication—no, we can just offer some help cracking the heads of some troublesome figures and that can let us in.
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816222812.png]]

Understanding his pitch now, we agree and ask a bit more about the actual power structures. The angels kinda watch the borders and there are two gangs as the main powers in the city: one run by an archdevil and the other by a demon lord.

Pixie points out that, as beings of the Outer Planes, they can sense our mortal souls—Otto says it's not an issue; we'll just kick their asses if they try to "eat our soul", as Claire had put it.

D'Arby asks about devils and demons, since he doesn't know much about them. Otto launches into a long explanation about the types of fiends, how to fight them, and how to tell them apart. D'Arby pays close attention. By the time Otto realizes how long he's been rambling, Claire has checked out and is playing on her Spacetendo DS.

Pixie routes us to a warehouse for some magic item shopping to prep for the trip.

While we're together for a sec, D'Arby points a finger at Claire and asks if she's okay—saying that she's been leaving lots of cups and stuff around lately and he doesn't remember her doing that. She says she thought that's what Vayb's _unseen servant_ is for, and accuses Vayb of having it not clean up for her as a way of messing with her somehow. D'Arby changes gears a bit and leaves it be, but first advises Claire to eat the crusts on her sandwiches. She just replies that it tastes gross. He leaves it be, then pursues Otto to investigate the devil tattoo on his arm.
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816222823.png]]

> \[!Claire Note]
> and now d'arby's being all weird, this is all vayb's fault

Otto has been spending a lot of his time off working out and then sparring with [[Darrett]] (and kicking his ass). Darrett has yet to kick his habit of announcing his attacks in advance. Otto explains that it's a bad idea, but also says it's something that can be worked around. As long as you're fast enough, you're still able to land a hit—he announces "gut punch!" and sucker punches Darrett, who whines in pain: "it hurts even when you don't use the smite...."
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816222833.png]]

D'Arby catches Otto for a moment, and asks if—since he's an infernal expert—Otto knows anything about the tattoo, which has spread(?) and has started glowing in the dark. Now that he can't sleep, it's been bothering him enough to talk to Otto about it now.

D'Arby explains how he got it from that machine thing we have in our hold now that we took it from the gang in [[Shilear]]. "The tank gave you a glowing tattoo...?"

Otto investigates it and uses his divine sense, which picks up fiendish energies in the runes of the tattoo. They _don't_ come from D'Arby, so Otto says we'll keep an eye on it but for now hopefully it's just a nightlight—but if D'Arby feels it changing or feels a burning sensation, he's to tell Otto.

Pixie alerts Otto that we've arrived at this warehouse—which is covered in scorch marks. Otto searches for signs of dangerous activity, but determines that the fire is not from blaster fire, but is rather something that burst out of the window.

We ask Pixie again to show us what we're looking for: she shows us the bracelet of soul dampening, made of braided golden thread.

Otto asks who's ready to buy some accessories. Claire mutters that they're pretty tacky—Otto side eyes her, since she's still in pyjamas. D'Arby diplomatically asks if she's done anything new with her hair. Claire considers, replies no, then mutters that her roots are showing and she really should dye her hair again. D'Arby simply purses his lips thoughtfully, thinking that he's on to _something_. Otto sees the look and assumed D'Arby must be crushing or something.

Otto asks Claire to set up a _detect magic_ as we arrive, and activates his own magic to search—finding a crate with about five of the items we're looking for. Claire's spell detects many crates around the room emanating out some magical glows of various crates. In one of the crates, on the far left, Claire can see an orb of flame that glows with conjuration magic (perhaps an elemental?).

Otto searches around and finds jewelry, rings, staffs, all sorts of items scattered around this place, scattered from broken crates. The items all have an eclipse symbol on them. Despite their resemblance to magic items, they do not read with _detect magic_. Additionally, there is a massive magical factory arm with the same eclipse symbol.

We recognize the symbol as Umbra Technologies—a magic item manufacturer that mass produces items for "adventurous individuals". Claire knows them from many many rants that her dad has given about that "ridiculous excuse for a company." They're a big rival of Ainscorp, if not the [[The Sindiath Line|Sindiath Line]] as a whole.

Otto simply comments that they've left it around—finder's keepers! The arm turns to Otto and a voice booms across speakers "excuse me, sir! I was scanning for that!" and yanks the crate away. Otto asks why this person didn't speak when he arrives—to which it replies that he hadn't announced his employee ID when he entered!

When prompted more, the voice explains that it is an artificial intelligence named MAAS. D'Arby says his employee number is 12345. The AI says that's not a valid number. Otto asks what would happen if we forgot our number. The answer is that we'd be terminated.

Claire tries to clarify: terminated as in fired, or murdered? It clarifies: fired! Claire then glances at the fire marks all over: fired as in unemployed or burnt up? It says your job would be terminated, which would make you... a customer!

> \[!Claire Note]
> maxim's maxims: an ainsworth always reads the fine print

We simply explain that we're customers—and it launches into a spiel about how the customer is always right, as long as the customers are in accordance with Umbra's services.

MAAS gives us a presentation (with exactly the infomercial background music you'd expect). It launches into a speech about the founder's desire to push the boundaries of adventurers' capabilities in violence. In recent years, losing more sales to the Sindiath Line, they created a breakthrough: aren't you tired of having to attune to only three items at once? Aren't you tired of switching between magic items with an action or bonus action? It waits for a response.

We halfheartedly agree.

It introduces itself again: Magic As A Service! For a subscription, we can get the MAAS Epitaph! A tablet that can select whatever magic item you need and whenever.

That's what MAAS has been up to! It's been draining the magic from hundreds of thousands of magic items, in order to prepare those enchantments for this service!

Otto asks some clarifying questions. It asks if he wants to engage in a free trial. Otto agrees, and in response, MAAS pulls out a simple white box with the Umbra logo.

MAAS asks us what we want. D'Arby says he's thirsty and asks for a _decanter of endless water_. MAAS replies that it was removed due to unpopularity. Claire asks for a _candle of invocation_—which has not been added to the database yet. We ask what it does have.

It has a _cloak of billowing_. Then it promptly removes the cloak for "data storage" reasons.

> \[!Claire Note]
> this is the most useless technology, we'd better not put tv shows on subscription services like this!

Otto asks to cancel his trial and is sent to a VERY complicated website and will be billed in 30 minutes. He asks PIXIE to help him get rid of it.

We ask about the bracelets, which MAAS says it is about to absorb for its service—D'Arby and Claire immediately launch into an irate customer/influencer routine and MAAS recalculates. It didn't realize we were influencers! It gives us the crate for free!
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816222841.png]]

The crate has a: _potion of greater healing_, a _sword of life stealing_, a _hide armor of radiance resistance_, _spell scroll of enervation_, and a _cape of billowing_. Otto takes the cape and says "maybe this trip wasn't a complete waste." Claire is given the potion, and we suggest giving the armour to Darrett.

Otto poses with the cape. D'Arby gives a polite clap to his posing.
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816222847.png]]

MAAS asks if we need anything else—and tells us that the company gives one free murder to each influencer. Otto asks what's in the crate with the fire orb. It says it does not recognize an Umbra item inside. "Smashing now." We cancel the smashing, then discuss what to do with it.

We're not sure if it's dangerous or a trap, so Claire summons her Tamagotchi familiar and asks, in a very sweet voice, if k1k1 wants to go be a sacrificial lamb to see if the crate is trapped.

It's hard to describe what Claire sees through the familiar's eyes. There's a sublime brightness to it, like a small star. This creature immediately presses itself to the back of the crate—almost... _shivering_.

Claire approaches physically. The thing shrinks back from her, too. She quietly tries to greet it. It continues pulling back and makes a slight _fwoo!_ whistling noise. Otto determines its creature type is an elemental.

> \[!Claire Note]
> aww, it's cute :D

Otto tells it that we have no issue with it, as long as it doesn't try to kill us.

It flies slowly out of the crate, and hovers near Otto's cheek. Otto gives the glowing orb a little bow and tells it that it's free to go.

Otto asks Claire what it is—all she knows, though she has to run a "pokédex" search with her tablet to figure it out. These things have shown up all across the Flow, but no one actually knows what they are. Usually, they're much bigger but it's not _unheard of_ for them to be this small. No one knows what their purpose are, but that they're like little stars: blazozoids.

Claire tries to coax it over with a lil _pspspsps_ like it's a cat, but it seems to enjoy lighting up Otto's new billowing cloak.

Otto can tell, from examining the area, that it seems as though this area was infested by these blazozoids—they wreaked destruction and were destroyed and exploded by the Umbra defense system. The little one might be one of the few survivors.

We agree that we'd like to take it in—see if we can find its way to others of its kind. In response, the flame turns purple. It whistles and gets a little closer. We decide to take it with us

Otto claims one of the magic greatswords, which looks cool, just as a decoration piece. Claire says that's a great idea and starts poking around to find cute accessories in the jewelry.

We cut to later on the ship—PIXIE shouts out for Captain. We get a visual of a certain tiefling floating in space. Meat, the tiefling. In zero gravity, his cart floating with him. He doesn't seem disturbed by this.

> \[!Claire Note]
> sorcerers are so ridiculous

"Would you like me to blast it?" Pixie asks. Otto tells her maybe. "Excellent! Weapons standing by!"

We broadcast a message to Meat: "You're in space."

Meat replies. "Oh! Oh hey, bruhs!"
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816222852.png]]

Considering he's slowly starting to suffocate, we agree to let Meat onto the ship. First, though, Otto asks if he has any parasites—Meat replies "Uhh, I have ringworm?"

We figure that's good enough and pull him into the ship: Claire steals Vayb's hoverboard to fly him in.

> \[!Claire Note]
> i know i _can_ cast fly, i just wanted to take it for a spin >:3c

We tell Meat that we're not on our way back to the [[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]], so we can't really help him get home since we're on our way to Lil' Avernus. Meat says "oh hell yeah!" And tells us that his cousin, [[Candy]], lives there. We figure it's cool to have a connection to a local.

We ask what he knows about Lil' Avernus, and he tells us a few details, including that it's got 100 degree weather. Claire goes "A HUNDRED?!" (elves use Celsius).
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816222903.png]]

The conversation turns to Meat telling us that he's happy to sell us some items. Maybe it will help him afford to charter a ship home!

Otto buys one, and pulls the _book of eavesdropping_. D'Arby spends his last 50 gold (HE SPENT ALL 3000 GOLD FROM 2NGEL ALREADY???) and pulls a _Nystul's Fantastic Token_ (read the acronym XD). Claire pulls _Tom's lens of peeping and creeping_. Claire also pays for Darrett to get an item: the _Reverse Card_. We also officially give Darrett the _sword of life stealing_ and the _hide armour of radiant resistance_.

After we're done, Meat asks if he can eat our coffee grounds. Otto surmises that at this point this might as well happen.

Afterwards, D'Arby asks Pixie if she can help look into the ninja from Shilear. She says that's an obscure thing to google, but she can try. Then he asks how to end the conversation: does he say power off? Does he say end program?

Pixie replies that she'd really rather he just says bye. So, he says bye and leaves.
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816225615.png]]

D'Arby asks Claire and Otto to help him check out the infernal machine, and finally shows off his tattoo to Claire. Claire tells him he could probably rock the tattoo if he removed a sleeve. D'Arby says he doesn't wanna damage his expensive silk shirts.

D'Arby then tries to get into the cockpit of the infernal engine with friend supervision—it seems to be a painless and normal activation, although it floods the garage with thick smoke. D'Arby asks the machine if it's alive. It is not, much to his relief!

Finally, we talk with Pixie, who has gathered some statistics for us on our emergent fandom. One sect of Otto's fandom calls themselves the harem of Otto, and is obsessed with considering themselves his many many wives. The other two sects of the fandom are generally fans of Otto's outlandish and adventurous performance in kicking ass and taking names. Both of these groups agree that Otto is VERY kickass, but they disagree on what class he is: some consider him to be an honorable paladin, others believe him to be a warlock of some eldritch aboleth, others say he's just a fighter with special effects. Some say he's a wizard, but everyone knows that's dumb. Otto makes an official post about how he's a "class of his own."

> \[!Claire Note]
> _harem of otto?????????????????_

D'Arby doesn't care about his fandom, but Claire and Otto look anyway. D'Arby has caught the attention of the Elvis Imperial Navy, who is deeply attracted to his totally groovy style. Comments include "Whoa, mama, this guy's fightin' makes me wanna rock and roll!"

One other thing they find is a 200 page slowburn enemies-to-lovers fanfiction about Vayb and Squall. About how they were on warring tribes in [[Athas]], but found a star-crossed relationship in the night and they escaped Athas together to find a world that would accept their love. But their secret enemy, Claire who works for _both_ clans is planning to assassinate them. Will their love survive?? Read to find out!
![[Images/Pasted image 20250816231451.png]]

And lastly, Claire's been public for a while, but her numbers are skyrocketing! She finds an email from her _father_, with a graph projecting her views, with an exponentially-increasing green. He accompanies this email with a long, heart-felt message: one thumbs-up emoji. A group of Ocean Court fans keep a tally of how often Claire gets knocked unconscious, and discuss how they'd do better in her place. A different group has a pitch for an official documentary on the Ace lifestyle, inspired by Claire's work. Different individuals are posting guides—people who want to know how they can BLC (be like Claire) and become [[Aces]] and live this flashy lifestyle.

> \[!Claire Note]
> ... hm.

Finally, D'Arby sees them chatting about this and asks what's going on—Otto explains how Claire's publicizing their adventures. We briefly discuss what to tell the world about Shilear, and then D'Arby notices that his name is on our new shipment of Luigifuel—Claire and Otto gaslight him about how he's the brand ambassador, and he shrugs in amused, confused defeat.

And with that, we fade out and roll credits for tonight's episode!
