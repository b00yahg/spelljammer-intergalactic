We begin our story on a red Spelljammer. We sit among, mostly, strangers. A person ahead of the group sits in front of a projected email—one that brought us together. "So," they say, "you're the ones who're going to do this."

![[Images/Pasted image 20251227204713.png]]

The first of the group is a man with a labcoat and deeply tired eyes. He's a botanist, here in part because a series of budget cuts have made his salary VERY small and also to accompany Humility as a primary care provider.

![[Images/Pasted image 20251227212528.png]]

Sitting next to Dr. Hickory is the aforementioned Humility. She's an old, wizened tiefling woman. Perhaps she shouldn't even be adventuring. She's currently lying down on a couch, with her bat familiar hanging on her cane—a seeing eye bat.

Sat in one of those ugly egg chairs is a young woman with tense posture, teal hair, pale skin, blue eyes, and a sweater. She's nervously tapping on a gas lamp—like it was removed from an old street lamp.

![[Images/Pasted image 20251227212546.png]]

6'3 in height, standing amid the group, is a slim but muscular person. They have cat ears, and two catlike tails that flick around. A tabaxi, and a chimera—the patterns of fur and colour of eyes split down the middle. Maru is wearing a face mask, and seems to be hard of hearing in one ear.

![[Images/Pasted image 20251227212518.png]]

Sitting in a recliner is Allie, the most talkative among the group. She's leaning back in the recliner, feet up. She wears a curved blade with a leather buckler strapped to it. Her outfit is pinks and reds with a black vest. Strawberry blonde hair in a ponytail. A scar is kind of hidden on her forehead, behind her bangs.

Our chauffeur for the evening is Captain Zed, an astral elf with bright green eyes. They slouch, lean on everything, and reek of Axe body spray.

![[Images/Pasted image 20251227205425.png]]

They summarize the mission. "We go to the location marked on this map thingie, and if we get all the notes and documentation that the doctor who hired us wants, we get to keep it."

Dr. Hickory remarks that we seem like an odd bunch of people, using language that would be quite offensive to gay people. Humility remarks that Hickory shouldn't say that—but he points out that he's including himself.

"Okay..." Zed pulls up a PowerPoint. A big cartoon ghost pops up with text reading "WARNING. GHOST AHEAD!"

Maru raises her hand.

We discuss whether or not (and why not) the group does not have a cleric.

The next slide depicts a ship. We don't know, Zed explains, how this ship runs. That's part of what we need to figure out. Supposedly, the ship is "alive". Not in a plant way.

Then, the next slide is a HUGE slide of small text. Zed walks forward toward the screen, trying to read it. "Eh," they say. "None of that is important. You'll be fine."

Hickory says that it seems important. "Nah," says Zed.

Zed suggests we go get set up in the strapped-in seats because we're about to land. Then, it occurs to us: Zed is the _captain_. They are not, currently, _in the helm_.

Maru and Abby strap themselves in. Others follow. But, Allie goes to the helm to get a good look instead, saying this is her favourite kind of landing. Allie sees an ancient ship floating in space. When the PowerPoint said ancient, it meant ANCIENT. Not a Spelljammer, as such. An old, wooden galleon.

Zed leaps into the pilot's seat and quickly pilots the ship to its landing. Abby checks in with Allie if this is "go time," and is happy that Allie agrees we have a couple more minutes. Hickory and Humility start complaining that they don't have a rapport the way they do.

Hickory complains that he didn't even vet the rest of the group. Allie replies that "of course you didn't; we're not animals!" Hickory points out that he's a botanist.

Hickory and Zed establish that we're being paid an "exorbitant" sum for this mission, and then says to Humility that he'd like Humility to keep her eyes peeled, as he has ideas for a cure for Humility.

Before we leave, Abby sighs, casts _mage armor_, and then pulls off her sweater to reveal a bunnygirl outfit on. Allie and Zed whistle. Hickory and Humility complain about the others not wearing armour, like real adventurers should.

![[Images/Pasted image 20251227212603.png]]

Zed looks at Maru, winks, and takes off their punk jacket. They realize they're only wearing a t-shirt underneath. They put the jacket back on. Maru just gives them a pat on the head in pity. They assure Zed that they're adequately dressed for adventuring.

Then, we leave our Spelljammer, and set foot upon the vessel for the first time. An electric shiver travels up our spines. Here, on the Koílos Thyella, our footsteps echo through the empty ship. Then, more noises. Creaking, not that of wood... but those of joints. The ancient crew of the Spelljammer does not seem to be acknowledging our presence. Its undead, skeleton crew, keeps going. Some of the skeletons have the tatters of jumpsuits on them. Others seem to be dressed in simpler clothes.

Hickory nudges Maru and tells her that he'll give her 4 gold for collecting a skeleton. Maru says it would be pretty easy, that they'd be quite easy to pick up "like little mouse." Abby says she has a safe place we could store one. However, we decide we should make sure they actually won't mess with us first. Humility sends her bat, Prada, to search the area and examine the skeletons. They pay the bat no mind. Abby determines she can get their attention, but they're more focused on their jobs than on us.

Hickory tries to telepathically connect with one. But then, before he can even send a thought through the connection, his mind is _swarmed_ by a cacophony of voices. Many, many overlapping conversations. Then, the conversations stop, all at the same time. And a feeling scans across the telepathic bond. Wordless, but a message. "You should leave," it says.

Hickory asks why, "is there something here dangerous to us?"  Hundreds of voices reply in succession. "Y e s ."

Hickory pulls from the bond, his nose bleeding. He laughs. "It's a hive!! Holy shit, that's fascinating! They're connected to a single life force!!"

Maru voices concern: that a hive of bees protect their honey. Allie says she'd be more worried about the queen.

We decide we need to get to work, anyway, but to be careful too.

Hickory decides to give one of his elixirs to Humility, forgetting that she's blind. The bottle bonks her on the head, he fails to catch it, and then Allie swoops in and catches it on the flat of her sheathed sword.

Distantly, Humility can hear a door creak open.

A door has opened in front of us, leading inside the ship. A lantern is lit inside. Hickory says that the living ship might be beckoning us inside in some sort of carnivorous way. Allie asks his where his showmanship is. "Oh, sure, yeah," he replies. "I'll die, as long as it's got showmanship."

Hickory pulls out a monstrous homunculus made of plants from a jar, growing from seeds and spores inside. The creature moans in pain, then says "I am here, Daddy Hickory! Shall we get bones?"

Hickory explains to us that the homunculus just has a thing about wanting to grow inside of people's bones.

Allie turns to Abby and asks what she can glean about the ship. Abby hypothesizes that it's from the era of the first Spelljammers to take flight into Wildspace. The DM confirms this, and Abby can also glean that this ship should not be intact or able to fly, at its age. Maru says it might be like a hermit crab. Abby remarks that that might be right. Maru begins to purr.

Hickory takes the moment to ask Abby and Allie what's up with their outfits. Allie decides to take a stroll to look at the skeletons. Abby hands him a business card for her theatre on the [[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]].

Then, we step into the captain's quarters. A monitor in the room crackles to life. In the corner, a skeleton is in a chair, wearing a jumpsuit. In the Spelljammer helm. It is still, potentially not animate. The group examines the skeleton, which is also wearing some kind of helmet, like a primitive version of the helmet Zed wore.

Hickory examines the skeleton closely, determining that the skeleton must be VERY old. At least two centuries, based on the degradation of the calcium on the bones.

Abby distributes maps among the group, then pulls a 10-foot pole out of her bag. She pokes the skeleton with it, who seems to rouse and swat her away, like someone half-asleep swatting away a bug. Hickory comments that it's clearly the pilot, and it's a good thing we didn't kill it.

Maru looks to Zed, and says that the tech is old, but if the helm works similarly, maybe Zed could read the ship's logs. "Nope," they say. "I'm not putting on a dead man's hat."

"Maybe this will help," Hickory says, and hands Maru a scrap of paper he found on the skeleton. She unfolds it, and reads a message:

> "It will be greed that binds our fates and spirits here. I know it in my bones that what we've sought is not an answer, and yet, there is no moving backwards. The Potential cannot be bottled or stored, it must only be."

The monitor flickers, and reveals an image of the main deck on the faded screen in green hues. Something has wrapped itself around the mast, seemingly starting to form into something tangible. The rest of us see the form as... a woman's legs?

Hickory knows these legs, though. He knows every part of his ex-wife. Using the mast for balance, the arch-demon becomes more and more clear on the screen. His ex-wife is here. All ten feet of her.

He just stares at the screen. He gets closer, staring at nothing, eyes wide. Hickory just seems to speak as though compelled. "You fucking bitch I had a restraining order what are you doing here??????"

The screen flickers, revealing a new room. This time, amongst the wreckage of one of the ship's rooms. Allie sees the energy coalesce into someone familiar, though it takes a moment to recognize them as the image comes together. Someone she used to know, an old crew member... but it seems to not quite work. It is... an amalgamation? One person's nose. Another's eyes. Something she'd recognize at a glance, but _wrong_ if she looks clearly.

Hickory continues to rant incoherently, asking why the hell she's here and rambling about her taking the abyssal chicken and half his savings. Maru pats him on the back, though accidentally fairly roughly. However, he doesn't seem to notice. Used to it. Of course, as his ex-wife is 10 feet tall.

Allie pulls him and Abby together and suggests they put their heads together, see what they can figure out about it. Hickory says it's illusion, Abby says it may just be a psychic shapechanger. Like a cuttlefish, Maru comments. Abby casts _protection from evil and good_ on Hickory. This seems to calm him.

We hear clamouring and thudding from outside. Allie comments that, if it can touch things, that means Maru can probably punch it. Maru grins wickedly. Maru kicks open the door and we step into the halls to attack the creature. Hickory casts _faerie fire_ into the hall as we rush out. Hickory, seeing that it looks like his ex-wife, screams out that he's gonna have a lot of fun with this, then calms himself and reminds himself that the divorce was supposed to be amicable.

Abby fires off a fire bolt, misses. Allie attacks with a starry wisp, and misses too.

We enter a combat with the ghost in the form of his ex-wife. On her turn, enwebbed by the party, the ex-wife's mouth splits open and she begins to sing. Maru and Abby fail their saves and are charmed by the creature. We realize that she is just a sweet, innocent woman and we're the ones trapping her. She murmurs to us, "Help."

Maru, who wants to obey the command, decides to light the web on fire to free her. Allie attempts to end the charm with _silence_, to little avail. Hickory starts yelling at us to not listen to her "stupid 'I'm just a girl' shtick!!!" He attacks, and hits, but the charm does not end.

The homunculus (whose name is Jeremy Renner) approaches, screaming that "OooooOOOOoooo, you look like you have really big bones!!!!!"

Hickory solemnly replies that "she really does... she really does..."

Jeremy's attacks save Abby from the charm, but not Maru. On her turn, the spectral ex-wife obliterates Jeremy with a swipe from her claws. Hickory holds the dying Jeremy, and they mouth to each other: "My only regret... is that I never got to grow... inside of her bones." "I'll plant you there, buddy. I'll plant you there."

Finally, Hickory lands a crucial sharpshooter shot, and ruminates on his relationship with his ex-wife. It dies, becoming a blobfish thing of sorts.

Humility and Hickory have a short sort of heart-to-heart about the experience of killing his ex-wife. Not as fun of an experience as one would hope.

Maru, who had scrambled up the mast and gotten captured by the ghost blobfish thing, is covered in goo. Allie cleans her up and reassures her for being caught in the fight.

Then, Zed returns, talking to a mirror image of himself, which he calls Dez. Dez, upon arriving, remarks that "Oh, what the hell, you killed my sister!" Zed reacts in confusion, and Dez explains that "yeah, I'm one of them!" And they try to wander off.

We catch up to them and ask what they're up to and why they aren't killing us. Dez remarks that Zed cast a spell on them, and that's why they're chill now. Allie starts trying to make conversation, and as she does, Zed returns. They plant a knife in Dez's back, killing them. "I found some more stuff in the captain's quarters that I think we need."

Hickory finished up poking around the corpse and announces that he's ready to question Dez. Then he notices that Dez is dead and goes "OH WHAT THE _FUCK_?"

Zed mentions that they found that there's a medbay we should check out, and that the captain's quarters had an empty vial labeled "Potential". As well as several vials with things labeled with various things. Very old, very long-expired potions. For pain, for a cough. Medicine. They also hand us a map of the ship.

We head to the med bay. Wood, metal, and tile seem to bleed together in a weird twist. They tried to build something inside here... something beyond their capabilities. There is a desk, a simple screen, a patient's cot.

Abby pokes around the screen. It's a computer. There's a patient file. "The helm has infected his consciousness somehow. The Potential has infected the ship. This should not be possible. His cravings grow." It's labeled Captain Fortress. Hickory, who had the bottles, can realize this is the same name on the prescription: Noelle Fortress.

Hickory pokes around at the medical equipment. It's a wreck, but he can tell that it was once cutting edge technology... for the time. However, Abby notices that some of the equipment is melded in with the ship. New and old technology blended into one mess. Abby asks what they were doing. Allie corrects her, pointing out that "what ARE they doing?" might be the better question.

Hickory, a doctor, knows better how to look through files so he follows after Abby and checks the files. "Captain Fortress. Half Astral Elf Half Human. 56. M. Declining. Chesty cough, paling eyes, cold hands." But, he notices something. If this person was in decline... he probably shouldn't have been piloting the Spellajmmer. And these medicines weren't strong enough for that decline.

Allie checks in on Abby, who has been put off-kilter by the charm. Assured that Abby's a little bit better, Allie begins leading the way onward.

The group descends deeper into the ship... there's movement here. Not the same as the skeletons. But spirits. Food piles up on the tables. There seems to be a celebration. The conversation among the spirits stops, and they look at us. They begin chanting. "Entertain us! Entertain us! Entertain us!"
