We resume our programming with a retcon scene because Zed was supposed to tell us something. :P

When our party gathered together for the _first_ time, to meet our patron... we'd gathered in the gnome doctor's outpost. He looks out the window across the horizon toward mountains. He mutters that he's not sure what the dwarves there actually MINE. They gather it, ship it out... but only to other dwarves.

He then turns to us. With all of us here, and our captain approaching, he should tell us something. He's very interested in the Koílos Thyella, but he's not built for the excitement. (He's needed here, besides) He explains, reminding us that he's not interested in treasure. All he wants to know is how the ship is still RUNNING.

We smash cut back to the mess hall of the ship. Spirits risen, chanting a call for entertainment. Allie comments that she and Abby are good entertainers, and says we should "give them a show!"

Abby sets up a _silent image_ scene around Allie's performance of a musical build-up into a bar-room swing song. As she performs... the ghosts around us seem to deepen in colour. They become less translucent. A phantom beer stain seems to grow wetter and acquires a spell.

It's as though we're pulled back into the room with them.

Hickory, curious, tries to bump into a specter, testing how real they are. He passes through it, but there's some resistance. A cold sensation. He considers a lone elf at the back of the crowd, and can't tell much except that the clothes don't seem Imperial.

Zed hangs to the side, and is handed a drink from a specter. Zed and Hickory realize that they just had an INTERACTION with one of the specters. Then, despite Hickory's protests, Zed just decides to drink the drink. Hickory snatches it out of their hands.

Hickory points out the elf to Zed, wondering if it could be the captain. Zed raises an eyebrow. "Where else would the captain be?" "How do you know that's the captain?" "There's no one else tall enough to match the bones we found."

Hickory deadpans that he could have gotten that pointed out by anyone, but it sucks that it was Zed of all people who did so. "Do you think I'm stupid?" Zed asks, looking out with puppy dog eyes.

The two go ahead to try to reach the elf ghost. None of the ghosts really seem to be interacting with them again. They seem to be looping some sort of action—repeating the past, most likely? It's unknown whether they're even watching OUR performance or seeing some performance from the past.

Zed eats some ghost soup while they and Hickory try to figure out what to do. The soup seems to _heal_ a wound on their face. Hickory asks Zed to stab him. They say they're not gonna do that. Hickory then grabs a knife to try stabbing himself.

Humility and Zed interrupt—Hickory starts insisting that he wants to test the properties of this soup through observation. Humility asks how long she has left—and figures it's not very long. So she drinks some of the soup herself.

While Abby and Allie's performance ends... the ghosts fade away. Only the food remains.

Humility tells Hickory that you don't get to her age without taking a little risk.

The group has a rest and gathers back together. Hickory starts leading us into surmising what's going on on the ship. Hickory asks Zed if they've ever seen a ship like this and if it's got a different place where the "heart" would be. Metaphorically speaking. (Abby starts quietly begging it to be a metaphor)

Zed says that you can't really call a Spelljammer alive, but it's not NOT alive? But, given that it's older tech, it's not as though it has the same kind of engine as a modern Jammer, so... Abby points out that we just keep moving. They agree—it'll turn up somewhere.

Allie and Maru lead on. A board from the ceiling drops and almost hits them. Maru's tail puffs up. Allie peers into the gap, and sees more wires and spliced technology inside the ship.

Claw marks mar the ground of the cargo hold. Humility and Prada approach to inspect. Humility can't quite tell WHAT it is, but it has five claws. We discuss what it could be. Maru compares their claws to the marks. The marks are much larger. "This is very big cat. Much bigger than Maru."

Prada and Humility discover a hidden trapdoor. They open it.

There's a staircase beneath. It's a tight squeeze, but... the geometry doesn't quite make sense. There's no more wood. The walls are all metal. At the end of the stairs, there is a door with a keypad on it.

Humility manifests Prada on the other side of the door. The bat sees a ghostly woman on the other side.

Allie asks what our plan is, and if we wanna go in guns blazing. Maru says they don't have guns. Allie says "oh c'mon, love, seems like you've got a good set of guns on you." Abby deflates a little.

Hickory suggests there's a chance the ghost woman might not wanna attack, so we could keep weapons stowed. But first we've gotta hack that lock. Abby steps up to try.

It's a password lock, which takes up to 8 letters. She examines the worn-down letters: E F O R S T. Hickory angrily realizes that the password is the _captain's name_.

We input the code.

The ghost _screams_, hurting and frightening some of us.

We rush in, attacking it back. Hickory finishes the ghost off. It dissipates and something hits the ground—pills.

We begin poking around the room. We wonder if it's a good idea to pick up the pills. Hickory looks at the desk, and finds a medical file—including one on the ship's doctor. Her date of death is long, long before the death date on the captain. Supposedly the captain died at the helm.

Allie recalls an old wive's tales about death at the helm of a Spelljammer haunting the ship.

We approach a set of metal doors and enter a small, closet-sized room. Inside is a glass case. Inside, Hickory sees a wedding band with a black gem. It whispers to him. Abby and Allie see a tome that reads "The Perfect Trick". Humility (or rather, Prada) sees a medicinal treatise on cures for every conceivable disease known or unknown. And... Maru. Who had been touched by the siren... their brain is a little more conscious of what is happening. They see through the magic. They see a liquid, like mercury, floating within the case. It seems to react to stimulus around it.

"Surely this is too good to be true?" Humility asks. Hickory agrees, slowly putting his crossbow away. Maru puts their staff out to block us off. Allie asks them if it was magic.

Hickory then starts asking if we're sure it is inherently corrupt or if it was, potentially, just the previous people using it wrong. Maru sees the liquid inside _react_ to the word "potential". Seeing Hickory get more desperate, Allie rushes to cast Dispel Magic on this case.

Maru watches it pulse again.

The rest of us see the lights flicker. For just a second, we see the items waver.

Hickory gives a speech to the party about how the world is moldable nowadays. He gets more and more desperate.

We don't see a pale blue hand as Zed reaches into the container. He grabs at the liquid. Hickory panics and attempts to attack Zed/grab the container for himself. Humility whacks Hickory with her staff.

Allie casts _hideous laughter_ on Zed. They fail. As they fall, the little piece that they ripped away is clenched in their hand, all the while laughing on the floor.

Abby has an idea, grabs the outside of the container, and then pulls out her lantern. She _disappears_. Allie catches her genie lantern before it clatters to the floor. Abby reappears and tells everyone that it's stuck in there until morning, and asks everyone to "please _please_ calm down..."

Humility reawakens HIckory and Allie checks on Zed. They have one of the pills in their hand, and start saying that the "star" is gone. Allie breaks the news that what they saw was just an illusion. "Oh..."

As they start to say "let's get out of here," an alarm begins to blare. "Yep," says Allie. "Let's get outta here."

We make a break for it, Scooby Doo running style.

The DM says that something falls behind us. Nothing we'd notice—after all, the ship is falling apart around us! It's a book. An old tale, similar to that of The Cask of Amontillado—wherein a captain feeds another to the soul of his Spelljammer.

We reach the Cherry Bomb, get out of there. We compile our notes and findings, and return to the doctor. We tell him everything we discovered. He deflates, sadly shaking his head. "So... are you telling me that the Spelljammer ran on this stuff?"

He says he can contain the Potential here. Agrees to Allie tells him a bit of sailor's advice: if you ever think it's even slightly more than it's worth—throw it in the nearest black hole!

Humility says we should work together again some time. We kind of agree. Allie suggests she'll go off and get our ship ready for travel—invites Zed to join us if they want. She wishes Humility and Hickory well on their medical stuff. Invites Maru along for a job if they're interested.

After Allie leaves, Abby watches in her direction, sighs, and asks Maru and Zed if they think "she likes me." Humility says that "I thought I was the blind one!" Everyone says they thought they were already together. Married, even! Hickory rants about how marriage is a trap.

Maru hands a necklace to Abby (it's a long necklace with a star and moon that can be split into two necklaces), and tells her to give that to Allie and tell her how she feels.

Down the line, Cheese suggests, Humility would see one show in a year or so. Then is likely never seen again.

Right after the group splits, Hickory tells Humility that he'll bring her home. He apologizes, saying he really thought they had something with this one. Humility sighs, and replies that the church told her that genetics are beyond the power of the gods. Maybe magic is the same way. Hickory tells her that, every time in history it's been perceived that we'd hit a wall, we manage to find a way beyond that. Humility replies that even if this wall gets her... she wants him to keep going.

Hickory says he's got other options, though! He heard about this power over undead that he wants to look into and he's gonna send some "rube [[Aces]]" to go look into it for him.

Humility and Hickory agree that they'll keep trying, even if they're unsure of success. They've earned some time not solving the problem, Hickory supposes. He's got Benny for the weekend and invites Humility with him.
