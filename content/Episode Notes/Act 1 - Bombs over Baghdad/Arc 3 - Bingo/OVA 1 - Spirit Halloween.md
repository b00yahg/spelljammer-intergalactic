# Session Notes

_**Players beware... you're in for a scare!**_

We begin our adventure with the Ocean Court Express sailing through the Flow, traveling to [[Lil' Avernus]] (yes, the Halloween special is a flashback). We've all gone through our downtime activities: D'Arby's researching demons, [[Vayb]] and [[Squall]] had their moment, and Claire's breaking out of her shell, and Meat just teleported away (don't worry about the timeline, it just works <3)

[[D'Arby]] pours one out for the teleported Meat. As he'd drinking, the door opens and he hears Darrett's sheepish footprints. He's grinning and holding an iPad. "Hey, D'Arby! Is now a good time?"

D'Arby replies that it's _never_ a good time to be around an Apple product. [[Darrett]] doesn't know what that means, since he isn't holding an apple. But he tells D'Arby that he found this really good video. And since D'Arby doesn't have (or rather, doesn't check) his Nebula Net account, Darrett just wanted to show him in person. He keeps giggling.

He shows D'Arby a video of a carriage gently traversing an idyllic landscape—and then is suddenly interrupted by a man screaming and revving a chainsaw leaping right at the camera.

D'Arby's as cool as a cucumber.

Darrett says he's disappointed, the prank was sent to him by Acererak the Wizard and he nearly (actually, he did!) dropped his iPad!

D'Arby starts asking about stranger danger, but they conclude that since this is the guy Darrett was arguing with... he's been fighting with him, and fighting someone is basically like knowing them.

The two discuss and decide that they should send the prank to [[Claire]].

Darrett sends it to her, puts his ear on her door, and waits.

Out of earshot, D'Arby simply murmurs that "that was the scariest video I've ever seen..."

Claire sees a notification from D'Arby, mutters "oh no, did he get hacked?" She reads D'Arby's message: "Hello. Is D'Arby. I'm sending you this video. Haha. Watch the whole video. Haha. Surprise."

She clicks the video.

Right before the person pops up, suddenly... boom! There's a notification sent to all of us: an incoming call from Dr. Hickory!

Vayb puts the call on the ship speakers. "Yo, what up?"

We see Dr. Hickory's lab, which is a cluttered mess. There are bugs in cages on his worktable. A set of eyes, a kid's eyes, pop up above the table. The kid starts telling us frog facts, and is a little disappointed that we aren't shocked by the facts.

Hickory then appears, apologizing for the kid. He's got custody for the week.

He moves to asking why we weren't at the Rock—saying that the impression folks were getting is that we bitched out after losing our last bounty. Vayb just explains that it's "confidential."

Hickory concedes that he doesn't care, and more importantly, if he's right about our coordinates, he has a job for us. If we can make a pitstop for him, it would be great.

He's been tracking the whereabouts of various undead around—the reasons for which we shouldn't concern ourselves with.

He projects us a map of multiple planets. Arrows from other planets move toward them. Undead, pretty much any undead within a light years' radius, are gathering at those locations, apparently. And since he wants to figure out what's happening, he figures he'll send the most reckless people he knows.

We negotiate the price up from 500 to 750gp, and make the note that he doesn't want us telling [[Otto]].

The goal is simple: get down there, save some people or don't, find the source of the undead migration, and we'll send the data to him.

Vayb turns off the call, and the screamer video resumes, causing Claire to JUMP in surprise—she BRUTALLY messes up her eyeliner, and Darrett laughs in success. She shouts that he should "watch your back!!"

We convene at the helm to discuss our plan. We need to figure out why all these undead are moving, and why the people are all following them.

We arrive at our destination, and [[PIXIE|Pixie]] pings us to show us a map.

We've been sent to a place in [[Greyspace]] called Krule—once a place inhabited by humanoids, it's now a dead ruin. One day, the atmosphere simply _stopped_. All the air was sucked out in an instant, killing everyone. Since then, the place has been a ruin, one that doesn't really see any travel—because why would anyone go there anymore? A first wave of graverobbing stole the whole place of its treasure.

We arrive to see something unexpected. We find ruins, sure... but we see greenery. And orange. Vines encapsulate everything, pumpkins abound. There's a command tower peeking through the foliage. And it's crawling with... well, not life. But. _Unlife_. Zombies shamble around, managing some sort of civilized-seeming space. A bazaar, perhaps?

A bright orange sign glows above a ruin, reading: **Spirit Halloween**.

We aren't familiar with the concept of Spirit Halloween, but we know of the Halloween holiday. Squall surmises that maybe this has something to do with some powerful spirit of Hallow's Eve.

Vayb asks what the hell Halloween is. Claire has the horrible realization that _**none of the others**_ with her have gone Trick-or-Treating. She insists that everyone goes back onto the ship to get costumes for everyone.

She chooses costumes for everyone, improvising: D'Arby will be Ace Nicholas (since he has a lot of red in his wardrobe), Vayb and Squall will dress as each other, Darrett gets to be a cowboy (in her pink hat, of course), and Claire raids Otto's closet to dress up as an angel.

Then, we approach Spirit Halloween—a stone creature is perched behind a booth at the entrance.

"Hello there, travelers! Welcome to the spookiest place in the flow! Travelers beware, you're in for a scare! Muahahahaha! Step forward and I, the humble Eidolon, will be your guide!!"

The group banters about what kind of being an eidolon is, and how it would be weird to name a company after one.

Before the eidolon launches into a spiel, Claire interrupts and gets everyone to say "trick-or-treat"—and gets a chorus of "treater trick" "trick the treat" and "what's Halloween?"

In admiration for her Halloween spirit, the eidolon gives her a candy inspiration point. We hand it to D'Arby, who immediately eats it.

# Session Notes Cont'd

D'Arby briefly enters an argument with the eidolon when he interrupts—the eidolon thinks he's cramping his style and being rude, but eventually they calm together and discuss how the eidolon isn't even paid anything since he's here due to a magical thrall.

D'Arby looks at us about this and shakes his head.

Then the eidolon continues explaining what items we can earn through earning tickets: Vampire Fangs, Costume of the Changeling, Cloak of the Phantom, and a Ringmaster's Coat; an animated spider familiar, an undead deck guardian, a skeleton crew in a box, and **The Animatronic Horror**; a spooky candelabra, a bat swarm pennant, and a jack-o-lantern of warning; a bag of eternal candy, a vial of theatrical blood, a mist generator, and the reaper's hourglass.

D'Arby and Claire continue yapping with the eidolon, whose thrall compels him to tell us that this bit is taking too long.

In any case, Vayb and Squall have already rushed ahead to the game of dice with the reaper. The reaper stares at them, watching their movement.

It speaks a cryptic riddle at them, and welcomes them to its game, which they buy into by gambling years of their lifespan. Vayb wins the dice game by correctly reading the reaper's tells—and so it hands over and activates the tickets.

D'Arby and Claire make their way through the pumpkin patch and find the Gravekeeper's Riddles—an old woman with a withered face and bright blue eyes.

We pass one riddle, then lose another. Claire is going to fail a History check, at some point.

She tries again, and asks the riddler to just reveal the answer. She guesses wrong, then casts _gift of gab_, gaslighting EVERYONE around her to believe she got it right.

In the meantime, the gamblers keep going with the reaper, it would seem. They keep raising the ante for more and more tickets and powers. The reaper then asks if Vayb wants to gamble all the life they have left... then pulls out a deck.

They roll...

Reaper rolls 20.

Vayb rolls 21.

The reaper hands him the deck... but asks him why he spends his life like coin.

Vayb just lights a cigarette, shrugs, then says "go big or go home."

The reaper then asks to give him some advice before they part. Vayb inclines his head graciously.

"The house always wins, in the end."

"I'm countin' on it."

Claire finally realizes the last riddle, gets 1 ticket—and radios the group to rendez-vous and go to the Labyrinth of the Self for more tickets.

Claire is BAFFLED that the gamblers have earned 9 tickets and a deck—and is furious that she only got 1.

She tells D'Arby that we're gonna kick their butts. He says he can manage that, since he can grab their hands so they can't cast. Claire says they're gonna win the game—and D'Arby realizes she meant in the game, not a fight.

Claire remarks that yeah, she meant the game, but she appreciates him being so ride or die anyway.

A revenant welcomes us to the maze. Asks what we want to gamble for the attempt on the labyrinth. The price is high, but the prize is just as much: our sense of direction.

Vayb and Squall go left. Claire goes right.

Vayb and Squall 15 points of damage as a fireball EXPLODES around them. Shattered glass lies around them. Reflections of Vayb and Squall stare blankly at each other.

Claire looks to D'Arby and sighs. "Focus on my mirror, she'll be easy to kill." Then she webs them up and starts running.

Mirror D'Arby pulls out a gun as he gets restrained by the webs. Misses.

D'Arby and Claire decide to just keep going.

Vayb and Squall run into a dead end, turn around, and somehow end up on the OTHER side of the web due to the confusing maze. Squall logicks out that the trapped Claire and D'Arby must be the fakes.

Vayb _fireballs_ the webbed Claire and D'Arby. They die quickly.

Claire casts _fly_ on herself to go faster, leaving D'Arby in the dust too.

It occurs to D'Arby, though, that he can simply cast _jump_ to scale the maze. He hops up and sees the maze for what it is. However, he heads toward the north exit.

Following his cue, Vayb and Squall fly over and win the race.

D'Arby is almost about to front flip off of the wall, but Claire _vortex warps_ him to the centre: "I want all of those tickets!"

The revenant sighs and asks "what the fuck was that?"

Cutting ahead, the eidolon smiles and counts our tickets, then reels in shock—he's never seen this many tickets.

We get our audience with the King of the Night Court. He "awaits you in the command tower."

We approach. The room is lit up only by jack-o-lanterns. Bats fly through the air. Mushrooms cover the ground. Undead servants attend a person in the center.

We see a bookshelf packed with books. A table with half-made projects (attractions for Spirit Halloween). A cloak with a rainbow swirl is attached to a mask. Other masks lie around. One like a goose, one commedia dell'arte mask, a green mask familiar to Vayb(?).

A haughty archfey looks over the scene with a wicked grin. They have mothlike wings, painted nails, and the rest of their face is covered in a yellow-white mist.

![[Images/Pasted image 20251025223321.png]]

They speak, their voice echoing. "Happy Halloween, and congratulations on making it this far."

They lay out the rules for our parley: They are the creator, king of the Night Court. They deal in one commodity. _Secrets_. We get a question, they get a question, back and forth.

We ask how and why the undead are here: he has an item that brings them to him at his command.

In return: Did Squall want to gamble the way Vayb did? They didn't need to.

What is the purpose of this festival here? Two reasons. For one, "I am on vacation. The Material Plane is not my home. Instead it is a place I meddle in for entertainment. This is entertaining. The truth of the matter is that, as much as I hate to admit it, I can't help myself but take my work with me. I have... foes that must be crushed, and deals that must be met. And the leverage from my home is information and the various ethereal things you have given up or bet as currency, and my goals are better achieved if I am richer." Claire remarks that they choose a poor location for a money-making scheme, with no traffic like this.

Then they ask what wish we have, in coming to a being like this. "I guess we're here looking for answers," Vayb says, but we were told that Hickory wants the item.

D'Arby raises a hand and asks if there's a catch. "You've been studying up on devils, haven't you? The fey do not operate by such rules. The fey operate more on a mutual exchange. We are literal in the way that we communicate. Your deal will be laid out in front of you fully. I will not add some extra curse to whatever you are given."

Squall asks, point blank, what it would take to get access to the undead-controlling item for study. "It is a hefty cost. It has entertained me for such a long time."

D'Arby asks if, maybe, he could give it a hug—and they need something more, similar, but more permanent. D'Arby says no to that.

They ask a question of D'Arby: Did this festival scare you? D'Arby answered no. They seemed disappointed. D'Arby pointed out that he saw this scary video—and notably this fey seemed to know it and have distaste for it.

Squall asks, "What's with the green mask?" "I have made many deals and given many people many different things. That was a prototype for an old client."

In return, to Squall. "What scares you?" "Loss." They grin. "I was correct."

Squall asks, "What does the final version do?" "All sorts of things, I suppose. For one, it worked well with their technological amalgamations, but... in reality, the one who came to me had a strange request. In many ways, the thing they gave up was the thing they were given. In its purest form, the mask gave them one thing: a new identity. To take on a new life, and be completely stripped away of the old one. Except for their memories."

Their question. "Why do you fear loss? Why is that the place you go to, more than anything on earth?" "I suppose the truest answer is that these people mean something to me."

Claire offers information (access to the WWW) in return for the thrall item, and accidentally prompts a question. In return, she asks if the one with the green mask is the scariest among them and their companions. He says he only met one of those companions, but she ([[L1CH]]) seemed to be the one in charge.

He asks why we care about that old patron and why information is the thing Claire thinks she has to offer. "We've run into them before." "It's the only skill I have to offer."

Before Claire's questions, D'Arby asks if they want to see a front flip—they do not.

Claire asks a question: What is the best way to contact you when you aren't on this plane? Answer: The expected magic spells.

Vayb asks the second: What questions should we ask you? First, they point out that they mentioned an associate we haven't asked the identity of, and an old identity of L1CH's (though they freely confess that they don't actually know much). And, they note, we haven't asked anything about the goals _they_ are after so we can make a trade. Nor have we asked what entertains them.

"What entertains you?" "All emotions derive from the Feywild. It is the way that we contribute to the Material Plane. Your ability to scare is second-hand. You all are amateurs, and frankly it has disgusted me what mortals have done with such a thing. In a previous era, scares came from ghouls and undead and creepy individuals and people who took off their pumpkin heads to juggle them. It was delightful, whimsical, and spooky! This was fear to the fey, because we were small. We are not at the top of our food chain. We are afraid of the dangers in the world around us. Of LOSS. That entices us. That makes us curious." By contrast, it explains, our mortal fear comes from EACH OTHER, not from the unknown horrors in the world. That is the type of fear that excites him.

He asks what the Internet would get them: Claire replies that the internet wouldn't give them something specific. It would give them EVERYTHING. The internet is noise and chaos, and the unknown, and fear, and pure emotion. If what it wants is anywhere in the mortal realms, it is there, because the internet is a world of its own.

This explanation satisfies the archfey, who allows Claire to wire it into her tablet and connect him to the Weaves.

In return, it produces a mask with mushrooms growing off of it. And we have it send the mask to Dr. Hickory.

Before we leave, Claire asks who that associate is. "A popular person, though not one I have much access to. Though they have promoted my work, I've only spoken to them once, and they operated behind a username. One connected to my realms, though one I suspect has other reasons behind the name. [[H4G]]."

And Vayb asks one more to even us out. The most damning thing they know about L1CH. "I don't know much, admittedly. But their plans are _big_. So, no matter how well-hidden, if you pull the thread, you will find what it is attached to."
