We resume the session with [[Vayb]] and [[Squall]] doing okay now (they had a text RP between sessions), with Sugar on the comms. Sugar asks if she should, like… be going now or something? 

Vayb wipes a tear, notices that Squall is hardly hiding a look of disgust on their LCD face, then gives Sugar an earnest thank you. She says that it’s, like, no worries and “Oh my god, are you crying?” Vayb tells her that he’ll buy her a drink next time we’re on the Rock together. 

Squall points out that, earlier, Vayb had agreed that she was gonna pay before—and he should take her money. Vayb laughs and says that he may have taught them too well.

Suddenly, the smell of smoke permeates the ship. Suddenly, a familiar voice echoes: Meat! He doesn’t wanna interrupt what was going on, but he’s checking if there are any coffee grounds he can take, since he was told he can take them. 

Vayb and Squall, mind, did not know that Meat is on the ship. Vayb points him to a barrel, and startles Meat when [[Shaw]] pops out of the barrel. The two yap with Meat for a bit and ask about why he’s here—learning how he’s got a cousin in [[Lil' Avernus]], so he’s mooching a ride. Vayb says, “a cousin, eh?” and puts an arm around Meat, leading him out of the room. 

Eventually, the conversation steers toward them buying from Meat’s cart of magic items. Squall pulls a forked tongue. Vayb pulls the reverse card.

The trio stroll out to the helm and find [[Otto]]—Vayb remarks that the cloak of billowing looks gauche. Otto says that, on the contrary, it is extremely gauche! It’s inconvenient to wear indoors, sure, but it makes an impression. In any case, he offers to get Squall, Vayb, and Shaw caught up on the plan. 

> \[!Claire Note]
> leave it to otto to look incredibly tacky and also somehow own that

[[Claire]] emerges, her hair newly dyed to a deep red auburn colour. Vayb jokes that there’s some stranger on the ship, prompting an eyeroll. Claire says she wanted to get a little Infernal flair going on. 

Vayb seizes that segue and prompts Meat to tell us about how getting into Lil' Avernus works. He enthusiastically begins, enters a vicious coughing fit, and vanishes. _Of course._

Around this time, D’Arby is shuffling around in the background, picking up dirty dishes. Otto interrupts, placing his hand on D’Arby’s shoulder. He asks [[PIXIE|Pixie]] to show us an image of Lil' Avernus, and our objective. 

Otto summarizes the gist: a hellish place full of dangerous individuals and rampant crime. His plan is to coordinate with the [[AAPD]] and be brought in with their influence, rather than dealing with the criminal, fiendish gangs. 

Ultimately, going with the angels seems to be the play—Otto’s angelic nature would help us work with them too, but most importantly, we can get some profit off of it, too, while not having to make any shady deals. 

Otto tells Pixie he wants a written communiqué beamed to the AAPD headquarters. He sends the thing to her.

Vayb asks what the vibe is, and we discuss what we know: looking for the Eternal Codex, which is in Mammon’s Vault. The demon and devil gangs are at war. Claire asks if maybe we should look for Meat’s cousin to have a lead on the ground? We point out that all we have is a name, [[Candy]], but Pixie offers to perform a search through records about this person.

We descend into Lil' Avernus’ neon-lit streets. A sign reads: “Welcome to Lil' Avernus: Enter at your discretion”. A message beneath reads “Abandon all hope, ye who enter here…” 

While Vayb is abandoning hope, there’s a bang against the side of the ship. Two angels in white button-ups are tapping on the window, their wings spread out. They wait for us to address them. 

Vayb lights a cigarette, but makes no move. Otto, Squall, and Claire approach the angels. The angels monotonously explain that our ship is designated (numbers), and he asks to speak to the captain for our screening. 

Otto tells them our names, though it takes the group a moment to remember Vayb’s surname—we also discuss what the plan with [[Darrett]] is. Otto convinces him to stay behind, given how dangerous Lil' Avernus must be. 

We step off the ship into a bustling city, occupied by demons and devils; there are many tieflings, but also creatures as powerful as pit fiends wandering around the city. (Otto, we determine, has a magic item that allows him to comprehend other languages.)

Suddenly, Squall has to duck as a drone shoots out from an alleyway, nearly doming them as it shoots up into the air. From the alleyway, we can hear coughing and a guy shouting “Why?!”

In the alley, we find a kobold getting the shit kicked out of him by a group of robots. Otto leaps into action, using his new jet boots, to grab the poor kobold! 

_**Let’s Jam!**_

The skirmish is swift, as the Ocean Court makes short work of the offending drones! 

The kobold pops up and goes “holy shit! Holy fucking shit! What the fuck was that! Who the fuck are you! You got it without a chance for it to pull out its gun! What the fuck!” Finally, the kobold gets ahold of himself and introduces himself as Kilo. 

Vayb strolls up, blows a puff of smoke in Otto’s face, and asks when we started doing shit like this all the time. Otto tries to snatch his cigarette, prompting Vayb to stalk off in a huff. Claire follows. 

She asks him what the heck that was all about—Vayb retorts by asking since when we’ve “been in the hero business?” Claire asks what we just did with Shaw. Vayb says that that’s bigger, and when it comes to random kobolds it’s different. Claire asks why—”we’re strong enough”. Vayb says there’s always someone stronger. And he’s not here to get into the hero business “again”. Claire can’t argue with that, but chastises him for running off. Vayb throws that back at Otto, and Claire agrees but says they both share blame, then insists they get back to the group.

Meanwhile, Otto introduces himself to the kobold, who tells him that he’s a messenger, and he was carrying a message to the AAPD—but then the drone snatched it! He says he doesn’t know what was in it or why the drone wanted it. Otto thinks that’s very interesting. 

While they talk, D’Arby pokes around at the drones, finding runes inside the robots—ones very similar to the ones he has on his arm now. Additionally, he finds text inside it, in Common: “Property of BINGO”. 

> \[!Claire Note]
> and bingo was his name-o i guess

Squall sends their homunculus drone after the thieving drone, and rends the thing apart, leaving behind its cargo: an envelope, with a little golden stamp. 

Otto asks the kobold what the letter was: it was sent by the (noun?) of Mammon’s Vault. They send a message to the angels every day. Otto makes a deal with him to take us to the angels with the recovered message alongside him—as long as Kilo keeps his payment. 

When we realize we can’t communicate with Squall’s drone, Otto flies off to go find it. In the meantime, Claire takes the chance to sell Kilo on becoming a downstream distributor of Luigi-Fuel!

Otto and the drone return with the letter, and we check whether or not we can read the message, but realize we don’t really have the ability. 

Otto offers an “olive branch” to Vayb, asking him to trust in his ability to not lead us into danger. Vayb gives him a look, flicks his cigarette, and walks onward. Otto reiterates that he trusts the party to watch his back.

We head onward. 

Otto scans the area to get a vibe as we walk onward: the area’s clearly majority occupied by tieflings. But, there are many other lower-planar entities: hags, yugoloths, infernals and abyssals. Anything that would trip his Divine Sense. But, oddly, there’s a remarkable nonviolence for this kind of being.

As we continue, Claire pulls a lil huddle with Squall and D’Arby to ask them what they think about what happened with Vayb and Otto—they both explain and reassure Claire about what was going on, and agree to keep an eye on them, resolving some of Claire’s anxieties about the matter. 

We arrive at the imposing, brutalist Avernus Angelic Police Department HQ. A small angel secretary named Vanessa greets Kilo. He says he’s here with friends to deliver the message, and Vanessa says she’ll “go get him.”

An absolute MOUNTAIN of a man enters the room, with countless battle scars, a defiant scowl, and a gleaming silver helmet. A thick cigar burns between his clenched teeth. He wears a blue shirt beneath a white vest and white coat. He has a crescent moon tribal tattoo on his neck, and a red carnation at his breast. 

He speaks: Who in the Lord’s name are you?
