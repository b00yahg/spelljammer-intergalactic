Light surrounds the party, blinding us—and then we find ourselves in a conference room lit by fluorescent lights. [[Gabriel]] and Elijah are sitting across from us, arms crossed. They start by asking us if we're alright.

[[Otto]] replies that we could do with some rest, but we're mostly alright and we have a plan. Elijah asks for a debrief/to give him the run-down. Otto explains the situation as best he can (but I have no idea because his mic is being wonky).

Elijah asks if we know Bingo's current location—Otto replies that no, he does not. But that we may be able to track him down. Gabriel nods solemnly, but Elijah narrows his eyes—however, he says "excellent, but I'm sure you'll inform us once you have a greater idea where he is. If you can lure him out of the ward, even better." Otto agrees.

Elijah then says that he has fantastic news: assuming we don't disappoint and are able to produce results, we'll have our in.

[[Claire]] signals [[Vayb]] to put the telepathic field up (since Shmam is gone, we're considering it soft always active) and asks Otto if we're telling them about the traitor in their midst. Otto then mentions, without specifics, that we have a lead on the origin of the angel dust—and keeps an eye on them to gauge reactions.

Gabriel seems intense—like he wants to leap right into action. But Elijah's temperament seems nervous.

Otto elaborates that we haven't learned any names, but that there may be someone supplying the devils with angel feathers from within the [[AAPD]]. They reply that this is a _serious_ accusation, and compliment us on the good work—assuming we can get a name or anything else that allows for more action, they may be able to get us this meeting.

When the conversation turns more broadly, [[D'Arby]] asks them why they haven't considered being friendly. With the people who are selling things. "You mean the people who are using the souls of the dead to trade in illegal, sinful goods?" Gabriel replies.

D'Arby says "well, when you say it like that, it sounds bad." And hands him an origami swan he's been making. Gabriel begins a rant, but then loses his composure and asks "What _is_ this??". He asks if this is a prayer totem or something. And asks why D'Arby has given it to him.

D'Arby just says it's because Gabriel seems like he has a stressful job. Gabriel just says "okay..." But he keeps it next to him—and asks what we've been talking about.

We get back on track about the next steps of the mission.

We leave the AAPD office and return to the streets of [[Lil' Avernus]].

Claire murmurs that it was bright in there. D'Arby replies that it was, and that he's happy he had his shades. Claire says she needs some, and in response, a bright neon ad for sunglasses pops up in front of her.

The group chats about sunglasses for a while. Then Claire asks what the plan even is. Shopping, meeting with [[Bingo]]... D'Arby replies that he's not sure how he feels about being jerked around by Bingo like that.

Otto then interrupts and points out that Elijah looked nervous, so we might want to get the drop on him before he warns a collaborator to watch out for us. But first, we rush to our meeting with Bingo and see if there's maybe an angle in turning these cops against [[2NGEL]]. Or at least, to get all our wildcards in one place and make them fight. But ultimately, our goal should be to meet with Bingo quickly.

We make our way to the meeting point.

Rain falls heavily, neon lights reflect in the puddles. We go to the number, 375482, pinned on a digital map, which is in the middle of the street—which is empty. Muffled city sounds can be heard in the distance, but everything here is silent.

A woman lurks in the shadows—and she asks "were you followed?"

[[Candy]] steps out of the shadows, an orange tiefling with bright eyes, dressed in fancy city girl clothing. A flaming illusory tattoo burns on her neck.

Claire and [[Squall]] both ask "are you Candy?" Her expression drops and she goes "Oh. You know me?" Claire replies that we know her cousin, much to her surprise.

Otto asks for a change of scenery before we keep talking—she replies that that's the plan, but she wants to meet us personally first. She steps up toward Otto, clearly sizing him up. "What's your game, here?"

Otto replies that this isn't really a game here. "My game is to protect the vulnerable and take down bad people in positions of power." He says he thinks she and Bingo might be included in that.

She sighs, and says that sounds good, but she's heard lots of people use that same kind of speech to commit slaughter—and that those people often look like him. Otto replies that his actions will speak more than his words.

She scans the group again, and seems to recognize Claire. She tells the group to "forgive me for not trusting a paladin, bounty hunter, two people covering their face, and a SpaceTuber."

Squall reacts a bit confused and uncomfortable at their face being considered a mask—D'Arby tells Candy that she ought to know that if she watched Claire's videos. She says she isn't a subscriber or anything. Claire comments that she better. Otto replies that if we succeed at helping her out, she'll subscribe. She agrees, saying that if we can _actually_ help her out, she'll subscribe to whatever damn channel we want.

> \[!Claire Note]
> gotta earn those subscribers somehow >:)

She then opens a portal to teleport us to the meeting place—which we _refuse_ to get into, saying she won't get us another time. She sighs, and gets pressured into walking us there.

On the way, Claire films pretty aesthetic shots of Lil' Avernus.

Otto takes the chance to ask if she's seen Meat lately—she replies that she hasn't seen him, but that they exchange messages often, what with the situation in the city. The two discuss how long she's been here—having been stuck here by the lockdown when she was little, she feels like she was basically born here (like Bingo, who was born here).

The two talk about the city a bit, and Candy admits she doesn't much care about the city's state these days. It's just in the hands of those who came before her, and her circle's grown small. As long as she and Bingo can get out of here, that's what matters.

Otto tells her that there may be a way in which she won't have to run. Candy says she's not one for running, she just wants to fly. Claire empathizes with her point. The two seem to find some sort of understanding in that.

We arrive at our destination: a cathedral, baroque architecture, with stained glass windows depicting angels flying to battle in front of the rising sun. Many-coloured lights flash from inside the cathedral, and dance music echoes out of the door. A spike-backed devil acts as a bouncer.

> \[!Claire Note]
> okay this place is an AESTHETIC(tm)

Candy cuts past the line and walks right on in. The bouncer tries to intercept us, but Candy interrupts and says we're with her. He lets us in. D'Arby apologizes to all the people we pass.

We enter the cathedral club, a true den of vice and music. Drinks flow, drugs are imbibed, an imp DJ dances while he does the music, tiefling strippers dance in cages. Private booths are made out of the old confession booths.

We approach one of the private rooms, and a devil bouncer stops Candy—saying there's clown business in there. Candy sighs, saying that's why she's here. Claire interrupts, asking if the bouncer is stupid—and to let her in. Candy chimes in. "Yeah, are you stupid?" The devil looks sheepish, apologizes, and lets her in.

We arrive, and Bingo grins. "Well, look what the cat dragged in!"

Claire comments her disappointment that a spell drowns out the music. Bingo tells her there's a dial to adjust it in the corner. Otto complains that the room is boring and a little cramped, so he pulls out the _pocket spa_—inviting us inside to meet in comfort. Candy comments that she's sorry, but this is better than Bingo's demiplane.

Bingo sends several drones out of a tiny clown car to keep watch outside. Claire and Squall send k1k1 and their homunculus with them.

Otto half-undresses, and enters the hot tub for us to talk.

Bingo tells him to begin. Otto asks us to share our experiences with 2NGEL. D'Arby explains that 2NGEL is a crappy dude, rotten inside and out, and friendless. But, he clarifies, he's not the erudite one. Bingo replies that he likes the simple perspective.

Claire then produces her recordings and documents about [[Shaw]]. She explains his history, and how he was made to kill all his men by Project Cain. She explains that it's a weapon 2NGEL has been making, that causes you to kill (she looks to Candy) even people you love. And that his weapon was used on us when we boarded his ship to save his prisoners from [[Shilear]].

Candy furrows her brow, clearly convinced but—as she says—she's not sure what she and Bingo have to do with that. Bingo holds her hand and confesses that he's been selling souls to 2NGEL. He got into the clown business very young, and he's been passed around as a weapon for a long time. Between the demons, devils, and now to 2NGEL. And if what we're saying is true, he and Candy are in it—and that he refuses to be a weapon any longer.

But he can't just turn on 2NGEL willy-nilly. Claire says that we'll get them safely to the [[The Rock of Bral|Rock of Bral]]. D'Arby chimes in that this place sucks; they don't even know what origami is! Bingo replies in shoc. "That's my favourite thing!" They dap each other up.

Back to the topic at hand, Claire explains to Bingo that the Codex will act as a decoder. And that we can learn about Project Cain if we get our hands on it.

Otto starts to plan an ambush on the docks, but Claire disagrees. Bingo just suggests we focus on Step 1 for now. He asks what resources we have, and comments that we now have the best thief in the city on our side. Otto explains our deal with the AAPD to get a meeting with the Curator.

Then, he teaches us about what he's learned about Mammon's Vault. It has a section for collecting dangerous shit: weapons, gruesome things, stuff that'll cause things to burn. It has a section for information: secrets, paths to lichdom, that kinda stuff. The last contains sentient and cursed magic items.

The Codex'll be in one of those areas.

Additionally, it's under the effects of a _temple of the gods_ spell, so teleportation magic won't work in or out of there. He doesn't know the full details, but he does have a simple map on him, which he keeps somewhere safe. He pulls a hankerchief out of his mouth. Then another. Then another. Then another. Then another. Then another. Then another. Then another. Then another.

At the end is a file with the following map:
![[Images/Pasted image 20251122220823.png]]

This is as far as he's gotten, but he may have some other leads.

The first is that he has an old pal in the [[The Sindiath Line|Sindiath Line]] who owes him—and this guy runs things into the Vault. Not the high security stuff, but water being brought to be holy'd, items to be blessed, etc.

The second is that the mimes once tried to blow up the Vault to get in. It failed, but if we can talk to them, maybe we can learn that they've been cooking up for another attempt.

The last is that he'd been TRYING to steal passwords from Kilo, but we stopped him. If there's some way we can get those again, we might have options.

Then, after arranging for rooms to put us up in where we could meet again later, he and Candy get up to go. They ask us how to leave the spa. Otto says there's just a door. Bingo asks, confirming that the door goes to the same place he entered. Claire replies that he's the only one with a psychotic pocket dimension.

Bingo replies "IT WAS ONE TIME!!"

D'Arby also lets him know that he'll have to restock the booze in the pocket dimension. Bingo objects, but concedes that it's only fair for us being trapped.

Then they depart.

We get [[PIXIE]] to give us Kilo's number. We call him, and invite him out for drinks—agreeing to meet at a sushi bar. He says he'll wear his best suit.

We arrive at the location Kilo sends us, finding him panting as he's sprinting over in a suit with a red bow tie. He happily sits on the bar, asks if this is what he's supposed to wear—since no one has ever invited him to drinks before. He orders us some sasuke, then asks, "er, is that what it's called?" Claire says no, it's Shadowsuke.

He orders sake for all of us, and explains that it's his favourite drink. Since it's so sweet, because whiskey is so bitter. He likes whiskey cut with soda, but according to the angels, that's technically a sin. Anyway, he explains, sake gets him through his work a lot.

Then, Otto gets to business. Tells him that he's been extremely helpful in our time here—and requests for his aid one final time before we leave this place.

Kilo remarks in surprise, noting that it's cool that we'll actually get out of this place. Otto asks if he's ever been out of Lil' Avernus. He hasn't. "I mean, would there even be a place for someone like me to work? Does anyone need things run between two places out there?"

We get him excited about the prospect of going to space with us—then ask for his help in stealing from the Vault. He's SHOCKED that we would ask him to steal from the Vault. "I know I said the whole whiskey and soda thing, so I understand why you'd think I have a whole bad boy persona going on, but..."

We try to explain that we need to get information that an evil hacker has hidden stuff there, and the angels don't know what it is. Kilo says that sounds reasonable, but that's also what someone who's being whispered to by the amulets would say. Otto says that we're the good guys and has us show that we're not wearing any amulets. Besides, we're buying him sushi!

D'Arby reassures him that we aren't trying to force him to do anything. And he can choose whatever he wants. The only thing D'Arby really wants is for him to pass a piece of that sushi over.

Kilo thinks for a while, then says that he's never been asked to choose before. So... if he gets to choose... He'll choose to help us! And choose to _split_ the sushi with D'Arby.

He then just repeats, asking us that we're sure we're _100% sure_ that we're doing the right thing? Claire replies that she has a million subscribers and that a famous person would _never_ do anything bad! Kilo comments that that's gotta be true, otherwise why would so many people follow her? "Exactly! These people follow me because they think of me as a personal friend because I'm so good!"

Kilo agrees, and then tells us about how the passwords he's sent work—he's sent them in the morning, and there's maybe a detour where he can pass the info along to us.

In return for his help, Otto tells him that he's welcome in the Ocean Court Express crew—Kilo remarks that he's never been part of a crew before! He gets up, excited, and his tail knocks over the soy sauce.

Otto helps clean it up, then kneels in front of Kilo and uses _minor illusion_ to create a lil glowing star badge on his chest. Kilo puffs up his chest in pride.

Moving on, he asks if we're gonna finish this sushi. We laugh, and reply that of course we will. D'Arby comments on how he's enjoying his first time having sushi—to which Kilo replies that he's gotta try it with the wasabi. D'Arby scoops up a huge fingerful and pops it right in his mouth. He handles the spice surprisingly well!

We make a joke about him eating with his hands, which Otto thinks is ridiculous—we look over to see he's eating with a fork and knife. Kilo leans over to Squall and asks if they don't have chopsticks in space. Squall says they don't know what sushi or chopsticks are. So he asks Claire. She remarks how space does—she has them for her noodles on the ship. Otto comments that it's not like she'd have a fork for it, considering she doesn't wash them. Claire insists again that we have the unseen servant and Vayb's just fucking with her.

> \[!Claire Note]
> oh my gosh did they just get me to drop an f-bomb?

The group laughs, and as we settle down to chat over dinner...

We roll credits!
