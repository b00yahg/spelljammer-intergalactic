Hell is empty, and the mimes are all here. We cut, in medias res, to where we left off. The massive demons with painted white faces tower over the foxlike person.

_Blam!_

A shot from [[Squall]] takes one of the mimes on an infernal motorcycle in the face.

Another barlgura mime RIPS something out of its motorcycle, and places an engine in the water of the boardwalk, which begins to boil. From her boat, the hag pulls out a jack in the box covered in Infernal sigils—the glyphs depict what seems to be a countdown from three. She's winding it.

From our comms, our angel guide—Elijah—says "shit! She's calling the clowns! Stop her, before she calls the Siren!" [[D'Arby]] coolly replies that "well, I ain't about to clown around." [[Vayb]] has no idea what a clown is.

Vayb draws his wand of magic missiles and unloads the entire clip on one of the mimes. Another barrels down on [[Otto]], unhinges its jaw, and bites Otto. Then it zooms away by bike, preventing Otto from attacking. It laughs as it does, then spins in circles, smoke left in its trail.

Otto jets over with his boots, landing next to the fox fellow. Otto looks at him and says "can you move?" The fox says "uh! no!", as he's been invisibly lasso'd by the mimes. So Otto attempts to tear him out, but fails to do so—so Otto places himself between the barlgura holding the lasso. In response, the buddy casts _entangle_—though the spell fails to grapple any of the targets, so they dispel it in frustration.

One of the barlguras swerves past [[Claire]] on his bike, attempting to knock her into the boiling water. It misses, and so she rushes ahead to jury rig a _vortex warp_ through her Tamagotchii to teleport the fox to safety between D'Arby and Vayb. She yells at D'Arby to protect her, since she's pretty exposes right now.

D'Arby and the fox man awkwardly say "hey." The fox asks if D'Arby is with the girl who just teleported him. He says yep. Then, D'Arby backflips over to the hag, who's furiously winding the jack in the box.

D'Arby asks if she's gonna stop. She says that they were told to call the clowns if the mimes showed up—and things have gotten out of control. D'Arby looks over toward Otto, who is very casually dodging a flurry of attacks from the barlguras. From the distance, Claire shouts that "Does it look like we don't have this under control, are you stupid?"

The hag takes offence to this. D'Arby tells her to disregard that; Claire's just going through something and she's not even doing her dishes. It's a young rebellion kind of thing. But, she's right, and D'Arby may have to kill the hag if she won't stop. He ACES (heh.) his intimidation check, and the hag flips out, grovels and agrees to stop the winding of the jack in the box.

Another mime emerges from the motorcycle donut circles, a glowing red pentagram emerging on the street.

Claire traps them in a circle of glowing sickening blue light on one pair of barlguras. Vayb Akira drifts over with his hoverboard and fireballs another pair. Claire's magic fails to be of much use, so she angrily blasts off a _lightning bolt_.

Otto fucks up one of the barlguras so bad that it casts _invisibility_. He proceeds to cast _see invisibility_ and follows it anyway, much to its utter terror. Its buddy considers running away but half-assedly decides to try to help attack Otto, but misses.

After more keeping barlguras away from the fox man, we finish mopping up the remains and clear out the demon mime gang!

Roll credits!
