After some exploring, we return to the room, where Gabriel is watching over the unconscious form of Echo. She's hooked up to an IV drip of magical chemicals. We discuss what to do: awaken her now, try to transport her now, etc.

[[Claire]] steps forward to investigate the chemicals. Due to an exceptional roll, Claire gathers that the tech here is set up to be tied directly to her. As an organic, her ward can't have the same kind of ward of unconsciousness as the other rooms—therefore, an alarm will trigger when she leaves this room. It's connected to a chip in Echo's neck.

The dripper is tied to her sedation. If we unplug it, she will start to wake up—but theoretically, we could adjust the system to keep her sedated while moving her.

While we inspect, [[Vayb]] quietly focuses—and casts _detect magic_.

She's dreaming. Visions swirl, dancing across her mind: formulas. Mathematics, far too complicated for Vayb to solve on his own, flicker across her mind. She's solving them in her dreams.

Vayb is familiar with the psychic energy in someone's brain, due to the illithid experiments he's experienced. Her psionic energy is powerful.

Vayb chooses not to push further, to not be invasive, but he tries to telepathically tell her that she's gonna be okay. That we're gonna get her out of here.

Although he doesn't get an answer, and her thought patterns stay consistent... but the message seems to have found purchase.

[[Squall]] assures him they're good at this, and Claire points out that the chip causing an alarm or even being tracked is also deeply dangerous to her. [[Otto]] reassures Gabriel that his care for her is clear, and that we agree that we want her to get out of here safely. But, when we remove her, her safety becomes our responsibility, and we need to do what we can to ensure her safety. This seems like the best bet.
